# Business Capabilities

## Product Setup

- Define provider.
- Define product type.
- Capture product contract terms.
- Capture legal depositor/owner.
- Capture joint ownership.
- Capture currency eligibility.
- Capture funding source.
- Capture settlement destination.
- Capture minimum and maximum amount.
- Capture term, start date, maturity date, and interest rate.
- Capture interest method and payment method.
- Capture early withdrawal policy.
- Capture renewal policy and preference.

## Funding

- Validate funding source.
- Initiate funding.
- Track pending funding.
- Confirm provider acceptance.
- Reject or reverse failed funding.
- Preserve audit trail.

## Active Holding

- Track current cycle.
- Track principal.
- Track provider terms.
- Track locked or variable rate.
- Track accrued estimate separately from posted interest.
- Track provider status.
- Track concentration by provider.
- Track deposit insurance exposure.

## Interest

- Estimate expected interest.
- Track accrued interest.
- Track posted interest.
- Track compounding or payout method.
- Track rate changes.
- Track tax treatment.
- Support provider-confirmed interest overrides.

## Maturity

- Detect maturity windows.
- Create maturity decisions.
- Preserve 30/14/7 or approved cascade timing.
- Cancel duplicate reminders after decision.
- Present renewal, switch, withdraw, and settlement choices as business options.
- Prevent silent rollover where household confirmation is required.

## Settlement

- Settle principal.
- Settle interest.
- Support principal-only renewal.
- Support principal-plus-interest renewal.
- Support full withdrawal.
- Support provider failure, partial settlement, or duplicate settlement review.

## Early Withdrawal

- Preview eligible interest.
- Preview penalty or forfeiture.
- Require confirmation.
- Execute only after decision.
- Record final provider outcome.
- Handle emergency withdrawal.

## Renewal and Switching

- Renew same package.
- Switch package.
- Apply saved preference only as recommendation or pre-fill unless business rules evolve.
- Handle unavailable packages.
- Handle changed rates.
- Handle changed term lengths.
- Start new cycle with immutable snapshot.

## Provider Management

- Maintain provider catalog.
- Maintain package catalog.
- Mark provider inactive.
- Mark package unavailable.
- Preserve historical product snapshots.
- Separate regulated bank deposits from fintech savings-like products.

## Legal and Compliance

- Track legal ownership.
- Track eligibility/residency/currency restrictions where relevant.
- Track deposit insurance scope.
- Track pledged/collateral status.
- Track power of attorney, inheritance, or account freeze events.
- Track personal/business distinction.

## Notifications and Inbox

- Maturity reminder.
- Matured product decision.
- Early withdrawal confirmation.
- Penalty warning.
- Rate/package change warning.
- Provider unavailable warning.
- Failed funding or failed settlement review.

## Analytics

- Yield summary.
- Maturity calendar.
- Liquidity ladder.
- Provider concentration.
- Insured versus uninsured exposure.
- Expected versus actual interest.
- Savings rate metrics belong to Health or Plan, not Savings ownership.

## Reconciliation

- Compare expected and provider-reported principal.
- Compare expected and provider-reported interest.
- Correct settlement mismatch.
- Mark duplicates.
- Audit provider statement evidence.
