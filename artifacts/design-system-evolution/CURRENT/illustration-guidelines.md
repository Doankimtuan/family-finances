# Illustration Guidelines

## Allowed Placements

Illustrations may appear only in:

- Onboarding.
- Empty states.
- Educational moments.
- Success milestones.

## Personality

Illustrations should be:

- Soft.
- Household-centered.
- Warm but not childish.
- Simple enough to localize.
- Compatible with light and dark themes.

## Usage Rules

- Illustration supports the message; it never replaces financial facts.
- Do not use illustration in confirmations where source, destination, amount, and reversibility matter.
- Do not use mascot characters as the product voice.
- Do not use random stickers or decorative badges.
- Keep illustration rare enough that it feels helpful when it appears.

## Dark Mode

Illustrations must maintain shape, contrast, and emotional tone in dark mode. Avoid assets that rely on pale-on-white or dark-on-black detail.
