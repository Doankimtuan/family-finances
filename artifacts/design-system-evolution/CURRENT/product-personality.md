# Product Personality

## Product Character

ViNha is a Gen Z financial life companion for young Vietnamese households aged 20 to 35.

It should feel:

- Light
- Calm
- Flexible
- Dynamic
- Modern
- Warm
- Optimistic
- Simple
- Complete
- Trustworthy
- Slightly expressive
- Emotionally safe

It must not feel:

- Like accounting software
- Like enterprise finance tooling
- Like a bank back office
- Like a crypto product
- Like a gamified app
- Like a dashboard collection
- Like a marketing site inside the product

## Voice

Use calm, plain, bilingual-ready language. Prefer conversational clarity over banking terminology.

Good copy:

- "This changes your real balance."
- "This is an estimated value, not cash."
- "Review this before it affects your plan."
- "No decisions needed right now."

Avoid:

- Shame or guilt.
- Investment advice.
- Optimization pressure.
- Meme language.
- Dense accounting terms unless required for correctness.

## Emotional Rules

- Money facts should feel understandable, not dominant.
- Progress should be visible before complexity.
- Warnings should state risk and recovery.
- Partner-visible actions should feel respectful and explicit.
- Empty states should offer one valid next action.

## Youthfulness Rules

Allowed:

- Friendly motion.
- Expressive but consistent icons.
- Soft tonal contrast.
- Conversational microcopy.
- Contextual illustration.
- Fresh empty states.
- Subtle celebration after meaningful completion.
- Flexible emphasis when it helps scanning.

Forbidden:

- Neon gradients everywhere.
- Glassmorphism as a default material.
- Emoji as core navigation.
- Meme language.
- Excessive stickers.
- Childish mascots.
- Random module colors.
- Trend-first typography.
- Decorative animation without a product reason.
