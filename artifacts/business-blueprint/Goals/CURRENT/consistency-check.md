# Consistency Check

## No Contradictory Rules

- Goal progress is always intention unless source evidence is read-only.
- Contribution is never automatically a transfer.
- Completion is never automatically a purchase or payment.
- Savings association never gives Goals ownership of savings product truth.

## No Duplicated Responsibilities

- Goals owns future purpose and progress interpretation.
- Planning owns broader allocation and review.
- Accounts owns balances.
- Transactions owns movements.
- Savings owns product truth.
- Cards and Loans own obligation truth.
- Health remains read-only.

## No Circular Ownership

- Goals may consume source facts, but source domains do not depend on Goals for their truth.
- Health may consume Goals, but Goals do not consume Health for mutation.
- Planning may include Goals, but Goals do not own Planning allocation.

## No Orphan Flows

- Create, update, contribute, pause, resume, complete, cancel, evidence review, savings context, and deadline pressure all have defined triggers, preconditions, rules, expected results, and failure results.

## No Missing Lifecycle Stages

- Beginning, normal operation, change, completion, termination, recovery, and exceptional situations are all defined.

## No BR Violations

- BR-01 is preserved throughout money flow and business rules.
- BR-24 is preserved by keeping Health read-only.
- BR-14 is preserved by excluding AI explanation from current business behavior.
- No unnecessary automation is introduced.

## Open Cautions

- Vietnamese terminology remains important before implementation copy.
- Manual progress may become stale.
- Partner visibility may create blame if future scope expands.
- Evidence comparison must stay humble where facts are incomplete.
