# Business Boundaries

## Belongs Here

- Household identity.
- Active household status.
- Active membership.
- Limited inactive membership context.
- Partner/Admin responsibility distinction.
- Partner invitation.
- Invitation response.
- Invitation terminal states.
- Household-level policies.
- Current policy visibility.
- Material policy-change attribution.
- Recent material policy events.
- Household-level preferences.
- Member recognition identity.
- Household scope for other domains.
- Non-member protection.
- Simple descriptive household meaning.

## Belongs Elsewhere

Auth:

- Authentication, provider identity, password reset, and sessions.

Accounts:

- Account identity, legal holder facts, balances, and real-position contribution.

Transactions:

- Money movement, transaction correction, transfer, refund, and transaction truth.

Cards:

- Card lifecycle, credit limits, billing, APR, and payment obligations.

Loans:

- Loan terms, repayment, payoff, and completion.

Savings:

- Savings product terms, maturity, renewal, withdrawal, and settlement.

Planning:

- Jars, allocations, recurring patterns, and Month Ritual execution.

Goals:

- Goal targets, progress, funding, and completion.

Inbox:

- Review item lifecycle and decision resolution.

Health:

- Read-only reflection, score, narrative, and insight.

Categories:

- Classification vocabulary and mappings.

Platform/Security:

- Technical enforcement, logs, secrets, and infrastructure controls.

## Must Never Own

- Real money balances.
- Ledger transactions.
- Jar allocations.
- Goal funding.
- Savings maturity actions.
- Card or loan payoff.
- Health score calculation.
- AI-generated financial facts.
- Legal marriage or family status.
- Relationship dispute outcomes.
- Partner performance or engagement scoring.
- Granular custom permissions.
- Automatic money movement.

## Responsibility Leakage Warnings

- Policy is not execution.
- Visibility is not legal authority.
- Attribution is not blame.
- Admin is not owner.
- Historical member is not active participant.
- Household is not legal family registry.
