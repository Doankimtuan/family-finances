# Decision Points

## Track Container

Who:

- Household user or partner.

Why:

- Decides whether a real-world container matters to household money.

Possible outcomes:

- Create account.
- Do not track.
- Keep as Candidate.

Business impact:

- Determines completeness of household real position.

## Choose Account Name

Who:

- User creating or updating the account.

Why:

- Household must recognize the container.

Possible outcomes:

- Household-friendly name accepted.
- Name revised.
- Account remains Draft.

Business impact:

- Affects partner understanding and long-term trust.

## Choose Broad Type

Who:

- User creating or updating the account.

Why:

- Type communicates financial behavior and liquidity.

Possible outcomes:

- Cash/bank/wallet/savings/card-adjacent/other type accepted.
- Type requires review.

Business impact:

- Affects real-position eligibility and misunderstanding risk.

## Set Starting Balance

Who:

- User with knowledge of the container.

Why:

- Establishes first recorded position.

Possible outcomes:

- Balance accepted.
- Account stays Draft.
- Account enters Needs Review later if wrong.

Business impact:

- Anchors future trust.

## Include In Real Position

Who:

- Business rules decide based on account type and eligibility; user decides household relevance.

Why:

- Real position must represent owned money only.

Possible outcomes:

- Included.
- Excluded.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Prevents inflated or misleading household money totals.

## Reconcile Difference

Who:

- Household user, often active money operator.

Why:

- Recorded truth differs from real-world source.

Possible outcomes:

- Confirm no change.
- Explain and adjust.
- Keep Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Restores or preserves trust.

## Mark Historical

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Account is no longer active but history matters.

Possible outcomes:

- Historical.
- Needs Review if unresolved active meaning exists.

Business impact:

- Keeps current account set clean while preserving history.

## Close Account

Who:

- Household user with knowledge of real-world closure.

Why:

- Real-world container ended.

Possible outcomes:

- Closed.
- Historical.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Ends active use without erasing meaning.

## Restore Account

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Historical account becomes relevant again or was changed by mistake.

Possible outcomes:

- Active.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Prevents duplicate or lost account history.

## Reject Boundary-Breaking Action

Who:

- Business rules.

Why:

- Protect BR-01, BR-24, and household safety.

Possible outcomes:

- Invalid Attempt.
- Previous valid state preserved.

Business impact:

- Prevents product philosophy collapse.
