# Exceptional Scenarios

## Cancellation

Scenario:

- Purchase, fund subscription, gold purchase, or family deal never completes.

Business expectation:

- If no ownership formed, investment becomes Cancelled.
- If cash moved and returned, Transactions owns refund/reversal truth.
- Cancelled investment does not create realized gain/loss.

## Correction

Scenario:

- Wrong holding, asset class, quantity, contribution, value, date, source, liquidity, or ownership was recorded.

Business expectation:

- Correction preserves household explanation.
- If correction changes domain ownership, reclassification occurs.
- If correction cannot be resolved, holding enters Under Review.

## Recovery

Scenario:

- Missing statement, stale value, or uncertain family update is later clarified.

Business expectation:

- Under Review or Impaired holding may return to Active, Exited, Written Off, or Archived based on clarified facts.
- Recovery does not create cash unless a real cash movement occurred.

## Emergency

Scenario:

- Household needs cash for job loss, health, rent, childcare, family support, or other urgent need.

Business expectation:

- Investments may provide liquidity context.
- Market value must not be treated as available cash until proceeds are realized.
- Investment domain does not decide which holding to sell.

## Conflict

Scenario:

- Partners disagree about risk, secrecy, ownership, loss, or whether an asset is household money.

Business expectation:

- Holding may enter Under Review.
- Together owns household visibility and permission context.
- Investments preserves facts and uncertainty; it does not resolve relationship authority.

## Expired Data

Scenario:

- Value is old, source is missing, or provider/family update is stale.

Business expectation:

- Value remains estimated with stale or unknown freshness.
- Stale value must not be treated as guaranteed current value.

## Invalid State

Scenario:

- Archived holding is edited as active, cancelled holding receives value update, or exited holding receives market movement.

Business expectation:

- Invalid transition is blocked at business level.
- New active exposure requires new recognition.

## Unexpected User Behavior

Scenario:

- User tries to count unrealized gain as spending money, use investment purpose as goal progress, or record contribution as profit.

Business expectation:

- Business interpretation rejects the meaning.
- Correct domain is Accounts, Transactions, Goals, or Planning depending on the fact.

## System Interruption

Scenario:

- User loses connection or provider information becomes unavailable during review.

Business expectation:

- No investment decision is assumed.
- Last-known facts remain with date/source.
- Holding may remain Under Review until user confirms.

## Scam Or Fraud Suspicion

Scenario:

- User suspects unauthorized trade, fake investment, non-recoverable family deal, or fraudulent provider.

Business expectation:

- Holding becomes Under Review or Impaired.
- Value may be unknown or written down only after household determination.
- Investments does not provide legal or recovery advice.

## Margin Or Leverage Shock

Scenario:

- Investment exposure includes borrowed money or margin loss.

Business expectation:

- Leverage is risk context only.
- Loan/card obligations remain owned by Loans or Cards.
- Investments does not optimize leverage or trading response.
