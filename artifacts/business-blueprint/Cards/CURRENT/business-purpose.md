# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Cards exists because household card spending behaves differently from cash, bank transfers, e-wallet spending, savings, and scheduled loans.

A debit card gives access to real money in an account. A credit card creates a future repayment obligation. A card transaction may be visible before cash leaves the household, and a credit-card repayment may happen days or weeks after the original purchase.

## Core Responsibility

Cards is responsible for making card-specific financial reality understandable:

- Which card exists.
- Which issuer stands behind it.
- Whether the card is mainly account access or credit obligation.
- What credit limit exists.
- What statement cycle and due date apply.
- What amount is billed, paid, and still due.
- Which card activity belongs to a billing period.
- Whether a card event is purchase, repayment, refund, fee, interest, cashback, or card-origin installment.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is:

Credit is not cash, and repayment is not the same financial event as purchase.

Cards must protect BR-01 by keeping real ledger money, card obligations, and virtual planning separate. Cards must protect BR-24 by allowing Health to read card risk but never change card state.

## Business Outcome

The intended business outcome is that young households can use cards without losing track of where money is, what is owed, who is affected, and when repayment pressure will arrive.
