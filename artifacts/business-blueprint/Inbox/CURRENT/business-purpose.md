# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Inbox exists because household financial life creates open loops. A transaction may lack meaning, a savings product may require a maturity decision, a payment reminder may require acknowledgement, or a repeated pattern may be safe to process with clear history.

Without Inbox, these decisions are scattered across bank apps, wallet apps, receipts, partner memory, and month-end review.

## Core Responsibility

Inbox collects, preserves, and resolves unresolved financial attention.

It must:

- Identify that household attention is needed.
- Preserve why attention is needed.
- Keep the item active until a valid outcome occurs.
- Record the business outcome.
- Route outcome meaning to the owning domain without taking ownership of that domain.

## Financial Principle

Inbox protects accountability over drift.

It supports BR-01 by keeping real ledger truth, virtual planning, and read-only information separate. It supports BR-24 by allowing Health to read context without letting Health change Inbox.

## Business Outcome

A healthy Inbox outcome is not "more activity." It is fewer unresolved financial decisions and more trustworthy household understanding.

The desired business outcome:

- The household knows what still needs attention.
- Decisions are traceable.
- Source domains remain authoritative.
- No user believes Inbox moved money unless another money-owning domain actually did so.
