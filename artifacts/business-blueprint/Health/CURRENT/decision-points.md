# Decision Points

## View Health

Who makes it:

- User or household member with permission.

Why:

- To understand household financial condition.

Possible outcomes:

- Assessment is shown.
- Assessment is partial.
- Assessment is unavailable.

Business impact:

- Creates awareness only; no source facts change.

## Trust Or Question Health

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Health may not match lived reality when data is incomplete.

Possible outcomes:

- User accepts the interpretation.
- User treats it as partial.
- User investigates source facts elsewhere.

Business impact:

- Health remains unchanged unless source facts later change.

## Investigate A Factor

Who makes it:

- User or partner.

Why:

- A factor indicates pressure, incompleteness, or improvement.

Possible outcomes:

- User reviews the owning domain.
- User ignores the factor.
- User returns later.

Business impact:

- Investigation belongs to the source domain.

## Correct Source Facts Elsewhere

Who makes it:

- User in the owning domain.

Why:

- Health interpretation is partial, stale, or wrong because source facts are incomplete or incorrect.

Possible outcomes:

- Source facts are corrected by the owning domain.
- User abandons correction.
- Correction is invalid in the owning domain.

Business impact:

- Health can reassess only after source facts change.

## Discuss With Partner

Who makes it:

- Household members.

Why:

- Health is most valuable when partners share understanding.

Possible outcomes:

- Partners align.
- Partners disagree.
- Partners defer conversation.

Business impact:

- Health may later reflect decision rhythm, but cannot judge or score a partner.

## Act In Another Domain

Who makes it:

- User or partner with permission.

Why:

- Health identifies pressure that may require action elsewhere.

Possible outcomes:

- Plan changes in Planning.
- Inbox item resolves in Inbox.
- Transaction records change in Transactions.
- Debt/card/savings/account action occurs in owning domain.
- No action occurs.

Business impact:

- Health never performs the action; it may reassess after source truth changes.

## Ignore Health

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- User may disagree, feel overwhelmed, or not need action.

Possible outcomes:

- No change.
- Source-domain pressure remains.
- User returns later.

Business impact:

- Health does not escalate or automate.
