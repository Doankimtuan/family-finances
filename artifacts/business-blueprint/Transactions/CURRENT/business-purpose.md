# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Transactions exists because households cannot manage long-term shared money by memory alone.

Young households make many small and large payments across bank accounts, cash, cards, wallets, partners, family, and merchants. Without a factual activity record, balances feel mysterious and financial conversations become guesswork.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility of Transactions is to record, preserve, and explain real money movement.

It answers:

- What money moved?
- Did money enter, leave, or move between owned containers?
- Which account or container was affected?
- When should the household understand the movement?
- What household meaning is known?
- Is the transaction resolved, still needing review, refunded, corrected, reversed, or historical?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is cash-flow truth.

A transaction is not a wish, budget, forecast, goal, or plan. It is a real ledger fact. Categories, jars, notes, and review decisions can explain a transaction, but they do not change the underlying money event.

## Business Outcome

Successful Transactions behavior produces:

- A trustworthy household activity history.
- Clear evidence behind account changes.
- Correct distinction between income, expense, and neutral transfers.
- Reviewable unresolved activity.
- Audit-safe recovery from refunds and corrections.
- Factual input for other domains without responsibility leakage.
