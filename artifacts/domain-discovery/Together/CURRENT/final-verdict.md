# Final Verdict

## Is This Domain Correctly Understood?

Yes, at discovery level.

Together is best understood as the household trust boundary. It defines who belongs to the shared financial space, what shared policies exist, and which material changes must be visible.

It is not a ledger, planner, bank connector, authentication system, relationship counselor, or legal registry.

## Knowledge Gaps

- How often target Vietnamese young households use formal joint accounts versus individual accounts treated as shared.
- Whether users understand "Household" as a natural term or prefer more relational language in conversation.
- How couples distinguish shared visibility from personal privacy.
- How financially relevant parents, siblings, or dependents should be understood without adding them as product members.
- How households handle separation, member exit, or lost trust in real practice.
- Whether "Admin" feels practical or hierarchical to target users.
- What level of policy-change history ordinary households actually review.

## Validation Needed Before Design Begins

- Interview Vietnamese couples with different pooling models.
- Review anonymized household money coordination examples such as chats, bill screenshots, contribution transfers, and monthly check-ins.
- Validate role language with both active money managers and less-engaged partners.
- Validate privacy expectations around personal accounts, wallet balances, and external bank data.
- Validate sensitive scenarios: former partner, wrong invite, hidden debt, family support, and unsafe visibility.
- Confirm current Vietnam regulatory implications with legal/compliance review before any external financial-data sharing design.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.82

Reasoning:

- High confidence in core domain purpose, boundaries, lifecycle, and household behavior patterns.
- Medium confidence in Vietnam-specific formal joint account adoption and long-term consent expectations.
- Lower confidence in safety-sensitive edge cases without direct user research.

The domain is stable enough for future business design, provided validation happens before turning these observations into product decisions.
