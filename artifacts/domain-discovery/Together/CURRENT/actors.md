# Actors

## User

The authenticated person using the product.

Responsibilities:

- Joins or creates a household.
- Views household financial information permitted by membership.
- Performs daily money actions in other domains.
- Carries personal identity and authentication credentials.

## Partner

A household member participating in shared finance.

Responsibilities:

- Shares household visibility.
- Contributes financial facts, decisions, or review.
- Understands shared policies.
- Acts within household trust expectations.

## Admin

A household member with elevated responsibility for household-level policies.

Responsibilities:

- Maintains policy settings.
- Invites or manages membership where applicable.
- Creates material changes that other partners must be able to see.

## Household

The practical financial unit.

Responsibilities:

- Provides the shared scope for money records.
- Owns shared assumptions.
- Serves as the unit of financial visibility, planning, and review.

## Family

People outside the formal household who affect financial decisions.

Responsibilities:

- May receive support or contribute support.
- May influence decisions through cultural, relational, or emergency needs.
- Does not automatically belong to the product household.

## Bank

Financial institution holding real accounts, cards, loans, or savings products.

Responsibilities:

- Owns legal account-holder records.
- Executes real money movement.
- Provides statements, balances, identity checks, and transaction evidence.

## Fintech / E-Wallet Provider

Payment or wallet provider used by a household member.

Responsibilities:

- Holds or facilitates wallet balances and payments.
- Performs KYC and payment-account linkage.
- Provides transaction evidence and notifications.

## Employer / Income Payer

Source of salary or income.

Responsibilities:

- Pays income to an individual account.
- May determine salary timing that shapes household planning cadence.

## Merchant / Biller

External party receiving payments.

Responsibilities:

- Creates obligations or receipts that the household may need to discuss.
- May issue refunds, corrections, or due-date changes.

## Third-Party Provider

Any connected service that can provide read-only financial, identity, notification, or communication information.

Responsibilities:

- Provides external data under its own rules.
- Does not define the household boundary.

## System

The product itself.

Responsibilities:

- Maintains household scope.
- Protects access based on membership.
- Presents shared policies and household context.
- Does not resolve relationship disputes.

## Background Worker

Automated process operating on household-scoped records.

Responsibilities:

- Performs scheduled or asynchronous tasks under existing household rules.
- Preserves attribution and scope.
- Does not become a household actor with independent financial judgment.
