# Capabilities

This document lists natural capabilities of the Together domain. It is not a roadmap.

## Core Capabilities

- Define a household.
- Identify active household members.
- Distinguish member roles.
- Represent partner participation.
- Invite a person into the household.
- Accept or decline an invitation.
- Recognize a pending, accepted, revoked, expired, or declined invitation.
- Define household-level policies.
- Expose policy state to members.
- Attribute material policy changes.
- Provide household scope to other domains.
- Protect household records from non-members.
- Identify whether a user has an active household.

## Optional Capabilities

- Display household-level preferences such as locale, timezone, and base currency.
- Track member display names and contact identity.
- Show recent policy events.
- Show membership history.
- Represent inactive members.
- Represent who initiated an invitation.
- Represent who changed a household policy.
- Support manual clarification of household meaning.

## Future Capabilities

- Multiple households per user.
- More complex family structures.
- Temporary access.
- Separation or household split workflows.
- Account-level consent records for external financial data.
- Household-level export and deletion audit.
- Partner engagement summaries.
- Consent renewal for connected providers.
- Cross-border household settings.

These are observations about possible domain evolution only.
