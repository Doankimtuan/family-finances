# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

- Reflect household financial condition.
- Summarize liquidity visibility.
- Identify pressure from open obligations.
- Identify pressure from unresolved decisions.
- Interpret plan presence and planning rhythm.
- Surface debt and card burden as risk signals.
- Show recent activity as evidence of tracking rhythm.
- Compare current condition with prior known condition.
- Explain the factors behind a score, level, or signal.
- Stay read-only and grounded in source-domain facts.

## Optional Capabilities

- Medical-expense exposure awareness.
- Emergency-buffer adequacy awareness.
- Income concentration awareness.
- Irregular income volatility awareness.
- Seasonal pressure awareness.
- Partner alignment awareness.
- Insurance coverage visibility.
- Reimbursement timing awareness.
- Data completeness awareness.
- Household confidence or stress check-ins as qualitative context.

## Future Capabilities

- Longer trend interpretation.
- Scenario comparison based on verified household facts.
- Cross-domain risk clustering.
- More nuanced protection-gap interpretation.
- Provider-derived read-only insurance or employment benefits context.
- Household lifecycle adjustments for children, elder care, housing, and retirement approach.

These are natural domain capabilities only. They are not roadmap commitments.
