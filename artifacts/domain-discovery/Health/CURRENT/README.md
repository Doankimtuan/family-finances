# Health Domain Discovery

## Overview

Health is the household's financial condition mirror. It answers: how stable does the household look, what pressures are visible, what risks need attention, and what should be noticed before money stress becomes unmanageable?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where young households commonly combine salary income, cash, bank accounts, e-wallets, informal family support, public health insurance, private medical costs, debt obligations, seasonal spending, and partner decision-making.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Health before business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, modify Source of Truth, or propose implementation.

## Scope

Included:

- Household financial health as an interpretive domain.
- Read-only assessment of stability, pressure, resilience, behavior, and risk.
- Vietnam-specific financial-health realities, including healthcare cost exposure because medical shocks materially affect household resilience.
- Boundaries with Accounts, Transactions, Planning, Goals, Loans, Cards, Inbox, Together, Month Close, and third-party providers.

Excluded:

- Medical diagnosis, treatment advice, clinical records, and provider selection.
- Investment advice, insurance advice, tax advice, or regulated financial planning.
- Automatic money movement or operational mutation of other domains.
- Product recommendations, implementation models, database, API, or UX proposals.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
