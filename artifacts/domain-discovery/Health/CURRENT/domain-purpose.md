# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

Health exists because households need a calm, honest mirror of financial condition. Individual transactions, accounts, debts, plans, and decisions can each be correct while the household as a whole is becoming fragile.

Young families often do not need more raw data. They need to understand whether their money life is stable enough for ordinary living, upcoming obligations, emergencies, family support, and long-term commitments.

## Core Responsibility

Health's core responsibility is to interpret household financial facts into a read-only view of condition, pressure, and resilience.

It should help answer:

- Is the household financially stable or strained?
- Are obligations, plans, and liquidity aligned?
- Are unresolved decisions increasing risk?
- Are recent patterns improving, weakening, or unclear?
- What real-world pressure is visible from existing facts?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle behind Health is resilience under uncertainty.

A household is financially healthier when it has:

- Grounded knowledge of real money.
- Enough liquidity for ordinary needs and shocks.
- Manageable debt and recurring obligations.
- Clear plans that do not pretend to be bank balances.
- Timely decisions instead of avoided uncertainty.
- A realistic buffer for irregular and medical expenses.

## Value Provided

Health provides reflection. It does not create money, move money, approve decisions, diagnose users, or replace financial judgment.

Its value is highest when it converts scattered household facts into an understandable condition signal without overstating precision.

## Product Responsibility

Health should solve these problems:

- Household cannot see overall financial condition from separate accounts, plans, debts, and decisions.
- Household does not know which visible facts are creating pressure.
- Household loses sight of resilience during ordinary spending rhythm.
- Household delays attention because the situation feels too vague.
- Household needs a shared, non-judgmental language for condition.

Health should not solve these problems:

- It should not execute transfers, repayments, or plan changes.
- It should not replace Accounts, Transactions, Planning, Goals, Cards, Loans, Inbox, or Together.
- It should not provide medical, credit, insurance, investment, tax, or legal advice.
- It should not decide what the household must do.
- It should not invent balances, obligations, income, claims, or risk facts.

## Long-Term Stability

The domain can survive 5-10 years if it remains an interpretive, read-only layer over durable household finance concepts: real money, obligations, plans, decisions, shocks, and resilience.

The model becomes unstable if Health starts owning source facts, making decisions, automating actions, or presenting advisory conclusions as certainty.
