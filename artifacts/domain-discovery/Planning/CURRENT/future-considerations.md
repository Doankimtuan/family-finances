# Future Considerations

These are observations about capabilities that may become useful in 3-5 years. They are not roadmap items, requirements, or product recommendations.

## Household Complexity

- Multi-income-source planning.
- Irregular income confidence ranges.
- Partner-specific salary timing.
- Child education milestones.
- Parent support and elder-care planning.
- Wedding, birth, relocation, home purchase, and major medical event planning.
- Extended-family or multi-household obligation context.

## Financial Product Complexity

- Debt payoff scenarios.
- Promotional-rate expiration awareness.
- Savings maturity ladder planning.
- Insurance premium and coverage planning.
- Investment contribution intention.
- Foreign-currency income or expense planning.
- Tax-aware household planning if validated as relevant.

## Data And Automation

- Recurring detection from transaction history.
- Provider bill extraction.
- Balance projection based on facts and assumptions.
- Cash-flow stress indicators.
- Stale assumption detection.
- Calendar import or export.
- Read-only provider integration for bill, card, loan, and savings dates.

## Collaboration

- Partner review notes.
- Agreement history.
- Private-versus-shared planning boundaries.
- Household policy review.
- Planning changes visible without blame.

## Advisory Maturity

- Scenario comparison.
- Emergency fund sufficiency modeling.
- Affordability stress tests.
- Life-event planning.
- AI-assisted explanations grounded only in household facts.

All items require future validation before design begins.
