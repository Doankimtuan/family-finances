# Final Verdict

## Is This Domain Correctly Understood?

Yes, at discovery level.

Planning is best understood as the household intention domain. It coordinates expected income, planned allocation, goals, recurring obligations, upcoming pressure, and periodic review. Its stability depends on never confusing plan state with real ledger truth.

## Knowledge Gaps

- How Vietnam young families name planning concepts in natural Vietnamese household language.
- How often target households use formal budgets versus mental envelopes.
- Whether partner conflict is caused more by missing facts, different priorities, privacy needs, or tone.
- How important irregular income is for the target segment.
- Which recurring obligations matter most by household stage.
- Whether users expect annual, Tet, school, and insurance planning in early product phases.
- How much users understand the difference between jar intention and bank balance without repeated teaching.

## Validation Needed Before Design Begins

- User interviews with young Vietnamese couples and families.
- Review of real household budgeting artifacts such as sheets, notes, chats, and app screenshots.
- Validation of Vietnamese terminology for jars, goals, planned amount, recurring obligation, and month review.
- Observation of payday planning behavior.
- Validation of cash, wallet, QR, and bank-transfer tracking pain.
- Partner review of blame, privacy, and shared-control risks.
- Confirmation of how current users interpret existing Plan, Jars, Goals, Recurring, Calendar, and Month Ritual concepts.

## Confidence Score

0.82

Confidence is high for the core domain boundary and financial principle. Confidence is moderate for Vietnam-specific household language and behavior details that require direct user validation.
