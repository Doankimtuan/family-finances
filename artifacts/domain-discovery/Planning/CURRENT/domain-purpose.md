# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

Planning exists because household money decisions happen before bank balances change. Families need a shared mental model for salary, bills, daily spending, goals, emergency reserves, debt pressure, and future obligations.

In real life, people rarely experience money as a single account balance. They experience it as commitments:

- Rent is already spoken for.
- School fees are coming.
- Groceries must last until payday.
- A loan payment cannot be missed.
- A holiday, laptop, baby expense, or family support goal needs protection.

Planning gives structure to those commitments without pretending that money has already moved.

## Core Responsibility

Planning owns household intention.

Its core responsibility is to represent expected use of money, desired allocation, recurring commitments, goals, and periodic review. It should make upcoming pressure and shared priorities understandable.

Planning does not own factual balances, bank truth, card debt truth, loan contract truth, savings product truth, or executed transactions.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle behind Planning is separation of intention from fact.

Real money is confirmed by accounts, transactions, providers, cash, and contracts. Planned money is a household decision about purpose, priority, timing, and trade-off. A good planning model must help people decide without blurring this distinction.

## Value Provided

Planning provides:

- Clarity about where income is meant to go.
- Shared agreement between partners.
- Early visibility into recurring pressure.
- Protection for goals and buffers.
- A place to reason about future obligations before they become stress.
- A review rhythm that helps the household learn from the month.

The value is behavioral and coordination-oriented, not banking execution.
