# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

These are naturally central to household Planning:

- Represent household income intention.
- Represent purpose-based jars or envelopes.
- Represent fixed and variable allocation targets.
- Represent active, paused, completed, cancelled, or archived intentions.
- Represent savings goals as intention.
- Represent recurring income and recurring expense expectations.
- Represent expected due dates.
- Distinguish planned money from real money.
- Compare intention against factual evidence.
- Support month or period review.
- Support corrections to intention.
- Support shared household visibility.
- Consume read-only facts from Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Savings, and Inbox.

## Optional Capabilities

These are natural but not always necessary for a simple household planning model:

- Annual or seasonal expense planning.
- Irregular income planning.
- Sinking funds for known future expenses.
- Debt payoff intention.
- Family-support planning.
- Goal priority ranking.
- Partner notes or decision context.
- Planned-versus-actual variance language.
- Calendar projection.
- Rollover awareness.
- Emergency-plan handling.
- Multiple planning periods.

## Future Capabilities

These may become relevant as households mature or data quality improves:

- Provider-assisted recurring detection.
- Household cash-flow forecasting.
- Scenario comparison.
- Major-life-event planning.
- Child education planning.
- Retirement or long-term wealth planning.
- Insurance coverage planning.
- Tax-aware planning.
- Multi-household or extended-family planning.
- Advisory-grade planning with explicit disclaimers and validation.

These are observations only, not roadmap commitments.
