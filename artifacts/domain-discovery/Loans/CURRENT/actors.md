# Actors

## User

The person who records, reviews, pays, or manages the loan in the household finance product.

Responsibilities:

- Enter known loan facts.
- Confirm repayment events.
- Interpret provider statements or informal agreements.
- Decide whether a tracked obligation still reflects reality.

## Partner

The household member affected by cash-flow pressure and repayment decisions.

Responsibilities:

- Share awareness of household obligations.
- Coordinate payment source, timing, and repayment priority.
- Validate whether informal or family borrowing is mutually recognized.

## Family

Relatives who may be lenders, co-borrowers, guarantors, helpers, or repayment beneficiaries.

Responsibilities:

- Provide or receive informal loan money.
- Negotiate flexible repayment expectations.
- Create social consequences when financial terms are unclear.

## Lender

The party owed money.

Responsibilities:

- Disburse money or purchasing power.
- Define terms, schedule, interest, fees, collateral, and collection rules.
- Confirm payments and outstanding balance.

## Bank

A regulated credit institution that may lend directly or hold payment accounts.

Responsibilities:

- Assess creditworthiness.
- Disburse loans.
- Collect repayments.
- Apply interest and fees.
- Report credit behavior where applicable.

## Finance Company / Fintech

A consumer-credit provider, BNPL provider, or app-based lender.

Responsibilities:

- Offer faster or smaller-ticket lending.
- Present contract terms.
- Collect installments.
- Manage late-payment and collection processes.

## Merchant / Seller

A seller that arranges store financing or installment purchase.

Responsibilities:

- Initiate financed purchase.
- Explain purchase-linked installment terms.
- Coordinate with a lender or finance partner.

## Employer

An employer may provide salary advances or payroll-linked loans.

Responsibilities:

- Advance funds.
- Recover through payroll or agreed transfer.
- Define whether the amount is a loan, wage advance, or benefit.

## Guarantor / Co-Borrower

A person legally or socially responsible if the borrower fails to pay.

Responsibilities:

- Accept contingent obligation.
- Provide identity, collateral, income support, or repayment backup.

## Credit Bureau / CIC

The external credit-information actor in Vietnam.

Responsibilities:

- Hold credit-history information.
- Influence future borrowing access.
- Reflect late payment, default, or repayment behavior where reported.

## System

The household finance product.

Responsibilities:

- Represent user-provided or imported loan information.
- Separate planned schedule from real money movement.
- Preserve loan history and status.
- Avoid pretending to be the lender of record.

## Background Worker

A product/system process that may check dates, status, imports, or reminders.

Responsibilities:

- Observe timing and state changes.
- Surface stale, due, or completed conditions if such capabilities exist.
- Avoid creating real-world truth without confirmation.

## Third-Party Provider

Any integration source such as bank API, statement parser, payment service, or document provider.

Responsibilities:

- Provide read-only loan, account, statement, rate, or payment evidence.
- May be incomplete, delayed, unavailable, or differently formatted.
