# Loans Domain Discovery

## Overview

Loans are household obligations created when money, goods, services, or purchasing power is received now and repaid later under agreed terms.

For young families in Vietnam, this includes bank loans, consumer finance loans, home and vehicle loans, tuition or medical loans, store financing, BNPL, and scheduled family or friend borrowing. The domain is about understanding repayment obligation, not about redesigning the product.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Loans before any business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is observational. It does not create product decisions, redesign ViNha, propose implementation, or modify any Source of Truth.

## Scope

Included:

- Borrowed principal, remaining principal, interest, fees, repayment terms, lender relationship, and obligation lifecycle.
- Scheduled repayment loans and installment-like obligations.
- Vietnam-first household practices, including formal and informal borrowing.
- Boundaries with Debts, Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Savings, Inbox, Calendar, and external providers.

Excluded:

- Product redesign.
- Implementation model, database model, API model, or UX proposal.
- Credit-card statement mechanics and revolving credit behavior except where they clarify boundaries.
- Investment leverage, business lending, tax/accounting treatment, or enterprise credit.
