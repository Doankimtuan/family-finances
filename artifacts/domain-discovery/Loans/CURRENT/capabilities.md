# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

Natural domain capabilities:

- Represent a loan obligation.
- Identify lender and borrower context.
- Track original principal.
- Track remaining principal.
- Represent loan purpose or type.
- Represent repayment term.
- Represent repayment frequency.
- Represent scheduled due dates.
- Represent expected payment amount.
- Distinguish principal, interest, and fees conceptually.
- Track real repayments.
- Track active, completed, cancelled, defaulted, or archived state.
- Preserve history after completion.
- Support comparison between planned repayment and actual repayment.
- Support awareness of overdue or upcoming obligations.

## Optional Capabilities

Capabilities that are real but not always required:

- Collateral tracking.
- Guarantor or co-borrower awareness.
- Variable-rate history.
- Promotional-rate period awareness.
- Early payoff estimate.
- Prepayment fee awareness.
- Grace period awareness.
- Restructure / reschedule awareness.
- Payment-method preference.
- Provider statement attachment.
- Contract evidence.
- Informal agreement notes.
- Credit-history impact awareness.
- Insurance or bundled product awareness.

## Future Capabilities

Capabilities that may matter over a longer horizon:

- Provider-confirmed loan import.
- Automatic reconciliation against bank statements.
- Rate benchmark and margin tracking.
- Multi-currency borrowing.
- Debt consolidation analysis.
- Household affordability stress testing.
- Refinancing comparison.
- Collateral exposure view.
- Co-borrower responsibility tracking.
- Regulatory disclosure normalization.

These are observations only, not roadmap items.
