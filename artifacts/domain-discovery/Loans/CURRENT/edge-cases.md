# Edge Cases

## Creation And Disbursement

- Approved loan is never disbursed.
- Partial disbursement.
- Disbursement goes to merchant, not borrower.
- Disbursement date differs from contract date.
- First payment date differs from start date.
- Loan is denominated in foreign currency.
- Informal loan has no written term.
- Borrowed amount includes insurance or fee rolled into principal.

## Schedule And Interest

- Promotional rate ends.
- Floating rate changes.
- Payment date falls on weekend or holiday.
- Month-end due date shifts for shorter months.
- Interest is calculated daily, monthly, flat, reducing balance, or by provider-specific convention.
- Equal monthly amount changes after rate reset.
- Grace period delays principal repayment.
- Balloon payment at end.
- Interest-only period.
- Fees are charged separately from scheduled payment.

## Payment Events

- Missed payment.
- Late payment.
- Partial payment.
- Overpayment.
- Early payoff.
- Payment reversed.
- Payment pending at bank but not posted by lender.
- Cash payment lacks proof.
- Payment posted to wrong loan.
- Payment split incorrectly between principal, interest, fee, or penalty.
- Partner pays from a different account.

## Correction And Dispute

- Wrong principal entered.
- Wrong interest rate entered.
- Lender revises schedule.
- User discovers hidden fees.
- Provider statement conflicts with household record.
- Loan was actually a gift.
- Family lender forgives part of the balance.
- Contract is cancelled after cooling-off or dispute.
- Fraudulent loan or identity misuse.

## Lifecycle Interruptions

- Borrower loses income.
- Loan becomes overdue.
- Loan is restructured.
- Loan is refinanced.
- Collateral is sold.
- Lender transfers loan to another entity.
- Collection agency becomes involved.
- Guarantor becomes responsible.
- Divorce, separation, or household membership change affects responsibility.

## Completion

- Final payment leaves a small residual balance.
- Lender closes loan later than expected.
- Early repayment fee applies.
- Payoff statement expires.
- Completion proof is missing.
- Closed loan remains visible in credit history.
- Archive happens before true closure.

No solutions are proposed in this discovery phase.
