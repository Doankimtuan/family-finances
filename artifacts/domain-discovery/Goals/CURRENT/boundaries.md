# Boundaries

## What Belongs Here

- Goal name.
- Goal purpose.
- Target amount.
- Optional target date.
- Perceived funded amount or progress.
- Goal contribution as intention/progress record.
- Goal lifecycle state.
- Goal-level notes and meaning.
- Household-level goal visibility.
- Distinction between goal intention and real money truth.

## What Belongs In Another Domain

Accounts:

- Real account balances.
- Cash balances.
- Wallet balances.
- Provider account identity.

Transactions:

- Executed income, expense, transfer, refund, correction, and payment facts.

Savings:

- Savings product balance.
- Interest rate.
- Tenor.
- Maturity.
- Withdrawal rules.
- Renewal or settlement.

Planning:

- Monthly allocation.
- Jars and envelopes.
- Recurring expectations.
- Month review.
- Calendar pressure.

Cards:

- Card balance.
- Statement due.
- Installment truth.
- Card repayment facts.

Loans:

- Loan principal.
- Interest.
- Contract schedule.
- Payoff truth.

Health:

- Read-only scoring, trends, and insight synthesis.

Inbox:

- Review items, confirmations, and decision queue.

Together:

- Household membership, partner visibility, permissions, and shared context.

## Where Integrations Happen

- Goals may read account or transaction facts for context.
- Goals may be associated with savings products without owning product truth.
- Planning may use goals as intention inputs.
- Health may read goals for analysis.
- Inbox may surface goal-related decisions if another domain creates review pressure.

## Where Ownership Changes

- When money actually moves, ownership belongs to Transactions, Accounts, Savings, Cards, Loans, or external providers.
- When a goal is fulfilled by purchase or payment, the real event belongs to the relevant money domain.
- When a savings product is opened for a goal, product truth belongs to Savings.
- When a goal affects monthly allocation, allocation behavior belongs to Planning or Jars.
