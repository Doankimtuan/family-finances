# Future Considerations

These are 3-5 year observations only. They are not roadmap, requirements, or recommendations.

- Goal evidence quality may matter if users connect more bank, wallet, or savings product data.
- Households may want to understand whether real money fully covers goal progress.
- Goal priority may become useful when families manage children, housing, parents, debt, and travel at the same time.
- Inflation-aware target review may matter for education, housing, healthcare, and imported goods.
- Savings product association may become useful where term deposits or flexible savings are used for specific purposes.
- Multi-source goal funding may matter for partner contributions, family gifts, bonuses, and cash.
- Life-event goal templates may become relevant for marriage, childbirth, relocation, school admission, and home purchase.
- Long-horizon goals may require separation from advisory-grade wealth planning.
- Goal history may matter for household reflection and partner trust.
- AI explanations may become useful only if grounded in existing facts and clear uncertainty.
- Provider-assisted matching may become useful but will need consent, reliability, and correction boundaries.
- Extended-family goals may matter if households support parents or siblings across homes.
