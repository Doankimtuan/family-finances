# Goals Domain Discovery

## Overview

Goals are named household intentions for future outcomes that require money, time, and prioritization. They answer: what are we trying to prepare for, how much do we believe it needs, how much progress do we think we have made, and when does it matter?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where young households often combine salary savings, cash, bank accounts, e-wallet balances, informal family support, term deposits, big purchases, education needs, Tet expenses, travel, emergency buffers, and partner negotiation.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Goals before business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, modify Source of Truth, or propose implementation.

## Scope

Included:

- Household savings and preparation goals.
- Goal intention, progress, target, deadline, pause, completion, and abandonment.
- Vietnam-specific household behavior around short-term and medium-term goals.
- Boundaries with Planning, Jars, Savings, Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Inbox, Health, Together, and external providers.

Excluded:

- Product recommendations.
- Implementation models.
- Database, API, or UX proposals.
- Investment advice, insurance advice, credit advice, or retirement planning.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
