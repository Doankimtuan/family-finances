# Final Verdict

## Is This Domain Correctly Understood?

Yes, at discovery depth.

Goals are best understood as named household future intentions, not money containers. They help young families protect priorities, communicate trade-offs, and interpret preparation progress across time.

The domain is stable if it preserves the separation between goal intention and real money truth. Goals may be informed by Accounts, Transactions, Savings, Cards, Loans, and Planning, but they should not own those facts.

## Knowledge Gaps

- Vietnamese terminology validation for goal, funded amount, contribution, emergency fund, sinking fund, and progress.
- Real user behavior around manual progress versus provider-backed evidence.
- How often young Vietnamese households use separate accounts, term deposits, wallets, or cash for goals.
- Whether partner-specific contribution visibility feels helpful or sensitive.
- How users interpret completed versus purchased versus funded.
- Whether date-driven goals need distinct overdue or expiration meaning.

## Validation Needed Before Design Begins

- Interview young households about actual goal examples from the last 12 months.
- Validate whether "funded amount" language is understood as intention, not balance.
- Validate Vietnamese copy for goal status and contribution.
- Observe whether users confuse Goals with Jars or Savings.
- Confirm how current users interpret existing Plan Goals behavior.
- Validate which goal types are common enough to matter for product language.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.82.

Rationale: The domain shape is clear and consistent with existing product boundaries, but Vietnam-specific terminology and actual household evidence-tracking behavior still need direct user validation before design begins.
