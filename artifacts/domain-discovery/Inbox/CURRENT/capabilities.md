# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

Natural Inbox capabilities:

- Collect pending financial decisions.
- Link an item to its source event or evidence.
- Present why attention is needed.
- Preserve current state.
- Support household resolution.
- Support dismissal or acknowledgement when no further action is needed.
- Preserve decision history.
- Distinguish active items from historical items.
- Carry enough context for the owning domain to act.
- Keep real money, virtual planning, and read-only information separate.

## Optional Capabilities

Capabilities that are natural but not essential in every household:

- Defer an item.
- Assign or target an item to a specific partner.
- Show suggested resolution based on prior behavior.
- Group similar items.
- Identify likely duplicate items.
- Track staleness.
- Expire time-bound reminders.
- Surface evidence such as receipt, invoice, or provider message.
- Capture user confidence or dispute notes.

## Future Capabilities

Capabilities that may become natural as volume, provider coverage, or AI quality increases:

- Exception-only review.
- Pattern-based auto-resolution with audit trail.
- Cross-provider matching.
- Provider-message classification.
- Household-specific decision learning.
- Richer invoice and receipt review.
- Review workload metrics.
- Partner coordination analytics.
- Regulatory or tax evidence retrieval for household business users.

These are observations only, not roadmap commitments.
