# User Behaviors

## Common Behaviors

- Check banking notifications quickly but postpone categorization.
- Ask a partner "what was this?" when a transaction is unfamiliar.
- Clear small obvious items first and leave ambiguous ones for later.
- Use screenshots as temporary evidence.
- Review spending in bursts: after salary, weekends, or month end.
- Treat repeated merchants as obvious after a few reviews.
- Ignore low-value items when life is busy.
- Reconstruct context from bank app history, chat messages, and memory.

## Expectations

- The household expects the queue to show only items that matter.
- Each item should explain why it needs attention.
- Resolution should feel faster than searching through a statement.
- The queue should not shame users for delay.
- Both partners should see a consistent financial picture.
- Items that are no longer relevant should not remain active forever.

## Frustrations

- Too many small decisions after a busy week.
- Missing merchant names or vague transfer descriptions.
- Duplicate reminders from multiple channels.
- One partner resolving an item without the other's context.
- Financial apps mixing alerts, marketing, and decisions in one feed.
- Unclear difference between "seen", "done", and "financially resolved".

## Mistakes

- Marking items done without making the underlying financial decision.
- Categorizing incorrectly just to empty the queue.
- Forgetting cash expenses until no evidence remains.
- Treating provider notifications as complete financial records.
- Dismissing reminders that still affect cash flow.
- Leaving stale items unresolved until reports become unreliable.

## Decision Patterns

- Obvious transaction: decide immediately.
- Repeated merchant: rely on memory or prior pattern.
- Ambiguous transaction: ask partner or defer.
- High amount: review more carefully.
- Due-date item: decide based on urgency and cash availability.
- Savings maturity: decide based on liquidity, rate, and upcoming plans.
- Refund or correction: compare against original purchase or expected amount.
