# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

Inbox exists because household financial life produces unresolved decisions faster than families can comfortably process them. A transaction may need a jar, a savings product may mature, a bill may need acknowledgement, or a provider message may require interpretation.

The domain's real-world reason is not message storage. It is decision surfacing.

## Core Responsibility

Inbox is responsible for collecting and presenting financial items that require household attention.

Its core responsibility is to turn "something needs review" into a visible, bounded decision. It should preserve the fact that a decision is pending, the source of that decision, its current state, and enough context for the household to understand why attention is needed.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle behind Inbox is accountability over drift.

Household finance becomes inaccurate when undecided items disappear into transaction lists, provider notifications, screenshots, chat messages, or memory. Inbox protects accuracy by making unresolved decisions explicit until they are resolved, dismissed, expired, or otherwise no longer active.

## Value Provided

Inbox provides:

- A shared place for financial attention.
- Reduced risk that unreviewed facts silently distort budgets or plans.
- A calmer review habit than searching across apps and statements.
- Decision history and accountability.
- A bridge between real-world events and household intention.

Inbox creates value when it helps the family make timely financial decisions. It loses value if it becomes a general notification feed, a task manager, or an automation engine that hides judgment.
