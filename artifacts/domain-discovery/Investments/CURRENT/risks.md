# Risks

## Business Risks

- Treating investment tracking as investment advice.
- Blurring investments with guaranteed savings products.
- Treating volatile market value as household cash.
- Encouraging short-term trading behavior through overemphasis on daily movement.
- Misrepresenting provider data freshness or completeness.
- Failing to handle informal investments honestly.
- Creating household conflict by exposing previously private risk-taking.

## Financial Risks

- Principal loss.
- Market volatility.
- Liquidity constraints.
- Concentration risk.
- Currency risk.
- Issuer default.
- Broker, custodian, or provider operational risk.
- Fraud, scam, or unauthorized trading.
- Margin/leverage loss exceeding original cash.
- Tax, fee, or spread reducing actual proceeds.
- Inflation risk if assets underperform household needs.

## UX Risks

- Users misunderstand unrealized gain as spendable money.
- Users panic during market downturns if values are too prominent.
- Users ignore stale manual valuations.
- Users cannot distinguish account cash from holdings.
- Users confuse fund NAV timing with live prices.
- Users cannot explain investment data to a partner.
- Too much financial terminology overwhelms beginner households.

## Misunderstanding Risks

- "Invested" does not mean money is still cash.
- "Current value" does not mean guaranteed sale value.
- "Dividend" is not salary.
- "Transfer to broker" is not investment return.
- "Gold value" depends on buy/sell spread, unit, and seller.
- "Bond" is not automatically risk-free.
- "Insurance investment value" is not equal to premiums paid.
- "Portfolio up" may be due to new contributions, not performance.

## Future Scalability Risks

- Asset classes vary widely in data shape.
- Provider integrations may be partial or inconsistent.
- Multi-currency and tax handling can expand complexity quickly.
- Corporate actions are frequent enough to matter but complex enough to overwhelm.
- Private assets need manual valuation and uncertainty language.
- Regulatory changes can affect products, settlement, and disclosure.
- International expansion changes tax, retirement, and brokerage assumptions.
