# Terminology

## Preferred Terms

| Term | Business definition |
|------|---------------------|
| Investment | Asset purchased or held with expected return and accepted risk. |
| Investment account | Account or platform where investment assets and related cash are held. |
| Holding | Specific asset position owned by the household. |
| Asset class | Broad investment type, such as stock, fund, bond, gold, crypto, private investment, or real estate. |
| Security | Tradable financial instrument such as share, bond, fund certificate, ETF, or warrant. |
| Fund | Pooled investment vehicle managed by a fund manager. |
| ETF | Exchange-traded fund traded on an exchange. |
| Open-ended fund | Fund whose units may be subscribed or redeemed according to fund rules. |
| Bond | Debt instrument that may pay coupon and return principal at maturity, subject to issuer risk. |
| Gold holding | Physical or provider-recorded gold treated as an investment/store of value. |
| Quantity / units | Number of shares, fund units, bonds, gold units, or other measurable ownership amount. |
| Cost basis | Amount paid for the holding, before or after fees depending on documented convention. |
| Market value | Estimated current value based on price, NAV, statement, or manual valuation. |
| Valuation date | Date/time the value was observed or estimated. |
| Unrealized gain/loss | Value change on a holding that has not been sold. |
| Realized gain/loss | Gain/loss after sale, redemption, maturity, write-off, or exit. |
| Dividend | Distribution from equity ownership. |
| Coupon | Interest paid by a bond. |
| Distribution | Fund or investment payout. |
| Redemption | Converting fund units or investment product back into cash under provider rules. |
| Settlement | Completion of trade cash/securities exchange after execution. |
| Custodian | Institution responsible for safekeeping or recordkeeping of assets. |
| Liquidity | How quickly and reliably the asset can become usable cash. |
| Volatility | Degree of price fluctuation. |

## Forbidden Synonyms

| Avoid | Use instead | Reason |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Guaranteed growth | Expected return or potential return | Investments can lose value. |
| Savings | Investments | Savings products and investments have different risk profiles. |
| Balance | Market value or account cash | "Balance" can imply cash certainty. |
| Profit | Gain | Profit can imply realized certainty. |
| Loss | Unrealized loss or realized loss | Loss type matters. |
| Available money | Market value or sale proceeds | Market value is not automatically spendable. |
| Safe investment | Lower-risk investment or capital-protection product | Safety is contextual and should not be overstated. |
| Portfolio account | Investment account | "Portfolio account" is imprecise. |
| Play money | Investment holding | Casual language hides real risk. |
| Crypto savings | Crypto holding | Crypto is not a savings product. |

## Consistency Rules

- Use "Investment" for risk-bearing assets.
- Use "Savings product" for fixed-term, bank-like products with stated terms.
- Use "Account" for real money containers.
- Use "Holding" for an asset position.
- Use "Market value" when value depends on price.
- Use "Investment account cash" when cash sits inside a broker/fund platform.
- Use "Unrealized" until sale, redemption, maturity, or write-off occurs.
