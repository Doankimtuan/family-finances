# Actors

## User

The person recording, viewing, or interpreting investment information. May be the investor, spouse, or household operator.

Responsibilities:

- Identify holdings and accounts.
- Provide manual values when provider data is unavailable.
- Distinguish investment transfers from household spending.
- Decide whether information is household-visible.

## Partner

The spouse or household partner affected by investment risk and returns.

Responsibilities:

- Understand household exposure.
- Participate in significant investment decisions when household money is involved.
- Raise questions about risk, liquidity, or trust.

## Family

Parents, siblings, relatives, or extended family involved in informal investment decisions, gold custody, property deals, or business funding.

Responsibilities:

- May hold assets physically or informally.
- May provide incomplete statements or verbal updates.
- May create social pressure or trust-based ambiguity.

## Securities Company

Licensed broker or securities firm that opens trading accounts, routes orders, holds securities records, and provides statements.

Responsibilities:

- Execute buy/sell orders.
- Maintain account cash and securities positions.
- Provide trade confirmations, fees, and portfolio reporting.
- Support deposits, withdrawals, and settlement.

## Fund Management Company

Entity managing mutual funds, ETFs, bond funds, money market funds, or other regulated funds.

Responsibilities:

- Manage fund portfolio.
- Publish NAV and fund reports.
- Process subscriptions and redemptions through permitted channels.
- Disclose fees, risks, and investment policy.

## Bank

Funding account provider, distributor, custodian partner, bond seller, or investment product channel.

Responsibilities:

- Move cash into or out of investment channels.
- Distribute funds, bonds, or insurance-linked products where applicable.
- Provide account statements and payment confirmation.

## Fintech Provider

Consumer app used for fund distribution, portfolio display, gold access, or investment education.

Responsibilities:

- Present provider-side investment information.
- Facilitate orders, recurring contributions, or redemptions where licensed/partnered.
- Provide app-level statements and notifications.

## Exchange

Market infrastructure where listed securities trade.

Responsibilities:

- Provide trading venue and market rules.
- Publish listed prices and market data.
- Coordinate with clearing and depository infrastructure.

## Depository / Clearing Institution

Central securities depository and clearing infrastructure.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain securities registration, depository, clearing, and settlement records.
- Coordinate settlement obligations between market participants.

## Issuer

Company, government, bank, or fund whose asset the household owns.

Responsibilities:

- Issue shares, bonds, fund certificates, or other investment instruments.
- Provide disclosures, corporate actions, coupons, dividends, or reports as required.

## Insurer

Provider of insurance-linked investment products.

Responsibilities:

- Administer policy, investment allocation, charges, protection coverage, and surrender value.
- Provide policy statements.

## System

The household finance product, if it observes this domain.

Responsibilities:

- Preserve objective records.
- Avoid investment advice unless a future, separately governed capability exists.
- Avoid treating volatile values as guaranteed cash.

## Background Worker

Automated process that may refresh valuations, detect stale data, or import provider information in a future implementation.

Responsibilities:

- Read provider data without changing real-world holdings.
- Flag stale or missing information.
- Preserve auditability of value changes.

## Third-Party Provider

External market data, broker feed, fund feed, price feed, or account aggregator.

Responsibilities:

- Provide read-only information about prices, holdings, transactions, or account values.
- Communicate data freshness, coverage, and limitations.
