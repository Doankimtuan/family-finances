# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

Investments exists because households sometimes move money from stable containers into assets whose future value can rise or fall. This creates financial reality that is different from cash, spending, debt, term savings, and planning intentions.

The domain exists to understand real investment holdings as assets with risk, valuation uncertainty, liquidity constraints, ownership records, and life events.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility of Investments is to represent the household's investment position as real-world financial holdings.

It concerns:

- What investment asset is held.
- Who holds or controls it.
- Where it is custodied or recorded.
- How much was contributed.
- What the current value is believed to be.
- What income or proceeds have occurred.
- Whether gain/loss is realized or unrealized.
- What liquidity or restriction affects exit.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is risk-based wealth building.

Unlike Savings products, Investments usually do not guarantee principal or return. The household accepts uncertainty in exchange for possible long-term growth, income, diversification, inflation protection, or ownership exposure.

## Value Provided

This domain provides clarity about:

- Household assets that are not ordinary cash.
- Exposure to market movement.
- Difference between invested principal and current value.
- Whether money is liquid, locked, volatile, or difficult to verify.
- How investment activity affects real money without confusing it with everyday spending or virtual plans.

This discovery does not decide whether or how ViNha should support the domain.
