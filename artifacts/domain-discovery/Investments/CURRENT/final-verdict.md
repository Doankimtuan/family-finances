# Final Verdict

## Is This Domain Correctly Understood?

Yes, at discovery level.

Investments are best understood as risk-bearing household assets whose value, liquidity, and return are uncertain until realized. The domain is separate from Accounts, Transactions, Savings, Goals, Planning, Health, and Together.

The most important discovered distinction is:

- Accounts hold cash.
- Transactions record cash movement.
- Savings products have defined principal/rate/term behavior.
- Investments hold risk-bearing assets whose value may change without cash movement.
- Planning and Goals express intention, not ownership.

## Knowledge Gaps

- How ordinary Vietnamese young families currently split between stocks, open-ended funds, gold, real estate, crypto, and informal family investments.
- Which investment products the target users actually hold today.
- How much detail users expect: account-level only, holding-level, or performance-level.
- Whether households want partner-visible investment records or private-per-partner visibility.
- How often users can provide reliable current values manually.
- Which local providers, if any, offer reliable data export or integration paths.
- Tax treatment and reporting expectations for target households.

## Validation Needed Before Design Begins

- Interview target households about actual holdings and language.
- Validate whether "Investments" or "Wealth" is the better user-facing concept.
- Validate confusion points between savings products, funds, bonds, and gold.
- Validate whether users need current value, contribution history, liquidity, or performance first.
- Validate partner trust and privacy expectations.
- Validate Vietnam-specific provider data availability and statement formats.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.78

Rationale:

The domain's financial boundaries are stable and well-understood. Confidence is reduced by Vietnam-specific household adoption differences, informal investment behavior, provider-data uncertainty, and fast-changing securities-market infrastructure.
