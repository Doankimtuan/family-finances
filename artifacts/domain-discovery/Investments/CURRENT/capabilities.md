# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

Natural capabilities of the domain:

- Identify investment accounts or custody locations.
- Record investment holdings.
- Distinguish asset classes.
- Track contribution amount.
- Track quantity or units where applicable.
- Track cost basis.
- Track estimated current value.
- Track valuation date and source.
- Track realized gain/loss after exit.
- Track unrealized gain/loss before exit.
- Track dividends, coupons, distributions, and sale proceeds.
- Distinguish cash movement from value movement.
- Distinguish liquid and illiquid holdings.
- Preserve ownership and household visibility context.
- Preserve historical holdings after sale or closure.

## Optional Capabilities

Capabilities that naturally exist but are not always needed:

- Portfolio allocation by asset class.
- Investment account cash balance.
- Price/NAV refresh.
- Provider statement import.
- Manual valuation confidence.
- Fee and tax tracking.
- Corporate action tracking.
- Foreign currency value.
- Margin and leverage visibility.
- Risk labels or volatility context.
- Household contribution versus gifted/inherited asset distinction.
- Document attachment for receipts, confirmations, or statements.

## Future Capabilities

Capabilities that may become relevant over time:

- Broker or fund provider integrations.
- Multi-currency performance reporting.
- Time-weighted or money-weighted return analysis.
- Household net-worth contribution.
- Concentration and diversification visibility.
- Retirement or education portfolio framing.
- Private-market or family-business investment tracking.
- Regulatory/tax document summaries.
- Read-only investment health signals.

These are observations only, not roadmap commitments.
