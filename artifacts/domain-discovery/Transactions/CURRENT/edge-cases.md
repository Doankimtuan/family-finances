# Edge Cases

## Real-World Edge Cases

- Merchant cancels a payment before settlement.
- Card authorization amount differs from posted amount.
- Hotel, fuel, or rental deposits create temporary holds.
- Refund is partial.
- Refund arrives to a different account or wallet.
- Refund arrives after the household month has closed.
- Merchant issues store credit instead of cash refund.
- Provider reverses a mistaken transfer.
- Bank charges a fee after the original payment.
- Cash spending is recorded late from memory.
- Duplicate transaction appears from manual entry plus provider import.
- Same merchant appears with multiple names.
- QR transfer description is blank, generic, or wrong.
- Partner records the same shared expense twice.
- Transfer between own accounts is treated as an expense.
- Credit card payment is counted separately from card purchases.
- Salary is split across bank accounts.
- Employer reimbursement offsets a prior household expense.
- Family support transfers are partly gift, partly loan, or partly shared bill.
- Currency conversion changes between authorization and posting.
- Provider date differs from user memory because of cutoff times.
- Leap days, weekends, bank holidays, and time zones affect posting dates.
- Account is closed after transactions exist.
- Category or jar is archived after transactions reference it.
- A chargeback reverses a transaction months later.
- Subscription renews unexpectedly.
- BNPL or installment purchase creates several related money events.
- Cash withdrawal is followed by many unrecorded cash expenses.
- Manual adjustment is needed to align household records with reality.
- Information is missing: merchant, note, account, category, receipt, or original transaction link.

## Discovery Note

This file identifies cases only. It does not define handling rules or product solutions.
