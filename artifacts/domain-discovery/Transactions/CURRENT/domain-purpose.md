# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

The Transactions domain exists because household finance needs a truthful record of money movement before it can support planning, accountability, reconciliation, or reflection.

Balances show the current position. Transactions explain how the household arrived there.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility of Transactions is to record real money movement as factual events.

It answers:

- Did money enter, leave, or move between real containers?
- Which account or provider recorded the event?
- What amount and currency were involved?
- What date should the household use for review?
- What category, purpose, note, or mapping is known?
- Has the event been refunded, reversed, corrected, or left unresolved?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is cash flow with auditability.

Money is not only a balance. It moves through time. A household can trust its financial view only when movements are traceable, directionally clear, and not confused with intentions, forecasts, or budgets.

## Value Provided

Transactions provide:

- A factual activity history.
- Evidence behind account balances.
- A shared household memory.
- Inputs for category review and planning allocation.
- Reconciliation against bank, wallet, cash, and card records.
- A basis for insight without becoming advice itself.

The domain's value is truth and traceability. It records what happened; it does not decide what should happen next.
