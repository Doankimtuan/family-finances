# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

Categories exist because raw money movement is not enough for household understanding. A transaction records that money moved; a category explains the real-world meaning of that movement.

For a young household, "250,000 VND expense" is incomplete. "250,000 VND for groceries" is usable memory, planning evidence, and conversation context.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility of Categories is classification.

Categories provide a consistent household vocabulary for labeling income and expense facts. They make transaction history understandable and help other domains interpret patterns without changing the original financial fact.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is purpose-based classification.

Classification groups individual money events into meaningful families. It does not create money, move money, reserve money, repay debt, earn interest, or enforce limits. It turns ledger facts into interpretable evidence.

## Value Provided

Categories provide:

- Shared household language for money.
- Faster understanding of transaction history.
- Better spending and income pattern recognition.
- A bridge between real ledger facts and virtual planning interpretation.
- Cleaner communication between partners.
- A way to compare actual behavior over time.

## What Categories Are Not

Categories are not accounts, jars, budgets, goals, rules engines, payment methods, merchants, balances, or financial products.

They describe money events. They do not own money.
