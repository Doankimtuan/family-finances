# Capabilities

This list describes what the Categories domain is naturally capable of in the real world. It is not a feature proposal.

## Core Capabilities

- Define a household category.
- Distinguish income categories from expense categories.
- Categorize a transaction.
- Leave a transaction uncategorized when meaning is unknown.
- Correct a wrong category.
- Use categories to filter transaction history.
- Use categories to summarize spending or income by purpose.
- Maintain household-specific vocabulary.
- Preserve category meaning for historical interpretation.
- Separate category meaning from account, merchant, payment method, and plan.
- Provide classification evidence to other domains.

## Optional Capabilities

- Rename a category.
- Archive a category from future use.
- Restore a category.
- Merge category meaning.
- Split a mixed transaction across multiple categories.
- Attach visual identity such as icon or color.
- Use merchant or provider hints for suggested categorization.
- Detect duplicate or near-duplicate categories.
- Track category confidence for inferred labels.
- Support category-specific notes or explanations.

## Future Capabilities

- Transparent merchant-to-category learning.
- Better Vietnamese merchant normalization.
- Receipt-line or item-level classification.
- Household-specific category templates by life stage.
- Cross-provider category reconciliation.
- Longitudinal category drift detection.
- Category portability for exports or financial-adviser review.
- Support for international or multi-currency category contexts.

## Natural Limits

Categories cannot naturally:

- Hold money.
- Represent available balance.
- Execute payment.
- Enforce a spending cap.
- Replace a jar or budget.
- Certify provider truth.
- Decide household intent without user or household context.
