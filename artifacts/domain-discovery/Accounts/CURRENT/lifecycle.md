# Lifecycle

## Beginning

An account begins when a real-world container exists or when the household decides to track an existing container.

Common starts:

- A bank account is opened.
- A salary account becomes relevant to household money.
- Cash is counted and tracked.
- An e-wallet is installed or linked.
- A credit card is issued.
- A savings product is opened.
- A household adds a first account during onboarding.
- An old account is added for history or reconciliation.

## Middle

During active life, the account may:

- Receive income.
- Pay expenses.
- Send and receive transfers.
- Fund savings products.
- Settle obligations.
- Accumulate fees or interest.
- Become the source for transaction records.
- Be reconciled against external statements.
- Change name, institution metadata, or visibility relevance.
- Become temporarily stale if not updated.

## End

An account ends operationally when it is no longer used as an active money container.

Common endings:

- Bank account closed.
- E-wallet abandoned or closed.
- Cash container no longer tracked.
- Credit card closed or cancelled.
- Savings product matures and is settled.
- Account is duplicated and one record becomes historical.
- Household no longer considers the account part of shared finances.

## Possible Exits

- Closure with zero balance.
- Transfer of remaining money to another account.
- Archival while preserving history.
- Migration to a new institution.
- Removal from active household totals.

## Possible Interruptions

- Bank app unavailable.
- Account frozen.
- Card blocked.
- Wallet restricted pending verification.
- Lost phone or authentication issue.
- Pending transfer not completed.
- Disputed transaction.
- Missing statement.
- Provider integration failure.
- Household member loses access or leaves.

## Possible Recoveries

- Reconciliation after statement review.
- Manual cash count.
- Re-linking or re-authenticating external provider.
- Restoring account access with bank or wallet provider.
- Correcting duplicate, missed, or wrong-account transactions.
- Marking an account historical after closure.
- Reconstructing balance from opening balance plus known movements.

No lifecycle stage requires product redesign in this discovery document.
