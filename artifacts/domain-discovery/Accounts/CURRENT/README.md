# Accounts Domain Discovery

## Overview

Accounts are the household's named containers of real money and real financial obligations. They answer: where is the money, who holds it, and what real-world institution or place would confirm it?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where ordinary households commonly combine bank payment accounts, physical cash, e-wallets, debit cards connected to bank accounts, credit cards, term savings, and occasional informal family-held cash.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Accounts before any business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is intentionally observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, or modify existing Sources of Truth.

## Scope

Included:

- Real money containers used by households.
- Account identity, balance reality, liquidity, ownership, access, visibility, and reconciliation.
- Vietnam banking, e-wallet, QR transfer, cash, and household usage patterns.
- Boundaries with Transactions, Cards, Savings, Jars, Goals, Health, Inbox, Together, and external providers.

Excluded:

- New product features.
- Implementation models.
- Database, API, or UX proposals.
- Official business-rule changes.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
