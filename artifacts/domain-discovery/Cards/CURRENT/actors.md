# Actors

## User

The household member who records, reviews, or interprets card activity. The user may be the primary cardholder, supplementary cardholder, or only a household observer.

## Partner

A household member who may share repayment responsibility, use a supplementary card, review spending, or contribute money toward repayment.

## Family

Dependents, parents, or relatives who may use a supplementary card, receive card-funded purchases, influence spending choices, or contribute to repayment.

## Primary Cardholder

The person or organization that contracts with the issuer. For credit cards, this actor is normally responsible for debt, fees, interest, and supplementary-card obligations.

## Supplementary Cardholder

An authorized user of a card under the primary cardholder relationship. This actor can create card transactions but may not hold the issuer contract.

## Card Issuer

The bank, finance company, or eligible institution that issues the card, grants limits, sets terms, collects fees, bills the cardholder, handles risk, and owns the issuer-side card record.

## Card Acquirer

The institution that enables merchants to accept card payments and routes transactions into the card payment system.

## Card Network

The domestic or international network that defines acceptance, routing, messaging, dispute, and settlement rules. In Vietnam this can include NAPAS for domestic infrastructure and international card organizations for international cards.

## Merchant

The seller accepting card payment. The merchant initiates the purchase or refund flow and may participate in disputes.

## Payment Gateway or POS Provider

The technical provider that captures card credentials or card-present signals for e-commerce, POS, mPOS, or contactless transactions.

## Bank Account Provider

The provider of the underlying payment account for debit-card funding or credit-card repayment.

## Fintech or E-Wallet

A provider that may tokenize cards, store card credentials, initiate card payments, or combine card rails with wallet flows.

## System

The household finance product that records card information, classifies card events, separates real-money movement from credit obligation, and presents read-only or user-entered card facts.

## Background Worker

A system actor that may detect due dates, statement cycles, stale records, reminders, imports, or reconciliation differences. This is a conceptual actor only in discovery.

## Third-Party Provider

Any external data source, aggregator, statement parser, notification service, or bank-feed provider that supplies card-related information.

## Regulator

The State Bank of Vietnam and relevant authorities that define card-operation, payment, consumer, security, and foreign-exchange rules.
