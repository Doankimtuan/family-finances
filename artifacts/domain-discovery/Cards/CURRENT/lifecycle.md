# Lifecycle

## Beginning

- Household identifies a need for card access, credit, online payment, travel, rewards, or installment purchasing.
- User applies or registers through a bank, finance company, or provider.
- Issuer performs identification, eligibility, credit assessment, and contract setup.
- Card is issued as physical, virtual, or both.
- User activates the card, sets PIN or security controls, and receives limit, statement, due-date, fee, and interest terms.

## Middle

- Card is used for purchases, cash withdrawals, online payments, subscriptions, tokenized wallet payments, or installments.
- Issuer authorizes or declines transactions.
- Transactions move from pending or authorized state into posted statement activity.
- Fees, interest, rewards, cashback, refunds, chargebacks, or reversals may appear.
- Statement closes.
- Household pays in full, pays partially, pays minimum, misses payment, or converts eligible purchases into installments.
- Available credit and outstanding obligation change over time.

## End

- Card is fully paid and closed by the user or issuer.
- Card expires and is not renewed.
- Card is replaced after loss, fraud, damage, or reissue.
- Credit line is cancelled, frozen, charged off, or transferred.
- Prepaid card value is exhausted or expires under terms.

## Possible Exits

- Voluntary closure.
- Product migration by issuer.
- Upgrade or downgrade to another card.
- Limit cancellation.
- Account closure.
- Debt payoff followed by closure.
- Inactivity closure.

## Possible Interruptions

- Card lost or stolen.
- Suspected fraud.
- Temporary block.
- Declined authorization.
- Limit reached.
- Payment late.
- Statement disputed.
- Merchant cancellation.
- Refund delay.
- Issuer app or network outage.
- Card expiry before replacement arrives.

## Possible Recoveries

- Card unblocked.
- Replacement card issued.
- Transaction reversed.
- Dispute resolved.
- Refund posted.
- Late payment cured.
- Limit restored.
- Statement corrected.
- Repayment completed.

No implementation details are implied by this lifecycle.
