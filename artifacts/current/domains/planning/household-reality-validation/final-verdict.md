# Final Verdict

## Does This Domain Accurately Reflect Real Household Financial Behavior?

Yes.

Planning accurately reflects a real young-household need: families need to decide what money is meant to do before real money moves. Vietnamese households may not use formal budgeting language, but they already plan around salary, rent, debt, bills, family support, goals, school costs, emergencies, and seasonal expenses.

## Strengths

- Solves a real and frequent problem: committed money is not visible in account balance.
- Matches natural behavior around salary allocation, goals, and recurring obligations.
- Supports shared finance between partners.
- Protects the critical distinction between real money truth and household intention.
- Becomes more valuable as the household gains children, debt, savings products, and multiple accounts.
- Creates a place for learning from the month without changing ledger truth.

## Weaknesses

- Terminology may be fragile in Vietnam-first household language.
- Jars, capacity, reallocation, and virtual movement may feel abstract.
- Month review may feel useful to some households and burdensome to others.
- Partner visibility carries blame and privacy risk.
- Irregular income, seasonal expenses, and family support may be under-specified in the current understanding.
- Manual maintenance tolerance remains unproven over time.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The core domain is strongly validated by realistic household finance behavior and Phase 1 discovery. Confidence is reduced by unvalidated terminology, partner dynamics, manual-work tolerance, irregular income, and seasonal Vietnam-specific planning patterns.

## Validation Completeness

Completeness: Medium-high.

Validated from:

- Phase 1 Planning discovery.
- Existing household-reality validation patterns.
- Current product artifacts and Plan domain boundaries.
- Realistic Vietnam-first young household behavior.

Still incomplete:

- Direct user interviews.
- Vietnamese-language terminology testing.
- Observed payday planning sessions.
- Household artifact review.
- Longitudinal manual maintenance evidence.
- Partner conflict and privacy research.

## Readiness For Product Decision Phase

Readiness: Conditionally ready.

The domain is ready for Product Decision discussion at the principle and boundary level. Before final decisions, the team needs validation of terminology, Month Ritual comprehension, jar-versus-account understanding, partner trust dynamics, irregular income relevance, seasonal expense handling, and manual-maintenance tolerance.
