# Cross-Domain Impact

This document describes interactions only. It does not redesign boundaries.

## Accounts

Planning reads account context to understand real position. Accounts remain the source of where money is. Jars, goals, and planned capacity must not be treated as accounts.

## Transactions

Transactions provide factual evidence for plan review, mismatch explanation, and recurring expectation validation. Planning must not create or rewrite transaction truth.

## Cards

Planning may read card due pressure and repayment context. Cards own credit limit, statement, outstanding amount, and due truth. Debt payoff intention in Planning remains virtual.

## Loans

Planning may read expected repayment pressure. Loans own contract, schedule, rate, payoff, and repayment truth. Planning does not own loan balance.

## Planning

Planning owns intention, allocation, jars, goals, recurring expectations, review, and correction. Approved decisions strengthen Planning as a bounded intention domain.

## Goals

Goals are approved as Planning intention. When a real savings product backs a goal, Savings owns product truth and Planning owns purpose context.

## Inbox

Inbox may receive unresolved planning decisions or review items from adjacent domains. Planning should not turn every adjustment into Inbox work unless a discrete household decision is needed.

## Health

Health may read Planning as input for read-only interpretation. Health must not mutate Planning, create allocations, or provide advisory-grade financial planning.

## Categories

Categories explain household meaning and may inform plan review. Categories must not become virtual jars or imply money movement.

## Together

Together governs household membership, rights, and partner-visible policy. Planning decisions around shared visibility, assumptions, and family support depend on Together clarity.
