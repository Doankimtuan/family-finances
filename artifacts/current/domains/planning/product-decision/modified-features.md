# Modified Features

Capabilities below are approved only with scope or language modification before implementation planning.

| ID | Original idea | Required modification | Reason | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PL-PD-002 | Purpose-based jars or envelopes. | Present jars as intention containers only; never as balances or real accounts. | Prevents BR-01 confusion. | Users understand "what money is for" without thinking money moved. |
| PL-PD-005 | Savings goals as intention. | Keep goals separate from Savings product truth. | Avoids duplicating provider balances or maturity logic. | Goals stay motivational and safe. |
| PL-PD-006 | Recurring expectations. | Treat recurring items as expected, not paid or provider-confirmed. | Prevents false confidence. | Users remember obligations without assuming payment occurred. |
| PL-PD-007 | Expected due dates. | Label as expected unless source domain confirms due truth. | Cards, Loans, Savings, and billers own factual due data. | Calendar pressure remains useful and honest. |
| PL-PD-009 | Compare intention with facts. | Use plain mismatch explanation, not advanced accounting variance. | Reduces shame and cognitive load. | Review supports learning. |
| PL-PD-010 | Month or period review. | Keep lightweight and validate "Month Ritual" language with users. | Month review is useful but can feel ceremonial or tedious. | Closure without homework. |
| PL-PD-012 | Shared household visibility. | Scope around partner trust, privacy, and blame sensitivity. | Shared finance can create surveillance risk. | Planning supports collaboration. |
| PL-PD-016 | Sinking funds. | Express through simple goals or jars rather than a separate advanced model. | Avoids over-engineering. | Known future expenses can be understood simply. |
| PL-PD-017 | Debt payoff intention. | Keep as planning intention; Cards and Loans own debt truth. | Prevents payoff plans from becoming balance truth. | Households can prepare without duplicating obligations. |
| PL-PD-018 | Family-support planning. | Keep as ordinary planned meaning until Vietnam terminology is validated. | Family support is important but socially sensitive. | Supports real behavior without premature taxonomy. |
| PL-PD-021 | Planned-versus-actual variance language. | Use calm household language; avoid punitive budget framing. | Reduces shame and blame. | Mismatch becomes learning, not failure. |
| PL-PD-022 | Calendar projection. | Keep projection as expected pressure, not forecasted provider truth. | Prevents false certainty. | Users see upcoming pressure safely. |
| PL-PD-024 | Emergency-plan handling. | Frame as intention adaptation and reallocation only. | Emergencies may involve real money elsewhere. | Household can recover plan without implying money moved. |
| PL-PD-036 | AI-assisted planning explanations. | Future-only assistive explanation grounded in facts; no invented balances, advice, or autonomous actions. | Protects BR-14, BR-24, and financial safety. | AI can explain only after facts and policy are mature. |
