# Final Verdict

Planning is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear cautions.

The domain solves a validated young-household problem: money can look available while already committed. Planning gives households a shared intention layer without claiming to be real money truth.

- Jars, goals, recurring expectations, due dates, review, shared visibility, sinking funds, debt payoff intention, family support, variance language, calendar projection, emergencies, and AI explanations are approved only with strict modifications.
- Seasonal planning, irregular income, partner notes, rollover, multiple periods, provider-assisted detection, forecasting, scenarios, life-event planning, education, retirement, insurance, and multi-household planning are deferred.
- Tax-aware planning and advisory-grade financial planning are rejected.

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat expected income as received income.
- Treat recurring expectations as paid transactions.
