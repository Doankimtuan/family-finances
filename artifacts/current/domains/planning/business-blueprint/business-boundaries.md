# Business Boundaries

## What Belongs Here

Planning owns:

- Income expectation.
- Allocation intention.
- Purpose-based jars.
- Goal intention.
- Recurring expectations.
- Expected due pressure.
- Planning review.
- Planning correction.
- Emergency adaptation as virtual reallocation.
- Shared household planning visibility.
- Read-only interpretation of source facts for planning context.

## What Belongs Elsewhere

| Responsibility | Owning domain |
| --- | --- |
| Real account position | Accounts |
| Real money movement | Transactions |
| Credit-card statement, due amount, limit, and repayment truth | Cards |
| Loan contract, schedule, rate, payoff, and repayment truth | Loans |
| Savings product balance, rate, maturity, renewal, withdrawal, settlement | Savings |
| Discrete household decision queue | Inbox |
| Read-only health interpretation | Health |
| Category meaning labels | Categories |
| Household membership, rights, partner-visible policy | Together |

## What Planning Must Never Own

Planning must never own:

- Bank balance.
- Account balance.
- Transaction truth.
- Paid status.
- Provider-confirmed due truth.
- Card debt truth.
- Loan balance or payoff quote.
- Savings product balance or maturity truth.
- Health score mutation.
- Tax advice.
- Advisory-grade financial recommendations.
- Autonomous money movement.

## Responsibility Leakage Prevention

- A jar is not an account.
- A goal is not a savings product.
- A recurring expectation is not a payment.
- A due date is expected unless source-confirmed.
- A calendar projection is pressure, not guaranteed forecast.
- A correction to Planning is not a correction to Ledger.
- An AI explanation is not financial advice or action.
