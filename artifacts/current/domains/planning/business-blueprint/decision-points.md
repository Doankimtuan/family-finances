# Decision Points

## Create Intention

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- A new expected income, jar, goal, recurring expectation, due pressure, or support need appears.

Possible outcomes:

- Draft.
- Active.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Establishes new household planning meaning.

## Allocate

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Expected income must be assigned to purposes.

Possible outcomes:

- Allocation accepted.
- Allocation needs review.
- Allocation rejected as invalid.

Business impact:

- Defines what money is intended to do without moving money.

## Edit

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Amount, timing, purpose, or assumption changed.

Possible outcomes:

- Adjusted.
- Needs Review.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Keeps plan aligned with household reality.

## Pause

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Intention is temporarily inactive.

Possible outcomes:

- Paused.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Removes intention from active allocation without erasing it.

## Resume

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- A paused intention becomes relevant again.

Possible outcomes:

- Active.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Restores planning purpose.

## Complete

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Household considers intention fulfilled.

Possible outcomes:

- Completed.
- Needs Review.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Ends active planning for the intention without proving real money movement.

## Cancel

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Intention is abandoned.

Possible outcomes:

- Cancelled.
- Historical.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Stops future planning pressure.

## Archive

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Intention is no longer active but should remain interpretable.

Possible outcomes:

- Archived.
- Historical.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Keeps active Planning focused.

## Review

Who:

- Household member or partners together.

Why:

- Household needs to understand the period.

Possible outcomes:

- Reviewed.
- Locked.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Creates closure and learning.

## Correct

Who:

- Household member with planning rights.

Why:

- Prior planning information was wrong.

Possible outcomes:

- Corrected.
- Active.
- Historical.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Repairs planning trust without mutating real money truth.

## Reallocate During Emergency

Who:

- Household member with planning rights; partner review may be needed by policy.

Why:

- Emergency changes priorities.

Possible outcomes:

- Adjusted.
- Needs Review.
- Invalid Attempt.

Business impact:

- Changes intention only; real spending remains elsewhere.

## Challenge Or Reject Partner Assumption

Who:

- Partner or household member.

Why:

- Planning assumption is unclear, sensitive, or disputed.

Possible outcomes:

- Needs Review.
- Adjusted.
- Cancelled.
- Active after agreement.

Business impact:

- Protects shared trust and prevents silent household disagreement.
