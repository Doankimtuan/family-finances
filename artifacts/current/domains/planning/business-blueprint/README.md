# Planning Business Blueprint

## Overview

Planning is ViNha's household intention domain. It describes what household money is expected to do before real money moves, while preserving the distinction between real ledger truth and virtual planning.

This blueprint converts the approved and modified Planning Product Decision Board outcomes into a deterministic business contract for future implementation planning.

## Scope

Included:

- Income intention.
- Purpose-based jars or envelopes.
- Fixed and variable allocation targets.
- Intention lifecycle states.
- Savings goals as intention.
- Recurring expectations.
- Expected due dates and calendar pressure.
- Month or period review.
- Corrections to planning intention.
- Shared household visibility.
- Read-only facts from adjacent domains.
- Emergency adaptation as virtual planning.

Excluded:

- Tax-aware planning.
- Advisory-grade financial planning.
- Autonomous planning automation.
- Provider truth ownership.
- Real ledger mutation.
- Health write-backs.
- APIs, databases, DTOs, events, technical contracts, and implementation details.

## Business Responsibility

Planning owns household intention. It answers:

- What money is expected?
- Where is money meant to go?
- What goals are being protected?
- What recurring pressure is expected?
- What should the household review together?

Planning does not answer:

- Where is money actually held?
- Did money actually move?
- What is the legal card, loan, or savings product truth?
- What financial advice should the household follow?

## Relationship With Previous Phases

- Phase 1 discovered Planning as virtual household intention.
- Phase 2 validated that young Vietnamese households need salary allocation, goals, recurring obligations, shared planning, and review.
- Phase 3 approved the simple Planning core and modified risk-sensitive capabilities to protect BR-01 and financial safety.

This blueprint documents business behavior only. It does not modify any Source of Truth.
