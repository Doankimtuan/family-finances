# Target Users

## Primary Users

Young Vietnamese household members aged 20-35 who use cards as part of shared financial life.

Primary users commonly include:

- A partner who owns the main credit card.
- A partner who manages household payments.
- A spouse who reviews spending and due dates.
- A young parent coordinating family purchases, subscriptions, school fees, travel, or medical costs.

## Secondary Users

Secondary users include:

- A partner who does not manage the card but helps repay it.
- A supplementary cardholder.
- A parent or relative whose spending or repayment affects the household.
- A financially cautious partner who wants visibility before trusting shared card use.
- A household member who rarely uses credit but needs to understand card obligations.

## Household Situations

Single:

- Uses debit card for salary-account access and occasional credit card for online purchases.
- May optimize for cashback, installment promotions, or fee waivers.

Dating or cohabiting:

- Card spending may remain personal while some purchases become shared.
- Repayment responsibility can be ambiguous when a card funds joint expenses.

Newly married:

- One partner may bring an existing credit card into the household.
- Partners start negotiating visibility, trust, and who pays the bill.

Young family:

- Card use expands for appliances, hospital visits, travel, groceries, childcare, and subscriptions.
- Due dates matter more because cash flow is tighter.

Family with children:

- Card spending may include school, health, transport, family travel, emergency purchases, and online subscriptions.
- Future supplementary-card or dependent-card questions may appear.

## Financial Maturity Levels

Low maturity:

- Understands "card can pay" but may not distinguish debit from credit.
- May interpret credit limit as available money.
- May not know statement date, due date, or minimum payment meaning.

Medium maturity:

- Understands credit-card repayment and due dates.
- May pay in full but still struggle with refunds, installments, and statement timing.
- Uses bank apps and notifications as practical truth.

High maturity:

- Understands billing cycles, utilization, rewards, annual fees, and repayment timing.
- May compare cards by cost, benefits, and household cash-flow effect.
- More likely to reconcile card statements.

## Life Stages

- First salary and first debit card.
- First credit-card approval.
- First large online purchase.
- First shared household purchase.
- Marriage and shared repayment.
- First child and larger recurring expenses.
- Home setup, vehicle purchase, travel, and emergency spending.
- Long-term household planning with multiple accounts and cards.
