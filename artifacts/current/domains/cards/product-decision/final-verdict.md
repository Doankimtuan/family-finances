# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Product health: Strong, with scope discipline required.

The Cards domain is valuable for ViNha because it protects households from a common misunderstanding: card spending is convenient, but credit-card obligations are not cash and can create future repayment pressure. The approved scope is financially meaningful, household-first, and maintainable if provider-heavy automation remains deferred.

## Decision Summary

- Approve the credit-card obligation core.
- Modify card-type, network, cardholder, status, available-credit, fee, interest, cashback, installment, repayment-pattern, and Health-readonly capabilities to stay simple and safe.
- Defer provider feeds, reconciliation, disputes, pending states, subscriptions, tokenization, multi-currency analysis, reward optimization, household card-use policies, and country packs.
- Reject anything that treats credit as real cash, turns revolving card balances into Loans by default, lets Health mutate Cards, or executes automatic repayments.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.83

Reason:

Phase 1 and Phase 2 strongly validate the household need for card obligation clarity, due-date awareness, refund understanding, repayment source visibility, and partner trust. Confidence remains below 0.90 because Vietnamese terminology, manual tracking tolerance, supplementary-card usage, issuer-specific rules, and provider statement reliability still require direct research.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with constraints.

The Cards domain is ready for Business Blueprint work if the blueprint stays within the approved and modified scope, explicitly protects BR-01 and BR-24, avoids automatic repayment execution, and treats deferred provider-heavy or advanced analytical capabilities as out of scope until later validation.
