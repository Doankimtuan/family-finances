# Decision Points

## Track Card

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- A card affects shared spending, repayment, or trust.

Possible outcomes:

- Track as Active.
- Keep as Draft.
- Do not track.

Business impact:

- Determines whether Cards owns ongoing business interpretation.

## Classify Card Type

Who:

- Household user, based on issuer terms.

Why:

- Debit, credit, and prepaid cards have different money behavior.

Possible outcomes:

- Debit.
- Credit.
- Prepaid.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Determines whether card activity is account-funded spending or credit obligation.

## Confirm Credit Limit

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Credit limit defines maximum borrowing capacity, not cash.

Possible outcomes:

- Limit accepted.
- Limit corrected.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Affects available credit and risk interpretation.

## Confirm Statement And Due Dates

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- These dates determine repayment timing.

Possible outcomes:

- Dates accepted.
- Dates corrected.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Affects billing-period association, Planning, and Inbox attention.

## Interpret Purchase

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- A card charge must be understood as household spending, personal spending, fee, interest, refund, or correction.

Possible outcomes:

- Accepted.
- Categorized.
- Marked shared or personal by household meaning.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Affects obligation clarity and partner trust.

## Pay Statement

Who:

- Household user or responsible partner.

Why:

- Repayment reduces card obligation and real money in an account.

Possible outcomes:

- Full payment.
- Partial payment.
- No payment yet.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Changes paid amount, remaining due, and cash-flow pressure.

## Interpret Refund Or Credit

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Refunds and credits change obligation but can affect different billing periods.

Possible outcomes:

- Apply to current obligation.
- Apply to later obligation.
- Treat as card credit context.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Affects remaining due and trust in statement truth.

## Interpret Fee Or Interest

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Fees and interest are card-specific costs that affect debt pressure.

Possible outcomes:

- Accept known cost.
- Correct meaning.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Increases obligation and may affect Health interpretation.

## Recognize Card-Origin Installment

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- A card purchase may create future scheduled pressure.

Possible outcomes:

- Keep as card-origin installment awareness.
- Needs Review for boundary clarification.

Business impact:

- Prevents duplicate card and loan obligation.

## Close Or Archive Card

Who:

- Household user.

Why:

- Card no longer belongs in current active household view.

Possible outcomes:

- Closed.
- Expired.
- Replaced.
- Archived.
- Needs Review.

Business impact:

- Ends normal card activity while preserving history.
