# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Accounts remains the owner of real container identity, recorded balance context, active/historical distinction, and account recognition metadata.

## Transactions

Transactions depend on account context. Transfer recognition affects transaction interpretation because own-account transfers must not become false income or expense.

## Cards

Cards remain financially distinct from owned-money accounts. Card obligations may be adjacent in the Money surface, but card limits must not contribute to real position.

## Loans

Loans read payment account context. Accounts should not own loan schedules, interest, payoff decisions, or debt lifecycle.

## Planning

Planning reads account reality as grounding only. Planning must not convert accounts into jars or subtract allocations from account balances.

## Goals

Goals remain intentions. Account balances may inform goal confidence, but goals do not own accounts and accounts do not become goal containers.

## Inbox

Inbox may surface account-related decisions only when human attention is required. Informational account noise should not crowd decision work.

## Health

Health summarizes account reality but never mutates Accounts. Health trust depends on account trust.

## Categories

Categories describe purpose of transactions. Accounts describe location of money. These roles remain separate.

## Together

Together protects access and household membership. Account holder/relevance metadata must not become granular surveillance or complex permission design without future research.
