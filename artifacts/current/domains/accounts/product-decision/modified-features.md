# Approved With Modifications

| ID | Capability | Original idea | Required modification | Reason | Expected result |
|----|------------|---------------|-----------------------|--------|-----------------|
| ACC-PD-002 | Account type | Identify account type. | Keep broad, Vietnam-understandable labels; avoid fine taxonomy. | Simple before powerful; terminology still needs validation. | Users recognize account behavior without classification burden. |
| ACC-PD-006 | Real position contribution | All accounts contribute to total. | Only owned-money, eligible accounts contribute to real position; credit obligations are excluded from owned-money totals. | Prevents credit-limit-as-money confusion. | Real position remains financially safe. |
| ACC-PD-009 | Reconciliation | Support reconciliation against real records. | Approve lightweight reconciliation responsibility; defer advanced workflow. | Users need trust, but full reconciliation is complex. | Users can validate reality without process overload. |
| ACC-PD-011 | Liquid versus non-liquid distinction | Distinguish liquidity. | Use simple liquidity language only; no detailed scoring or product taxonomy. | Emergency usability matters, but complexity can confuse. | Households know what is usable now. |
| ACC-PD-013 | Institution/provider metadata | Add institution metadata. | Use as recognition metadata, not mandatory financial onboarding burden. | Users remember bank/wallet names; too much setup hurts adoption. | Better account recognition without heavy setup. |
| ACC-PD-016 | Holder/ownership metadata | Track holder or ownership. | Focus on household relevance; do not introduce granular permissions or partner surveillance. | Personal/shared visibility is sensitive and under-researched. | Supports real household context while protecting Together boundaries. |
| ACC-PD-017 | Manual balance adjustment record | Allow manual balance correction. | Adjustments must be accountable and not silent balance overwrites. | Silent changes damage trust. | Repaired balances remain explainable. |
| ACC-PD-020 | Balance freshness/staleness | Show stale manual balances. | Keep informational and calm; avoid noisy warning behavior. | Stale balances matter, but anxiety harms use. | Users understand confidence level. |
| ACC-PD-022 | Transfer recognition | Recognize own-account transfers. | Must remain Real Ledger only; no jar, goal, or planning impact. | Prevents false income/expense and protects BR-01. | Transfers do not distort household position. |
| ACC-PD-035 | Closed-account lifecycle metadata | Capture closure metadata. | Keep lightweight and historical; avoid complex account state machines. | Closure matters, but current users need clarity more than process. | Closed accounts remain understandable. |
