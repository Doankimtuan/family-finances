# Final Verdict

## Does This Domain Reflect Real Household Financial Behavior?

Yes.

The Accounts domain accurately reflects how young Vietnamese households reason about money at a practical level. Even when users do not use formal financial vocabulary, they understand money through containers: bank account, cash, wallet, savings, card, and partner-held money.

## Strengths

- Solves a real and recurring problem: scattered money.
- Uses a natural mental model: where money is.
- Supports shared finance between partners.
- Provides the trust foundation for Transactions, Planning, Health, Inbox, and Together.
- Stays relevant as households mature.
- Strongly protects the real versus virtual distinction.

## Weaknesses

- Terminology may need Vietnam-first validation.
- Credit cards are behaviorally adjacent to accounts but financially different.
- Savings accounts, savings products, and savings goals can be confused.
- Manual maintenance may decay over time.
- Cash and e-wallet balances may be too tedious if treated with equal weight.
- Personal versus shared account visibility is emotionally sensitive.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The domain is strongly validated by ordinary household behavior and long-term financial reality. Confidence is reduced by unresolved terminology, credit-card placement, savings-product confusion, e-wallet tracking tolerance, and personal/shared visibility questions.

## Validation Completeness

Completeness: Medium-high.

Validated from:

- Phase 1 discovery.
- Existing domain philosophy.
- Current product artifacts.
- Realistic Vietnam-first household behavior patterns.

Still incomplete:

- Direct user interviews.
- Longitudinal tracking evidence.
- Vietnamese-language terminology testing.
- Observed account-maintenance behavior over time.

## Readiness for Product Decision Phase

Readiness: Conditionally ready.

The core domain is ready for Product Decision discussion. Before final product decisions, the team should validate terminology, credit-card mental model, savings distinction, e-wallet tracking value, and personal/shared account visibility with target households.
