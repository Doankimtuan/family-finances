# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

The Accounts business exists because a household cannot manage money it cannot locate.

Before the household can plan, spend, save, reconcile, or discuss money together, it must know which real containers exist and which ones are relevant to household reality.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility is to maintain an explainable set of household financial containers and their recorded position.

Accounts must answer:

- What container exists?
- What is it called in household language?
- What broad type is it?
- Is it currently active or historical?
- Does it count toward real household position?
- What recorded balance is associated with it?
- Can transactions reference it?
- Does its record need review or reconciliation?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is custody before intention.

Money must have a real location before it can be assigned a purpose. Accounts describe location. Planning describes purpose. These must not be merged.

## Business Outcome

The successful business outcome is that any household partner can understand where the household's real money is, which accounts matter now, and which records explain past money movement.
