# Cross-Domain Interactions

## Accounts

Producer:

- Account identity, type, status, recorded position, real-position eligibility, recognition metadata.

Consumer:

- Transaction movement effects, household membership context.

Ownership:

- Owns container truth only.

## Transactions

Producer:

- Money movement records that explain account position changes.

Consumer:

- Account context.

Ownership:

- Owns income, expense, transfer, correction, refund, and transaction event meaning.

Responsibilities:

- Must not treat planning allocation as account movement.

## Cards

Producer:

- Card obligation, statement, due-date, limit, repayment context.

Consumer:

- Repayment account context.

Ownership:

- Owns credit-card lifecycle and credit-specific meaning.

Responsibilities:

- Card limit must not become owned-money account balance.

## Loans

Producer:

- Loan obligation, payment schedule, payoff meaning.

Consumer:

- Payment account context.

Ownership:

- Owns debt lifecycle, not account containers.

## Savings

Producer:

- Savings product lifecycle, maturity, renewal, withdrawal, settlement decisions.

Consumer:

- Funding and settlement account context.

Ownership:

- Owns term/product behavior.

Responsibilities:

- Must not turn savings goal into account balance.

## Planning

Producer:

- Intentions, planned allocations, recurring expectations.

Consumer:

- Account reality as grounding.

Ownership:

- Owns planned money purpose.

Responsibilities:

- Must not subtract planned allocations from account balance.

## Goals

Producer:

- Future target and progress intention.

Consumer:

- Account reality may inform confidence.

Ownership:

- Owns aspiration and target, not containers.

## Inbox

Producer:

- Human decision prompts.

Consumer:

- Account context when a decision needs it.

Ownership:

- Owns decision queue, not account truth.

Responsibilities:

- Should not become a notification dump for routine account information.

## Health

Producer:

- Read-only interpretation.

Consumer:

- Account position and account count.

Ownership:

- Owns health summary only.

Responsibilities:

- Must never mutate Accounts.

## Categories

Producer:

- Purpose labels for transactions.

Consumer:

- No account ownership.

Ownership:

- Owns spending/income classification language.

Responsibilities:

- Must not become account grouping.

## Together

Producer:

- Household membership, access, partner context.

Consumer:

- Account relevance may affect household trust.

Ownership:

- Owns access and household participation.

Responsibilities:

- Must not turn account holder metadata into granular permission design in current scope.
