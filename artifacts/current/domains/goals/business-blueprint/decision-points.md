# Decision Points

## Create Goal

Who makes it:

- Household member within valid household context.

Why:

- A future purpose is important enough to track.

Possible outcomes:

- Goal becomes Active.
- Action is invalid and no goal is created.

Business impact:

- Creates a new household intention without moving money.

## Set Or Change Target Amount

Who makes it:

- Household member within valid household context.

Why:

- Household estimates or revises needed amount.

Possible outcomes:

- Target changes.
- Prior target remains if invalid.

Business impact:

- Changes progress interpretation but not real money.

## Set Or Change Target Date

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Timing pressure appears, changes, or disappears.

Possible outcomes:

- Date changes.
- Goal remains without changed timing.

Business impact:

- Changes urgency interpretation only.

## Add Contribution

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Household wants to record progress.

Possible outcomes:

- Progress increases.
- Goal may become Completed if progress reaches target.
- Action fails if invalid.

Business impact:

- Updates intention progress; does not move money.

## Pause

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Goal is temporarily not being pursued.

Possible outcomes:

- Active -> Paused.
- Prior state remains if already terminal.

Business impact:

- Removes active pursuit without erasing meaning.

## Resume

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Household decides to pursue the goal again.

Possible outcomes:

- Paused -> Active.
- Terminal goal remains unchanged.

Business impact:

- Restores active pursuit without implying funding.

## Complete

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Household considers intention fulfilled.

Possible outcomes:

- Active/Paused -> Completed.
- Action fails if used as false proof of real money movement.

Business impact:

- Ends ordinary pursuit; does not prove purchase or payment.

## Cancel

Who makes it:

- Household member.

Why:

- Goal no longer matters or is replaced by another priority.

Possible outcomes:

- Active/Paused -> Cancelled.
- Prior terminal state remains if already terminal.

Business impact:

- Ends ordinary pursuit without erasing history.

## Interpret Evidence

Who makes it:

- Household, informed by source-domain facts where available.

Why:

- Household wants to know whether progress is believable.

Possible outcomes:

- Evidence supports progress.
- Evidence conflicts with progress.
- Evidence is insufficient.

Business impact:

- Clarifies confidence qualitatively; source truth remains elsewhere.

## Resolve Conflict Or Emergency

Who makes it:

- Household member or household together.

Why:

- Goal meaning is disrupted by emergency, disagreement, or changed reality.

Possible outcomes:

- Goal remains Active.
- Goal becomes Paused.
- Goal becomes Cancelled.
- Prior valid state remains.

Business impact:

- Preserves household trust and BR-01 boundaries.
