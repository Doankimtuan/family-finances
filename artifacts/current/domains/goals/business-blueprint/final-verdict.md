# Final Verdict

## Business Completeness

Completeness: High for approved and modified Phase 3 scope.

The blueprint defines the full business behavior for the simple Goals core: purpose, target, timing, progress, contribution, lifecycle, visibility, evidence context, completion, cancellation, and recovery.

Deferred Phase 3 capabilities are intentionally excluded from the business contract.

## Business Consistency

Consistency: High.

The blueprint preserves:

- Goals as intention.
- Real Ledger != Virtual Planning.
- Source-domain ownership.
- Health read-only.
- No unnecessary automation.
- Simple before powerful.

## Risk Summary

Key remaining risks:

- Users may still misunderstand progress as money.
- Contributions may be read as transfers.
- Completion may be read as purchase/payment completion.
- Partner visibility may create social pressure.
- Evidence may be incomplete across cash, wallets, accounts, and savings products.

These risks are controlled through explicit business rules and boundary language.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.85

Reason:

The business contract is strongly supported by discovery, validation, and product decisions. Confidence is reduced by terminology risk, manual-progress decay, and real-world evidence gaps.

## Readiness For Implementation Contract

Readiness: Ready with guardrails.

Implementation Contract work may proceed for this business scope only if it preserves:

- Goal progress as intention.
- Contribution as progress update unless Transactions owns real movement.
- Completion as intention state only.
- Savings, Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Inbox, Planning, Together, and Health ownership boundaries.
- No forecasting, provider matching, recurring contributions, confidence scoring, goal priority, AI explanations, or advanced life-event modeling in current scope.
