# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Goals exists because households need a durable way to protect future priorities from daily spending.

Young households often save for concrete outcomes: emergency fund, wedding, baby fund, travel, school, motorbike, home setup, family support, health buffer, or major purchase. These intentions need a shared place in the household operating system without pretending that money has moved.

## Core Responsibility

Goals owns:

- The goal's named purpose.
- The estimated target amount.
- Optional target timing.
- Perceived progress.
- Contribution records as progress updates.
- Lifecycle state.
- Simple household context.

Goals does not own real balances, transfers, payments, withdrawals, maturities, debts, card obligations, or financial advice.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is purpose-based intention with strict separation from real money truth.

A goal can say "we intend that VND X is for this purpose." It cannot say "this money exists in this account" unless Accounts, Transactions, Savings, or another source domain provides that fact read-only.

## Business Outcome

The business outcome is household clarity:

- Partners understand what they are preparing for.
- Progress is motivating but not misleading.
- Goal changes are explainable.
- Completion and cancellation are business states of intention, not proof of money movement.
- ViNha remains financially safe and maintainable over 5-10 years.
