# Cross-Domain Impact

This document describes interactions only. It does not redesign boundaries.

## Accounts

Accounts remain the source of real balance truth. Goals may reference account evidence only through read-only context.

## Transactions

Transactions remain the source of real money movement. Goal contributions are not transactions unless a transaction exists in the Transactions domain.

## Cards

Card repayment pressure may affect household capacity for goals. Cards own statement, debt, installment, and payment truth.

## Loans

Loans may compete with goals for future household capacity. Loan payoff truth remains in Loans; goal-like payoff intention remains planning context only.

## Planning

Planning owns the broader intention layer. Goals are a specific kind of future intention that Planning may use for household prioritization and review.

## Goals

Goals own name, purpose, target, progress interpretation, lifecycle state, and simple context. Goals do not own real money.

## Inbox

Inbox may surface goal-related review needs in future phases. Inbox owns decision queue behavior, not goal meaning.

## Health

Health may read goal data to form read-only insight. Health must not mutate goals, create contributions, or decide priorities.

## Categories

Categories may explain spending patterns that affect goal progress. Categories do not own goal purpose or lifecycle.

## Together

Together owns household membership and partner access. Goal visibility interacts with trust, privacy, and shared responsibility.

## Savings

Savings may hold real money intended for a goal. Savings owns product truth. Goals may carry purpose context only.
