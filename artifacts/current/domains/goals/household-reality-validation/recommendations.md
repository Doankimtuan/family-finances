# Recommendations

This document contains classified observations only. It does not redesign the business, create Product Decisions, or define implementation.

## Validated

- Goals as named future intentions match real young-household behavior.
- Young households naturally save for concrete outcomes.
- Goal progress is emotionally motivating.
- Shared goals can improve partner clarity.
- Goal lifecycle states such as active, paused, completed, and cancelled reflect real life.
- Emergency fund, travel, family support, home setup, wedding, baby, school, and major-purchase goals are realistic examples.
- The separation between goal intention and real money truth is essential.
- The domain remains relevant over 5-10 years.

## Needs Review

- Whether current goal language is simple enough for low-maturity users.
- Whether "funded amount" risks implying a real balance.
- Whether contribution language implies a bank transfer.
- Whether completion should be interpreted carefully in future design phases.
- Whether goal and jar concepts are distinct enough in household language.
- Whether goal and savings product language is distinct enough.
- Whether partner visibility creates trust, pressure, or blame.

## Needs Research

- Vietnam-first terminology testing.
- Household interviews about recent goals and failed goals.
- Manual tracking tolerance over several months.
- Partner dynamics around contribution fairness.
- Evidence behavior across cash, bank, wallet, term deposits, and family gifts.
- Common frequency of goal interruptions.
- How users understand completed versus used versus cancelled.
- Whether goal purpose types are naturally used or unnecessary complexity.

## Future Opportunity

- Deeper understanding of long-term family goals.
- Stronger evidence interpretation as bank and wallet connectivity matures.
- More nuanced life-stage goals for children, housing, health, education, and elder support.
- Better understanding of goal trade-offs with debt, cards, and recurring obligations.
- Household memory of completed, cancelled, and changed goals.

These are observations only, not roadmap priorities.
