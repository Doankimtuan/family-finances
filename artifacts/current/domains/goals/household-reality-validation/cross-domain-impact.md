# Cross-Domain Impact

This document describes natural interactions only. It does not redesign boundaries.

## Accounts

Accounts hold real balances that may support or contradict goal progress. Goals may need account context, but accounts remain the truth for where money is.

## Transactions

Transactions record real income, expense, transfer, refund, and correction facts. A transaction may explain how goal money was funded or used, but Goals do not own transaction truth.

## Planning

Planning uses goals as future intentions that compete with bills, jars, recurring obligations, and monthly capacity. Goals are one expression of household intention inside broader planning behavior.

## Jars

Jars represent allocation envelopes. Goals represent future outcomes. A jar and a goal may share a similar purpose, but the household needs their meanings to remain distinct.

## Savings

Savings products may physically hold money intended for a goal. Savings owns product truth such as balance, interest, maturity, withdrawal rules, and renewal.

## Cards

Card repayment pressure can reduce goal funding capacity. Card installment or statement obligations may compete with goal progress.

## Loans

Loans create contractual repayment pressure. A loan payoff intention may feel goal-like, but loan truth belongs to Loans.

## Categories

Categories may help users understand spending patterns that affect goal funding capacity. Categories do not own goal purpose.

## Inbox

Inbox may surface review or decision pressure related to goals if another domain creates a need for confirmation. Inbox owns the decision queue, not goal meaning.

## Health

Health may read goals to interpret financial confidence, preparation, or stress. Health remains read-only and should not mutate goal state.

## Together

Together owns household membership, partner access, and shared context. Goals may become trust-building or conflict-producing depending on visibility and partner expectations.

## Third-Party Providers

Providers may supply read-only evidence from bank, wallet, savings, or investment systems. Provider truth remains external.
