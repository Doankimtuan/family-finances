# Goals Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Goals domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared future financial intentions in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand named future money targets without confusing goal progress with real account, cash, wallet, or savings product balances?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Shared finance between partners.
- Single people preparing for shared household finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and families with children.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real goal-setting behavior.
- Shared partner decision patterns.
- Manual progress tracking tolerance.
- Emotional meaning and trust.
- Confusion risk between Goals, Jars, Savings, Accounts, and Transactions.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Goals as named household future intentions, not money containers. It identified the core distinction between goal progress and real money truth.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against young-household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
