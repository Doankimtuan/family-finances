# Target Users

## Primary Users

Young Vietnamese households where money decisions are shared, negotiated, or at least visible between partners.

Primary users include:

- Couples living together.
- Newly married partners.
- Young families with one or more children.
- Partners who share some accounts but still keep some personal spending.
- Households where one person tracks finance and the other provides context.

These users need Inbox most when financial facts arrive faster than the household can interpret them.

## Secondary Users

Secondary users include:

- Single people preparing for future shared finance.
- Engaged couples aligning spending habits.
- Parents or relatives who occasionally send money, reimburse expenses, or create obligations.
- Household members who do not actively manage money but need visibility.

## Household Situations

| Situation | Inbox relevance |
| --- | --- |
| Single | Helps build review habits before shared finance. |
| Dating / cohabiting | Helps clarify shared expenses without forcing full financial merging. |
| Newly married | Helps partners discover each other's spending and decision patterns. |
| Young family | Helps keep bills, child expenses, savings, and reimbursements from slipping. |
| Family with children | Helps manage higher transaction volume and more recurring obligations. |
| One income household | Helps the non-earning or lower-earning partner stay informed. |
| Dual income household | Helps coordinate spending from multiple accounts and wallets. |
| Informal family support | Helps track unclear transfers, reimbursements, and parent/sibling support. |

## Financial Maturity Levels

| Level | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Low maturity | Reacts to bank balance, rarely categorizes, avoids review when busy. |
| Developing maturity | Reviews after salary or month end, accepts some manual work. |
| Shared maturity | Partners discuss unclear items and review decisions together. |
| Advanced maturity | Maintains categories, goals, savings decisions, and historical accountability. |

## Life Stages

| Life stage | Inbox validation |
| --- | --- |
| First job | Useful for building awareness, but review discipline may be weak. |
| Stable relationship | Useful when personal and shared spending start to mix. |
| Marriage | Strong fit because decisions become jointly consequential. |
| Pregnancy / child | Strong fit because bills, savings, and unexpected expenses increase. |
| Home or car planning | Strong fit because missed decisions can distort cash planning. |
| Growing income | Strong fit if transaction volume and account count increase. |
