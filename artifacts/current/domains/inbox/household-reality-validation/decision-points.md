# Decision Points

## Review

Users decide whether to inspect an item now.

Why it matters:

- Review is the gateway from passive awareness to household accountability.
- Ignored items reduce trust in reports and plans.

## Resolve

Users decide the item has enough context to complete the required financial decision.

Why it matters:

- Resolution turns unresolved attention into a recorded household outcome.
- It affects downstream trust even when no real money moves.

## Categorize Or Map

Users decide what household meaning a transaction should have.

Why it matters:

- Categorization affects spending interpretation, jar tracking, and future recall.
- Wrong mapping can make plans look healthier or worse than reality.

## Acknowledge

Users decide that an item has been seen and understood without requiring deeper Inbox action.

Why it matters:

- Some items require awareness rather than a category or allocation.
- Acknowledgement can create false confidence if users treat it as financial completion.

## Dismiss

Users decide an item should leave active attention.

Why it matters:

- Dismissal prevents noise from overwhelming the queue.
- Incorrect dismissal can hide real obligations or unresolved data quality issues.

## Defer

Users decide not to act yet.

Why it matters:

- Deferral reflects real life when context or partner input is missing.
- Repeated deferral can turn attention into backlog.

## Approve

Users decide a suggested or partner-created outcome is acceptable.

Why it matters:

- Approval creates shared confidence.
- It can reduce conflict if the household recognizes the decision as jointly acceptable.

## Reject Or Challenge

Users decide not to accept an item, suggested meaning, or partner decision.

Why it matters:

- Shared finance includes disagreement.
- Challenge behavior reveals trust, privacy, and control boundaries.

## Renew

Users decide whether a maturity-related financial product should continue.

Why it matters:

- Renewal affects liquidity, interest, and future cash availability.
- It is a real financial decision owned outside Inbox.

## Withdraw

Users decide whether matured or saved money should return to spendable liquidity.

Why it matters:

- Withdrawal affects household cash position and goal discipline.
- It can be driven by emergency, opportunity, or planned need.

## Pause Or Wait

Users decide that a decision or related plan cannot continue normally right now.

Why it matters:

- Real households pause decisions during stress, low income, uncertainty, or emergencies.
- Waiting changes emotional pressure and report confidence.

## Correct

Users decide a prior decision was wrong or incomplete.

Why it matters:

- Correction preserves trust.
- It acknowledges that household memory and provider data are imperfect.
