# Recommendations

These are validation classifications only. They are not product decisions, roadmap priorities, or implementation recommendations.

## Validated

- Inbox as unresolved household financial attention.
- Shared queue as a real partner-finance need.
- Unclear transactions as a natural source of review.
- Savings maturity and installment completion as valid decision points.
- Backlog and decision fatigue as real risks.
- Need to keep Inbox separate from transaction truth and real money movement.
- Need to distinguish decisions from generic notifications.

## Needs Review

- Whether payment reminders fit Inbox or risk turning it into a notification center.
- Whether current status language is understandable to young households.
- Whether item type language feels financial or technical.
- Whether auto-resolution is emotionally acceptable.
- Whether archive history is meaningful for ordinary users.
- Whether partner assignment or targeted visibility matches real household norms.

## Needs Research

- Vietnam-first terminology testing.
- Review frequency and backlog tolerance among couples age 20-35.
- Manual-work tolerance for transaction review.
- Trust behavior around suggestions and repeated merchants.
- Privacy sensitivity for personal spending inside shared finance.
- Actual usage of receipts, screenshots, and e-invoices in household review.
- Provider-channel mix across bank apps, e-wallets, SMS, email, and chat.

## Future Opportunity

- Exception-focused review as transaction volume grows.
- Evidence-aware review for receipts, invoices, and provider messages.
- Stronger household memory around repeated decisions.
- Better handling of duplicated or conflicting source signals.
- More nuanced shared-finance behavior as families grow.

These are observations only and do not create a roadmap.
