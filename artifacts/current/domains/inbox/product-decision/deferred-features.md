# Deferred Features

## IB-PD-012 Assign Or Target Item To Partner

Reason:

- Real behavior but sensitive. Partner targeting can feel helpful or controlling.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Direct research validates privacy norms and household acceptance.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 1.x research-backed shared-finance refinement.

## IB-PD-014 Group Similar Items

Reason:

- Useful under volume but risks hiding one-decision-per-item clarity.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Backlog evidence shows users need volume processing.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 1.x.

## IB-PD-015 Identify Likely Duplicate Items

Reason:

- Real duplicate risk depends on provider imports and multi-channel evidence.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Provider/import channels become common enough to create duplicate pain.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 1.x or 2.x depending on integration maturity.

## IB-PD-018 Surface Receipt, Invoice, Or Provider Evidence

Reason:

- Evidence is useful but not validated as core for ordinary households.

Conditions to reconsider:

- User research shows receipts, screenshots, or e-invoices are regularly used to resolve items.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-019 Capture Confidence Or Dispute Notes

Reason:

- Real disagreement behavior, but can become comments, blame, or task management.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Partner-conflict research shows a lightweight safe need.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-020 Exception-Only Review

Reason:

- Requires trusted suggestions, imports, and pattern accuracy.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Core Inbox usage proves suggestions are reliable and trusted.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-022 Cross-Provider Matching

Reason:

- High value later, high complexity now.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Bank, wallet, card, invoice, or receipt integrations become reliable.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-023 Provider-Message Classification

Reason:

- Vietnam provider messages vary widely and false positives would create noise.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Provider-channel research identifies stable, high-confidence message patterns.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-024 Household-Specific Decision Learning

Reason:

- Depends on enough history, user trust, and explainability.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Users consistently accept suggestions and understand their basis.

Suggested future phase:

- Version 2.x.

## IB-PD-025 Richer Invoice And Receipt Review

Reason:

- More relevant to disputes, warranties, or household business than daily household review.

Conditions to reconsider:

- Evidence needs are validated for target households.

Suggested future phase:

- Future.

## IB-PD-028 Regulatory Or Tax Evidence Retrieval

Reason:

- Outside ordinary young-household core.

Conditions to reconsider:

- A validated household-business segment emerges.

Suggested future phase:

- Future specialized phase.
