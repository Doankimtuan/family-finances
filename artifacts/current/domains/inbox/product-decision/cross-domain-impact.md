# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Inbox may reference account context so users know where a source event came from. Accounts remain owner of real balances and account identity.

## Transactions

Unclear transactions may create Inbox items. Inbox resolution may provide transaction meaning, but Transactions remains owner of transaction truth.

## Cards

Card reminders, refunds, installments, and statement-related attention may appear only when decision-bearing. Cards remains owner of credit, billing, and repayment truth.

## Loans

Loan or installment completion may create review attention. Loans remains owner of contract, due amount, status, and repayment truth.

## Planning

Inbox decisions may affect virtual planning interpretation, such as jar mapping or plan context. Planning remains owner of virtual intention.

## Goals

Inbox can surface attention that affects a goal indirectly, such as savings maturity or unexpected spending. Goals remains owner of goal intention and progress interpretation.

## Inbox

Inbox owns queue identity, state, source linkage, and review outcome. It must remain a decision queue.

## Health

Health may read Inbox burden, unresolved age, or review consistency as context. Health remains read-only and cannot mutate Inbox.

## Categories

Inbox may ask users to choose or confirm household meaning. Categories remains owner of category vocabulary and meaning.

## Together

Inbox supports shared attention and accountability. Together or shared-household policy domains remain responsible for partner norms, consent, visibility, and relationship-sensitive rules.
