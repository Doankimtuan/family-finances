# Approved Features

## IB-PD-001 Collect Pending Financial Decisions

Why approved:

- Core validated household problem.
- Matches Inbox as unresolved financial attention.

Expected product value:

- Gives families a shared place for financial open loops.

Dependencies:

- Source domains able to create reviewable attention.
- Household membership and authorization.

Scope:

- Pending financial decisions only.

## IB-PD-002 Link Item To Source Event Or Evidence

Why approved:

- Prevents Inbox items from becoming generic tasks.

Expected product value:

- Users can understand why the item exists and where to verify it.

Dependencies:

- Source-domain identifiers or evidence references.

Scope:

- Linkage only; source truth remains outside Inbox.

## IB-PD-003 Present Why Attention Is Needed

Why approved:

- Users need plain reason before taking action.

Expected product value:

- Reduces confusion and improves trust.

Dependencies:

- Item kind and source context.

Scope:

- Explain attention, not prescribe financial advice.

## IB-PD-004 Preserve Current State

Why approved:

- Open, completed, dismissed, and expired items have different household meaning.

Expected product value:

- Prevents "seen" from being mistaken for "done."

Dependencies:

- Consistent state vocabulary.

Scope:

- State of Inbox attention only.

## IB-PD-005 Support Household Resolution

Why approved:

- Resolution is the core behavior validated in Phase 2.

Expected product value:

- Turns unresolved attention into a household decision.

Dependencies:

- Owning domains that can receive the outcome.

Scope:

- Inbox facilitates decision completion; it does not own money movement.

## IB-PD-008 Distinguish Active From Historical Items

Why approved:

- Users need a calm active queue and a way to recover past context.

Expected product value:

- Supports Inbox zero without losing history.

Dependencies:

- Current state and archival classification.

Scope:

- Active versus no-longer-active separation.

## IB-PD-010 Separate Real Money, Virtual Planning, Read-Only Info

Why approved:

- Required by BR-01 and financial safety.

Expected product value:

- Users understand that Inbox decisions are not automatically bank movement.

Dependencies:

- Cross-domain boundaries.

Scope:

- Inbox can carry attention across flow types, but ownership remains elsewhere.
