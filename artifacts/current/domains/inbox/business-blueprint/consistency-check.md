# Consistency Check

## No Contradictory Rules

Status: Passed.

Resolution, acknowledgement, dismissal, expiration, deferral, archive, and auto-resolution have distinct meanings.

## No Duplicated Responsibilities

Status: Passed.

Inbox owns attention state and outcome only. Source domains own financial truth.

## No Circular Ownership

Status: Passed.

Owning domains may produce attention and consume outcomes. Inbox does not become owner of the source fact.

## No Orphan Flows

Status: Passed.

Every flow begins from source attention, household action, time-bound rule, or recovery reason and ends in a defined state.

## No Missing Lifecycle Stages

Status: Passed.

Beginning, normal operation, changes, completion, termination, recovery, and exceptional situations are documented.

## No BR Violations

Status: Passed.

- BR-01 is preserved: Inbox does not merge Real Ledger and Virtual Planning.
- BR-24 is preserved: Health may read but cannot mutate Inbox.
- No unnecessary automation: auto-resolution is constrained and cannot move money.
- User understanding is protected: source, reason, and outcome must be explainable.

## Deferred And Rejected Scope Check

Status: Passed.

Deferred capabilities are excluded from active business flows except as boundary notes. Rejected capabilities are explicitly forbidden.
