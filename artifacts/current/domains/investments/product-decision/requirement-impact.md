# Requirement Impact

This document identifies requirement impacts only. It does not create or modify official requirements.

## New Requirements

| Proposed requirement impact | Affected capabilities |
|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| Future Investments scope needs a requirement for risk-bearing holding identity. | INV-PD-001, INV-PD-003, INV-PD-004 |
| Future Investments scope needs a requirement for contribution amount and realized outcome clarity. | INV-PD-006, INV-PD-012, INV-PD-014 |
| Future Investments scope needs a requirement for estimated value to include valuation date and source. | INV-PD-008, INV-PD-009, INV-PD-010, INV-PD-022 |
| Future Investments scope needs a requirement for simple liquidity/risk context without advice. | INV-PD-015, INV-PD-027, INV-PD-028 |
| Future Investments scope needs a requirement for ownership/visibility sensitivity. | INV-PD-016, INV-PD-039 |
| Future Health requirements may need read-only consumption language for investment exposure. | INV-PD-041 |

## Modified Requirements

| Existing area | Proposed impact | Affected capabilities |
|---------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Money / Accounts | Clarify that brokerage-like containers do not equal full investment holding support. | INV-PD-002, INV-PD-018 |
| Planning / Goals | Clarify that investment purpose does not automatically create goal progress or jar capacity. | INV-PD-030, INV-PD-038, INV-PD-044 |
| Health | Clarify read-only interpretation of investments if/when Investments enters product scope. | INV-PD-041, INV-PD-045 |

## Removed Requirements

No removed requirements identified.
