# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Investments is product-valid but not current-scope ready.

The domain reflects real household financial behavior and is important for long-term money management, but Phase 2 validation shows that terminology, adoption, asset-type priority, partner visibility, and manual tracking tolerance are not sufficiently proven for immediate product expansion.

## Decision Summary

- A simple future Investments foundation is approved around separation, holdings, contribution, valuation date, realized outcome, cash movement distinction, and history.
- Many valid capabilities are approved with modifications to protect users from cash confusion, advice risk, privacy harm, and over-precision.
- Integrations, analytics, tax, corporate actions, multi-currency, and net-worth depth are deferred.
- Advice, automation, gamification, Health write-back, unrealized-value plan funding, and crypto trading depth are rejected.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.74

Reason:

The board is confident in the financial boundaries and rejected-risk decisions. Confidence is lower for positive scope decisions because Phase 2 found unresolved user-language, adoption, partner-trust, and manual-maintenance questions.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Not ready for full Business Blueprint.

Limited readiness:

- A future Business Blueprint may proceed only for the approved and modified foundation after required validation is completed.

Blocking validation before full blueprint:

- Vietnam-first terminology.
- Asset-type priority among stocks, funds, gold, crypto, insurance-linked products, and family investments.
- Partner visibility and privacy expectations.
- Manual tracking tolerance.
- Local provider data and statement availability.

Final board position:

Investments should remain a clearly defined future domain, not an MVP or Version 1.x expansion.
