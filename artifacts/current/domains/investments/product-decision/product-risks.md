# Product Risks

## Financial Risk

- Users may treat estimated market value as cash.
- Users may ignore liquidity delays, fees, spreads, taxes, or settlement.
- Margin/leverage visibility may accidentally normalize risky behavior if not carefully constrained.
- Private/family assets may be recorded with false confidence.

## UX Complexity Risk

- Investment terminology can overwhelm young households.
- Users may not understand realized versus unrealized gain/loss.
- Too much valuation detail can create anxiety or trading-style checking behavior.
- Gold, insurance-linked products, real estate, and informal investments blur simple categories.

## Maintenance Risk

- Asset classes evolve faster than core household ledger concepts.
- Provider integrations require ongoing reliability, consent, and data-shape maintenance.
- Corporate actions, taxes, and multi-currency reporting expand scope quickly.

## Scalability Risk

- A simple holding model may later face pressure from active traders and complex portfolios.
- Manual valuation may decay as the number of holdings grows.
- Future international expansion may require different tax, account, and retirement concepts.

## Education Risk

- Users may need plain-language investment concepts before numbers make sense.
- Mislabeling funds, bonds, gold, and savings products can damage trust.
- Partner-facing language must avoid blame during loss events.

## User Misunderstanding Risk

- "Invested amount" may mean contribution, current value, or target.
- "Up/down" may reflect new contributions rather than performance.
- "Safe" may be used incorrectly for bonds, funds, insurance, or gold.
- "Long-term" may be confused with "untouchable."
