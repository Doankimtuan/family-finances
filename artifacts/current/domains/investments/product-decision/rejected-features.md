# Rejected Features

Rejected capabilities are not suitable for ViNha under the current product philosophy.

## INV-PD-042 Investment Recommendations

Why rejected:

- Recommending buy, sell, hold, rebalance, or switch decisions creates advice-like risk.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Financial safety is more important than convenience.
- Household-first clarity is not investment advisory.

Complexity:

- High regulatory, financial, and trust complexity.

Low value:

- Not necessary for household money understanding.

Financial risk:

- Could lead users to make harmful investment decisions.

Maintenance cost:

- High due to changing markets, products, and regulation.

## INV-PD-043 Automated Trading Or Rebalancing

Why rejected:

- Automatically buying, selling, rebalancing, or moving investment money violates household control.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- No unnecessary automation.
- User always understands where money is.

Financial risk:

- Critical; automation could create real losses.

## INV-PD-044 Treat Unrealized Value As Spendable Plan Capacity

Why rejected:

- Market value is not cash and should not fund jars or plans by default.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Direct BR-01 violation.

Financial risk:

- Critical; users may overspend based on value they cannot realize.

## INV-PD-045 Health Write-Back To Investment Data

Why rejected:

- Health must remain read-only.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Violates BR-24.

Maintenance cost:

- High and unnecessary.

## INV-PD-046 Gamified Trading Or Market-Timing Prompts

Why rejected:

- Encourages behavior that conflicts with long-term household financial management.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Simple before powerful.
- Financial safety over convenience.

Financial risk:

- Critical behavioral risk from FOMO, overtrading, and stress.

## INV-PD-047 Detailed Tax Optimization

Why rejected:

- Tax optimization exceeds ordinary household scope and enters specialist advice territory.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Too complex and not household-first for target users.

Maintenance cost:

- High due to jurisdiction and rule changes.

## INV-PD-048 Crypto Trading Depth

Why rejected:

- Crypto-specific trading, DeFi, staking, and yield mechanics are high-risk and weakly validated.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Simple before powerful.
- Financial safety over convenience.

Financial risk:

- Critical due to volatility, scams, and product complexity.
