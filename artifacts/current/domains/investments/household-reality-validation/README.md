# Investments Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Investments domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage long-term, risk-bearing financial assets in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a domain that separates investment holdings from cash, savings products, goals, and household plans?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Singles, couples, newly married partners, and young families.
- Shared finance between partners, including partly shared and privately managed investment money.
- Long-term household financial management over 5-10 years.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household behavior.
- Investment habits and mental models.
- Partner trust and emotional reactions.
- Manual tracking tolerance.
- Confusing concepts.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.
- Investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Investments as risk-bearing household assets whose value, liquidity, and return are uncertain until realized. It separated investment holdings from Accounts, Transactions, Savings, Goals, Planning, Health, and Together.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against the reality of young households. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
