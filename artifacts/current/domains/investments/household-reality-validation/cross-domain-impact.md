# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Investments interact with Accounts when cash moves into or out of a bank account, brokerage account, fund platform, or gold purchase.

Accounts answer where cash is. Investments answer what risk-bearing asset is held after cash becomes an investment.

## Transactions

Transactions record real cash movement: contributions, withdrawals, dividends, coupons, fees, taxes, sale proceeds, and transfers.

Investments explain the investment meaning behind those movements.

## Goals

Goals may express why the household invests, such as education, home purchase, retirement, or long-term security.

Goals do not prove that an investment exists or guarantee its value.

## Planning

Planning may include intended contributions or monthly surplus allocation.

Planning does not own investment performance, market value, or custody truth.

## Inbox

Inbox may naturally receive review-worthy uncertainty: stale value, missing statement, unclear dividend, partner question, failed redemption, or major loss.

Inbox does not decide investment strategy.

## Health

Health may interpret investment exposure as part of financial resilience, risk, liquidity, or long-term wealth.

Health should not turn investment information into advice without separate governance.

## Categories

Categories may help label investment-related transactions, such as contribution, fee, dividend, or proceeds.

Categories do not own holding truth or performance.

## Together

Together affects partner visibility, consent expectations, household ownership, and trust.

Investment information can be more sensitive than ordinary account balances because it may reveal risk-taking, loss, or private assets.

## Savings

Savings and Investments are adjacent but behaviorally different.

Savings products are typically understood as capital-preserving, term-based, and interest-bearing. Investments are risk-bearing and valuation can move.

## Loans / Cards

Loans and Cards interact when borrowed money is used for investment or when investment proceeds repay obligations.

This interaction can increase household risk and partner sensitivity.
