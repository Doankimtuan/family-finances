# Final Verdict

## Business Completeness

Completeness: Medium-high for approved and modified Phase 3 scope.

The blueprint deterministically covers investment recognition, activation, valuation, review, correction, partial exit, full exit, cancellation, reclassification, archive, money movement boundaries, state transitions, and exceptional scenarios.

Deferred Phase 3 scope remains intentionally outside this business contract.

## Business Consistency

Consistency: High.

The blueprint preserves ViNha's core distinctions:

- Investments are risk-bearing holdings.
- Accounts are cash containers.
- Transactions are cash movement facts.
- Savings products are not investments.
- Goals and Planning are intention.
- Health is read-only.
- Inbox is attention, not strategy.

## Risk Summary

Primary risks:

- Users may treat estimated investment value as available cash.
- Users may confuse savings products, funds, bonds, gold, and insurance-linked products.
- Partner trust may be harmed by unclear ownership, losses, or hidden exposure.
- Manual values may become stale.
- Private/family investments may be over-trusted despite weak verification.
- Risk context may be mistaken for advice if later phases are not careful.

Risk posture:

- Business rules explicitly reject advice, automation, false precision, and BR-01 leakage.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.73.

Reason:

The business behavior is internally consistent and traceable to Phases 1-3. Confidence remains limited because Phase 3 classified Investments as future scope and required more validation before full product implementation.

## Readiness For Implementation Contract

Readiness: Conditional.

Ready:

- Business boundaries.
- Core states and transitions.
- Approved/modified capability behavior.
- Cross-domain responsibilities.
- Money-flow correctness.

Not ready until validated:

- Vietnam-first terminology.
- Asset-type priority.
- Partner visibility expectations.
- Manual tracking tolerance.
- Provider data and statement availability.

Final board position:

This blueprint is suitable as a future-domain business contract, but not as an immediate implementation contract for MVP or Version 1.x.
