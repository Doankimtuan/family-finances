# Decision Points

## Recognize As Investment

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Determines whether an asset belongs in Investments or another domain.

Possible outcomes:

- Recognized.
- Reclassified.
- Under Review.
- Cancelled.

Business impact:

- Establishes domain ownership or prevents responsibility leakage.

## Confirm Active Ownership

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Determines whether the household currently has investment exposure.

Possible outcomes:

- Active.
- Under Review.
- Cancelled.

Business impact:

- Active exposure becomes visible as investment context.

## Classify Asset

Who makes it:

- User, household, or later correction.

Why:

- Asset type affects risk, liquidity, and terminology.

Possible outcomes:

- Stock, fund, bond, gold, crypto, insurance-linked product, private/family investment, other, or unknown.

Business impact:

- Supports household understanding without deep taxonomy.

## Confirm Contribution

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Contribution anchors trust and gain/loss interpretation.

Possible outcomes:

- Exact contribution.
- Estimated contribution.
- Unknown contribution.
- Disputed contribution.

Business impact:

- Determines cost context reliability.

## Update Value

Who makes it:

- User or household based on provider, statement, receipt, market quote, or family update.

Why:

- Household needs last-known value but must understand uncertainty.

Possible outcomes:

- Dated value.
- Source-known value.
- Manual estimate.
- Unknown value.
- Stale value.

Business impact:

- Changes estimated exposure, not cash.

## Review Liquidity

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Determines whether value can realistically support urgent cash needs.

Possible outcomes:

- Easy.
- Delayed.
- Restricted.
- Uncertain.
- Unknown.

Business impact:

- Clarifies emergency and planning expectations without moving money.

## Review Risk Context

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Identifies volatility, leverage, illiquidity, guarantee uncertainty, or unverifiable value.

Possible outcomes:

- Normal active risk.
- Higher risk.
- Leverage exposure.
- Impaired.
- Under Review.

Business impact:

- Improves safety awareness without advice.

## Resolve Ownership/Visibility

Who makes it:

- User, partner, household, or Together policy context.

Why:

- Investments can be personal, shared, partner-managed, family-held, or unclear.

Possible outcomes:

- Household-visible.
- Personal.
- Partner-managed.
- Family-held.
- Unclear.

Business impact:

- Determines trust context and review sensitivity.

## Decide Exit

Who makes it:

- Household or asset controller.

Why:

- Converts investment exposure into cash, transfer, write-off, or closure.

Possible outcomes:

- Partial exit.
- Full exit.
- Transfer out.
- Write-off.
- No exit.
- Under Review.

Business impact:

- May create realized outcome and real cash movement through Transactions.

## Correct Or Reclassify

Who makes it:

- User or household after new information.

Why:

- Prevents wrong domain ownership or false financial interpretation.

Possible outcomes:

- Corrected.
- Reclassified to another domain.
- Under Review.
- Archived.

Business impact:

- Preserves financial correctness.

## Archive

Who makes it:

- User or household.

Why:

- Active behavior has ended and only history remains.

Possible outcomes:

- Archived.
- Archive blocked due to active exposure or unresolved review.

Business impact:

- Keeps historical memory without active exposure.
