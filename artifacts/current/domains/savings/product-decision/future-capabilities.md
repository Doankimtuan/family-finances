# Future Capabilities

## Deposit Insurance Awareness

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Important when balances grow, especially across institutions and legal owners. Not needed for simple MVP.

## Liquidity Ladder

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Valuable once households have several products with different maturities. Premature for first experience.

## Legal Event Handling

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Pledge, freeze, inheritance, and power-of-attorney states are real but rare. They should not burden ordinary users.

## Multi-Currency Savings

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Vietnam household MVP should remain VND-first. Multi-currency adds FX valuation and eligibility complexity.

## Provider Statement Evidence

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Trust-critical later, especially with imports or provider feeds. Too operationally heavy for MVP.

## Provider Status and Package Availability

Decision: Deferred or v1 depending on data reliability.

Rationale: Useful only if ViNha can maintain trustworthy package data.

## Advanced Interest History

Decision: Deferred.

Rationale: Users care about outcomes, not finance analytics. Add only when expected-versus-actual becomes common.
