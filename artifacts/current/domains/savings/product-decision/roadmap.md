# Roadmap

## MVP

MVP should include:

- Manual savings product setup.
- Provider name.
- Product type basic.
- Principal.
- Funding source.
- Settlement destination.
- Term, start date, maturity date, rate.
- Expected interest estimate.
- Posted interest tracking.
- Current cycle.
- Maturity reminder.
- Matured product Inbox decision.
- Same-package renewal.
- Full withdrawal.
- Early withdrawal preview.
- Penalty/forfeiture warning.
- Decision-required renewal policy.
- Immutable cycle snapshot.
- Audit trail.

## v1

v1 should add:

- Legal depositor.
- Purpose reference without BR-01 violation.
- Partner decision memory.
- Pending funding.
- Failed/reversed funding.
- Provider-confirmed acceptance.
- Provider-confirmed interest override.
- Pending/failed settlement review.
- Principal-only renewal.
- Switch package.
- Mark package unavailable.
- Expected versus actual interest.
- Compare expected/provider principal and interest.
- Correct settlement mismatch.
- Mark duplicate settlements.
- Basic rate/package change warnings.

## Future Releases

Future should consider:

- Joint ownership.
- Foreign currency and eligibility.
- Deposit insurance exposure.
- Provider concentration.
- Liquidity ladder.
- Provider inactive state.
- Provider unavailable warning.
- Pledged/collateral status.
- Inheritance/freeze events.
- Provider statement evidence.
- Tax treatment metadata.

## Never

- Provider marketplace.
- Automatic recurring saving transfers inside Savings.
- Regulated investment-like advice.
- Business accounting behavior inside Savings.
