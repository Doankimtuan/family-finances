# Risk Review

## Highest Risks

Estimated interest becomes posted money.

Mitigation: Approved feature requires explicit expected/posted separation.

Savings becomes a jar or goal.

Mitigation: Purpose reference is approved only with BR-01 guardrails.

ViNha becomes a banking marketplace.

Mitigation: Provider marketplace and rate shopping are rejected.

Silent auto-renewal hides household decisions.

Mitigation: Renewal preference is advisory/pre-fill only.

Early withdrawal preview differs from provider reality.

Mitigation: Provider-confirmed final outcome is v1.

Partner conflict over shared money.

Mitigation: Partner decision memory is v1, not a heavy MVP gate.

Over-complexity for small savings.

Mitigation: Legal, insurance, provider status, and advanced compliance are deferred.

## Residual Risks

- Manual provider data can become stale.
- Families may still use bank apps as final authority.
- Emergency behavior may bypass planned flows.
- Deposit insurance complexity may matter earlier for high-income households.
- Legal owner and household relevance can remain sensitive.
