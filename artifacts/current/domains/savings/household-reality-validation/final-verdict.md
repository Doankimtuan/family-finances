# Final Verdict

Verdict: Savings is reality-valid for ViNha's target households.

## Confidence

High.

Young Vietnamese households genuinely use savings as a central family money behavior. They save from salary, bonuses, family gifts, and irregular income. They save for emergencies, children, housing, transport, and family obligations. They also interrupt savings when life demands it.

## What Is Already Correct

- Savings belongs to Real Ledger, not Plan.
- Maturity decisions belong in Inbox.
- Health should read savings context but never mutate it.
- Early withdrawal must exist.
- Renewal must be reviewed.
- Product snapshots and cycles match real term-deposit behavior.

## What Needs More Reality Before Future Evolution

- Household purpose context.
- Legal owner context.
- Liquidity context.
- Provider confirmation.
- Emergency withdrawal psychology.
- Partner consent and decision memory.
- Deposit insurance exposure.

## Final Assessment

Savings is not just a financial product for this audience. It is the household's psychological floor. It is how young families protect rent, childbirth, parents, down payments, and bad months.

ViNha should model Savings as real money under contract, surrounded by household meaning. The Phase 1 model is directionally correct and should continue, but future work must be careful not to over-financialize a behavior that is often emotional, irregular, and family-driven.
