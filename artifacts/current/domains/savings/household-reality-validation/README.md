# Savings Household Reality Validation

Domain: Savings

Status: Phase 2 household financial reality validation. This pack validates the Phase 1 Savings discovery against the real behavior of young Vietnamese households. It does not redesign product, UI, API, schema, or implementation.

## Purpose

Answer the practical question:

If a young Vietnamese family manages money for the next 5-10 years, how will they actually use Savings?

## Inputs

- Current source: `artifacts/current/domains/savings/` and `artifacts/current/specification/`

## External Behavior Evidence

- General Statistics Office / National Statistics Office of Vietnam household living standards and income reporting: [NSO statistical data](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/statistical-data/) and VietnamPlus coverage of preliminary 2025 VHLSS results.
- Digital adoption context: [DataReportal Digital 2025: Vietnam](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-vietnam) and [DataReportal Digital 2026: Vietnam](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam).
- Financial inclusion context: [World Bank Global Findex 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex) and [Vietnam Global Findex 2021 catalog](https://microdata.worldbank.org/index.php/catalog/5861).
- Payments context: public market research on Vietnam mobile payments and digital payments.
- Vietnam deposit and tax references already captured in Phase 1.
