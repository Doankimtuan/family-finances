# Emotional Journey

## Stress Moments

- Salary arrives but bills consume most of it.
- A parent asks for urgent help.
- A child or spouse needs medical care.
- A mortgage or rent deadline approaches.
- A term deposit is locked but cash is needed.
- A partner discovers hidden savings or hidden withdrawals.
- The household realizes savings are fragmented across too many banks.

Where ViNha should help: clarify available liquid money, show consequences, preserve decision records, and route high-stakes decisions to Inbox/Together.

## Happy Moments

- First emergency fund milestone.
- Bonus converted into protected savings.
- Maturity arrives with interest earned.
- Down payment target becomes realistic.
- A couple agrees on a shared goal.

Where ViNha should help: reflect progress calmly and connect real savings to household confidence without calling jars bank balances.

## Anxiety

Anxiety appears when households do not know whether they can afford both today's obligations and tomorrow's safety. Savings can reduce anxiety, but locked savings can also create it.

Where ViNha should help: separate liquid reserve from locked savings and show maturity timing.

## Confidence

Confidence grows when savings survives ordinary monthly friction. A young family feels capable when they can say: "We can handle one bad month."

Where ViNha should help: make resilience visible.

## Regret

Regret appears after early withdrawal, passive renewal at bad terms, overspending a bonus, or failing to save before an emergency.

Where ViNha should help: explain trade-offs before action and avoid shaming after action.

## Discipline

Discipline is often built by salary-day saving and short-term deposits. But too much discipline becomes fragility when money is inaccessible.

Where ViNha should help: balance commitment and liquidity.

## Where ViNha Should Stay Silent

- Do not tell the household which bank product is best.
- Do not shame early withdrawal.
- Do not judge family support decisions.
- Do not pressure investment when the user needs safety.
- Do not over-notify small products.
