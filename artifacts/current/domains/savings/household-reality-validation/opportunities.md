# Opportunity Analysis

These opportunities strengthen ViNha as a Household Money Operating System without copying bank products or adding unnecessary complexity.

## Maturity Decision as a Household Moment

Opportunity: Keep BR-10 strong. Maturity is not just a notification; it is a family decision point.

Why it matters: Young households often roll over by habit.

## Liquidity-Aware Savings Context

Opportunity: Help the family understand whether money can be locked.

Why it matters: A higher rate is not useful if next month's rent, birth cost, or parent support forces early withdrawal.

## Purpose Link Without Balance Conflation

Opportunity: Allow savings to be associated with a household purpose while preserving BR-01.

Why it matters: Families think in purposes, but the product must remain real money.

## Emergency Withdrawal Support

Opportunity: Treat early withdrawal as a valid emergency path, with penalty clarity and partner awareness.

Why it matters: Real families sometimes need cash more than interest.

## Provider Concentration Awareness

Opportunity: Show concentration and insured exposure as quiet context.

Why it matters: As savings grows, provider risk becomes real.

## Savings Ladder Awareness

Opportunity: Make upcoming maturities understandable.

Why it matters: Households can align savings with school fees, apartment payments, Tet, or childbirth.

## Partner Decision Memory

Opportunity: Preserve who decided, why, and what was chosen.

Why it matters: Reduces future conflict and financial ambiguity.

## Progressive Guidance

Opportunity: Start simple for small savings; reveal more context as household savings grows.

Why it matters: Over-modeling early users creates friction, while under-modeling mature households creates risk.
