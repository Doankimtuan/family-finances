# Recommendations

These are validation observations only. They are not product decisions, roadmap priorities, or implementation plans.

## Validated

- Categories represent a real household behavior: classifying money by purpose.
- Young households naturally understand broad labels such as food, transport, home, health, salary, and family.
- Categories are most useful when attached to transactions.
- Categories should remain separate from real balances.
- Categories should remain separate from virtual planning capacity.
- Shared category language supports partner communication.
- Vietnam-first category meaning must account for bank transfers, VietQR, cash, wallets, e-commerce, and family obligations.

## Needs Review

- The practical user understanding of category-to-jar mapping.
- Whether category creation tied to planning context feels natural or burdensome.
- Whether current system category examples are too broad for young Vietnamese households.
- Whether category meaning remains stable after rename, archive, or life-stage changes.
- Whether one active category per transaction is enough for common household purchases.
- Whether users can distinguish transfer, reimbursement, refund, income, and expense categories.

## Needs Research

- Vietnamese terminology for category, classify, uncategorized, provider category, and household meaning.
- Actual provider descriptor quality across Vietnamese banks, e-wallets, VietQR, and cards.
- Manual categorization tolerance over at least one month of real household use.
- Partner disagreement patterns around sensitive categories.
- Real frequency and financial impact of mixed-purpose receipts.
- How young households categorize family support and Tet-related obligations.

## Future Opportunity

- Merchant and provider data may become more useful for classification over time.
- Household category templates may be relevant for marriage, children, home, vehicle, and family-support life stages.
- Category drift may become a long-term reporting concern.
- Imported or inferred categories may require explicit trust handling as provider integrations mature.
- Receipt-level classification may matter for households with children or high supermarket/e-commerce spend.

## Validation Note

The strongest validated point is the category-as-meaning model. The weakest validated point is long-term household maintenance behavior without real user observation.
