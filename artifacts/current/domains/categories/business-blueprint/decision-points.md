# Decision Points

## Create Category

Who makes it:

- Household member with valid shared finance context.

Why:

- A repeated or important money meaning needs a reusable label.

Possible outcomes:

- Category is created as Active.
- Attempt is rejected as invalid.

Business impact:

- Household vocabulary expands.

## Select Category

Who makes it:

- Household member reviewing or recording transaction meaning.

Why:

- Transaction needs human-understandable purpose.

Possible outcomes:

- Transaction becomes Categorized.
- Transaction remains Uncategorized.
- Assignment is rejected as invalid.

Business impact:

- Transaction history becomes more or less understandable.

## Leave Uncategorized

Who makes it:

- Household member or household by inaction.

Why:

- Meaning is unknown or not worth deciding yet.

Possible outcomes:

- Uncategorized state remains.
- Inbox may carry review work if attention is needed.

Business impact:

- Financial fact remains valid; category interpretation remains incomplete.

## Correct Category

Who makes it:

- Household member who identifies wrong meaning.

Why:

- Existing category does not match household understanding.

Possible outcomes:

- Category assignment changes.
- Prior valid meaning remains.
- Transaction becomes Uncategorized.

Business impact:

- Actuals and filtered history use corrected meaning going forward.

## Rename Category

Who makes it:

- Household member responsible for shared vocabulary.

Why:

- Current wording is unclear, outdated, or mismatched to household language.

Possible outcomes:

- Label wording changes.
- Rename is rejected.

Business impact:

- Current comprehension improves if historical meaning remains clear.

## Archive Category

Who makes it:

- Household member cleaning up active vocabulary.

Why:

- Category should stop being used for future classification.

Possible outcomes:

- Category becomes Archived.
- Archive is rejected.

Business impact:

- Future selection is simplified while historical meaning remains.

## Restore Category

Who makes it:

- Household member who needs an archived category again.

Why:

- Old meaning becomes relevant again or archive was mistaken.

Possible outcomes:

- Category becomes Active.
- Category remains Archived.

Business impact:

- Category re-enters future household vocabulary.

## Accept Provider Suggestion

Who makes it:

- Household member reviewing suggested meaning.

Why:

- External evidence appears plausible.

Possible outcomes:

- Suggestion becomes accepted household category meaning.
- Suggestion is ignored.
- Suggestion is rejected or overridden.

Business impact:

- Convenience is gained only when user understanding is preserved.

## Review Category Summary

Who makes it:

- Household during review.

Why:

- Household wants to understand actual income or spending by purpose.

Possible outcomes:

- Summary is accepted as useful.
- Summary is treated as incomplete due to uncategorized or wrong meaning.

Business impact:

- Household awareness improves or confidence decreases.

## Question Shared Meaning

Who makes it:

- Partner or household member.

Why:

- Category meaning is unclear, sensitive, or disputed.

Possible outcomes:

- Meaning is clarified.
- Meaning is corrected.
- Transaction remains Uncategorized.
- Prior valid meaning remains.

Business impact:

- Trust may improve through clarity or suffer if category review becomes blame-oriented.
