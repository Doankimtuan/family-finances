# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Categories may classify transactions linked to accounts. Accounts continue to own real balances and location of money.

Impact:

- Category summaries must not look like account balances.
- Category changes must not mutate account truth.

## Transactions

Transactions are the main consumer of category meaning.

Impact:

- Approved decisions reinforce category as transaction meaning.
- Corrections can update meaning without rewriting money facts.
- Uncategorized state remains valid when meaning is unknown.

## Cards

Cards may expose merchant category, MCC, rewards category, statement, refund, or billing evidence.

Impact:

- Card provider category can inform category suggestion only.
- Household category may differ from card-network classification.
- Card repayment truth remains outside Categories.

## Loans

Loans own liability and repayment truth.

Impact:

- Category decisions clarify that loan repayment should not be confused with purchase purpose.
- Categories may label related transaction meaning but do not own amortization or debt state.

## Planning

Planning owns virtual intention and jar capacity.

Impact:

- Category-to-jar mapping remains interpretation, not category-owned planning.
- Category actuals may inform planning, but Categories do not create available-to-spend values.

## Goals

Goals own future outcomes.

Impact:

- Category evidence may help interpret goal-related spending.
- Categories do not own goal progress, goal funding, or completion.

## Inbox

Inbox owns unresolved review work.

Impact:

- Uncategorized or unclear category meaning can become reviewable work.
- Inbox resolution may use category vocabulary, but Inbox owns decision state.

## Health

Health is read-only.

Impact:

- Health may read categorized patterns.
- Health must not create, change, approve, or reject categories.

## Categories

Categories own classification vocabulary only.

Impact:

- Approved scope is intentionally modest.
- Modified scope protects historical meaning and provider-suggestion safety.
- Rejected scope prevents Categories from becoming money, planning, or automation authority.

## Together

Together owns household membership and shared finance context.

Impact:

- Categories are household-visible vocabulary.
- Partner meaning review must remain comprehension-oriented, not surveillance or approval workflow.

## Provider Integrations

Providers may supply descriptors, merchant identifiers, MCCs, wallet labels, or suggested categories.

Impact:

- Provider category is evidence only.
- Provider-driven automation remains constrained by user-understood household meaning.
