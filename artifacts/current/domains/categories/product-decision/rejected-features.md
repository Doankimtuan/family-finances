# Rejected Features

## Rejected Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why rejected | Conflict with product philosophy | Complexity | Financial risk | Maintenance cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAT-PD-021 | Category-specific notes or explanations | Duplicates transaction notes and partner discussion context. | Turns Categories into a discussion/policy surface. | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| CAT-PD-031 | Category holds money | Categories are labels, not containers. | Violates BR-01 and "user understands where money is." | High | High | High |
| CAT-PD-032 | Category available balance | Available money belongs to real accounts or virtual planning, not categories. | Creates category-as-budget confusion. | High | High | High |
| CAT-PD-033 | Category executes payments | Payment execution belongs outside Categories. | Violates financial safety before convenience. | High | High | High |
| CAT-PD-034 | Category enforces spending cap | Spending limits belong to Planning/Jars. | Turns Categories into budgets. | High | High | Medium |
| CAT-PD-035 | Category replaces jar or budget | Duplicates and undermines Planning. | Violates Real Ledger != Virtual Planning. | High | High | High |
| CAT-PD-036 | Category certifies provider truth | Provider categories are hints, not household truth. | Violates user-understood financial safety. | Medium | High | Medium |
| CAT-PD-037 | Category decides household intent autonomously | Household meaning cannot be safely inferred without review. | Violates no unnecessary automation. | High | High | High |

## Rejection Rationale

Rejected capabilities either blur ownership boundaries or create low-value complexity. The board rejects any model where Categories become money containers, available-balance surfaces, autonomous decision-makers, provider-truth authorities, or replacement planning objects.
