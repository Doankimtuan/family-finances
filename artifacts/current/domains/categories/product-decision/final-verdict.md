# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Categories are product-healthy when kept modest.

The domain solves a validated household problem: transaction history needs shared meaning. Categories are useful as classification labels, but they become risky if elevated into budgets, balances, automation authority, or provider truth.

## Decision Summary

- The core Categories model is approved: define household category, distinguish income/expense labels, categorize transactions, leave unknown meaning, correct meaning, filter history, maintain household vocabulary, and keep categories separate from money containers.
- Actuals summaries, historical meaning, read-only cross-domain evidence, rename/archive/restore, provider hints, and partner shared meaning are approved only with strict modifications.
- Merge, split, visual identity, duplicate detection, confidence, merchant learning, merchant normalization, item-level classification, templates, reconciliation, drift detection, export portability, and multi-currency context are deferred.
- Category-specific notes and all category-as-money, category-as-budget, category-as-payment, provider-truth, and autonomous-intent behaviors are rejected.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The core category-as-meaning model is strongly supported by Phase 1 discovery and Phase 2 household validation. Confidence is reduced by unresolved Vietnamese terminology, category-to-jar comprehension, provider-label trust, mixed-purchase frequency, partner sensitivity, and long-term maintenance behavior.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat categories as accounts, jars, budgets, balances, payment executors, or spending limits.
- Treat provider or merchant categories as final household truth.
- Let Health write to category meaning.
- Let Categories mutate real transaction facts beyond classification meaning.
- Introduce automation that users cannot understand or override.
- Expand deferred category power before Vietnam-first household research validates it.
