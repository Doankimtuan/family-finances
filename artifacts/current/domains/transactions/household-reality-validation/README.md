# Transactions Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Transactions domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared money in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a factual record of what moved, when it moved, and why it matters to the household?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Single people preparing for shared finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and households with children.
- Shared finance between partners, including partially shared and informally shared money.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household behavior.
- Transaction awareness.
- Shared review behavior.
- Manual-work tolerance.
- Emotional trust.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Transactions as factual records of real money movement: income, expense, refunds, corrections, transfer-like movement, provider activity, cash activity, and reviewable financial evidence.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
