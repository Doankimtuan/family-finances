# Rejected Features

No discovered Transactions capability is permanently rejected in this phase.

The board found several capabilities premature or risky, but they remain valid domain observations when tightly bounded or deferred. The strongest rejection stance is embedded as constraints on modified capabilities:

- Transactions must not become virtual planning movement.
- Health must not write back to transactions.
- AI must not invent, mutate, or autonomously classify real ledger facts as final truth.
- Provider data must not override household trust without validation.

These constraints are product philosophy protections, not rejected standalone capabilities from Phase 1 or Phase 2.
