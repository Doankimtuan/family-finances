# Deferred Features

## TX-PD-014: Pending Versus Posted Distinction

Reason: Real but provider-dependent and terminology-heavy.

Conditions to reconsider: Provider imports or user research show pending confusion is common.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x or 2.x.

## TX-PD-015: Merchant Normalization

Reason: Valuable but needs Vietnam-specific merchant and provider research.

Conditions to reconsider: Transaction diaries show cryptic names block review.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x.

## TX-PD-016: Receipt Attachment

Reason: Useful evidence, but not core to simple transaction memory.

Conditions to reconsider: Users frequently need receipts for refunds, school, medical, warranty, or disputes.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-017: Split Categorization

Reason: Real mixed-purchase pain, but high complexity and categorization burden.

Conditions to reconsider: Research shows mixed purchases materially distort household understanding.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x or 2.x.

## TX-PD-018: Duplicate Detection

Reason: Broad duplicate matching is premature without provider import maturity.

Conditions to reconsider: Manual/import overlap becomes common.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-019: Recurring Pattern Detection

Reason: Useful observation, but recurring behavior belongs beyond core transaction facts.

Conditions to reconsider: Households repeatedly miss subscriptions or repeated bills.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x.

## TX-PD-021: Provider Import

Reason: Vietnam provider reliability, consent, completeness, and duplicate risk remain unvalidated.

Conditions to reconsider: Provider access becomes reliable and user demand is validated.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-022: Cash Reconciliation

Reason: Cash leakage is real, but formal reconciliation may be too tedious.

Conditions to reconsider: Household diaries show cash gaps regularly destroy trust.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x or 2.x.

## TX-PD-023: Foreign Currency Metadata

Reason: Low current relevance for Vietnam-first young households.

Conditions to reconsider: Travel, remittance, remote income, or study-abroad cases become common.

Suggested future phase: Future.

## TX-PD-024: Fees and Discounts As Explicit Context

Reason: Adds detail before users prove need.

Conditions to reconsider: Wallet promotions, fees, shipping, or FX differences cause repeated confusion.

Suggested future phase: Version 1.x.

## TX-PD-025: Partner Comments/Explanations

Reason: Real behavior, but emotionally sensitive and can feel like surveillance.

Conditions to reconsider: Partner review research shows explanations improve trust without blame.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-026: Open Banking/Provider-Sync Enrichment

Reason: Same risk family as provider import; premature for simple-first scope.

Conditions to reconsider: Reliable, consent-safe provider ecosystem exists.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-027: Reliable Merchant Identity

Reason: Depends on normalization research and data quality.

Conditions to reconsider: Merchant recognition becomes a top review blocker.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-028: Confidence Scoring

Reason: Only valuable after imported or suggested classifications exist.

Conditions to reconsider: Users need to distinguish system certainty from confirmed facts.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-029: Dispute/Chargeback/Provider Correction Audit

Reason: Important but specialized and provider-dependent.

Conditions to reconsider: Card/wallet dispute scenarios become frequent enough for target households.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-030: Household-Level Review History

Reason: Useful for accountability but raises personal/shared sensitivity.

Conditions to reconsider: Research validates review attribution as trust-building, not blame-inducing.

Suggested future phase: Version 2.x.

## TX-PD-031: International Remittance/Currency Conversion Context

Reason: Future pressure only for current Vietnam-first target.

Conditions to reconsider: Cross-border households, remote income, or remittance become common target segments.

Suggested future phase: Future.
