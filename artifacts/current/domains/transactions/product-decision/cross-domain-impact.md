# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Approved transaction facts explain account balance changes. Transfer handling affects whether movement between owned accounts is treated as neutral rather than income or expense.

## Transactions

The domain remains the factual event layer. Approved and modified decisions protect it from becoming planning, analytics, or automation.

## Cards

Card purchases, refunds, and payments interact with Transactions. Card bill payment confusion remains a key validation concern. Provider lifecycle states are deferred.

## Loans

Loan disbursements and repayments may appear as transaction facts. Loan schedules and interest modeling remain outside Transactions.

## Planning

Planning may consume categorized or mapped transaction facts. Planning does not own or mutate transaction truth.

## Goals

Goals may use transactions as evidence of progress or spending, but goal intention remains separate from real ledger facts.

## Inbox

Inbox receives unresolved transaction facts for review. Inbox decisions may clarify meaning, but should not erase the underlying transaction fact.

## Health

Health reads transaction facts as signals only. It must not create, modify, or correct transactions.

## Categories

Categories explain household meaning. They must not be treated as real-money movement or as automatic planning movement.

## Together

Shared transaction review can build trust or create tension. Partner explanations and review history are deferred pending privacy and blame-risk research.
