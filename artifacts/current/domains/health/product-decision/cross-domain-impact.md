# Cross-Domain Impact

This document describes interactions only. It does not redesign boundaries.

## Accounts

Health may read account visibility and real-money context. Accounts continue to own balances, account identity, and account lifecycle.

## Transactions

Health may read recent activity rhythm, income/spending evidence, and medical-category pressure. Transactions continue to own cleared money movement.

## Cards

Health may read card burden and repayment behavior as risk signals. Cards continue to own card truth, statement state, payment state, and utilization facts.

## Loans

Health may read loan burden, payment pressure, and repayment state as risk signals. Loans continue to own loan truth and repayment lifecycle.

## Planning

Health may read plan presence and rhythm. Planning continues to own jars, allocations, capacity, and intention. Health must not treat planning values as real balances.

## Goals

Health may read goal or emergency-intention context later, if source truth exists. Goals continue to own future target intentions and progress.

## Inbox

Health may read unresolved decision burden and decision rhythm. Inbox continues to own review items and decision closure.

## Health

Health owns only interpretation: condition, factors, read-only scenarios, and completeness context. It must not own source-domain facts.

## Categories

Health may read category patterns such as medical, family support, or recurring pressure. Categories continue to serve classification and do not become Health-owned scoring rules.

## Together

Health may read household membership and visibility constraints. Together continues to own household membership, roles, and permissions.
