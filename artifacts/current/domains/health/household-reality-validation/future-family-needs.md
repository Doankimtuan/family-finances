# Future Family Needs

## Marriage

Marriage increases the need for shared visibility, trust, and neutral language. Health remains relevant because partners need a common picture of condition, not only separate balances.

## Children

Children increase recurring costs, medical exposure, school planning, emergency needs, and long-term anxiety. Health becomes more important as resilience matters more than simple monthly survival.

## Buying A Home

Home purchase introduces large debt, down payment goals, insurance, maintenance, and long-term cash-flow pressure. Health needs to remain meaningful when obligations stretch across years.

## Buying A Car

Vehicle purchase introduces loan payments, insurance, maintenance, fuel, parking, and depreciation. Health must continue to reflect pressure from ownership costs, not only purchase moment.

## Growing Income

Higher income can improve stability but can also increase lifestyle spending, debt capacity, and family expectations. Health remains useful if it reflects resilience rather than income alone.

## Multiple Accounts

More bank accounts, cards, wallets, savings products, and cash pockets increase visibility problems. Health becomes more valuable as a summary layer, but trust depends on source completeness.

## Family Expansion

Supporting parents, siblings, children, and relatives can become a major part of household finance. Health must remain compatible with emotional obligations that are not purely contractual.

## Career Changes

Job changes, freelance work, business income, maternity leave, and unemployment all affect stability. Health remains relevant if it can tolerate income uncertainty conceptually.

## Healthcare Needs

Pregnancy, childbirth, child illness, chronic conditions, and elder care increase medical-expense uncertainty. Health remains long-term relevant because medical shocks are financially material in Vietnam.

## Long-Term Household Management

Over 5-10 years, Health should still make sense as a reflection of liquidity, obligations, decisions, resilience, and trust. The core concept is stable even as the household becomes more complex.
