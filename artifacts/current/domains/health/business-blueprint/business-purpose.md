# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Health exists because household financial condition is not visible from any single record. A bank balance can look fine while bills, debt, medical costs, partner disagreement, or unresolved decisions create real pressure.

Young households need a shared, calm interpretation of visible financial reality.

## Core Responsibility

Health's core responsibility is to assess visible household financial condition and explain why that condition appears.

It must:

- Read source-domain facts.
- Determine whether those facts are sufficient for assessment.
- Produce a condition signal.
- Explain contributing factors.
- Mark incompleteness, staleness, or invalid interpretation.
- Remain read-only.

## Financial Principle

The governing financial principle is grounded resilience.

A household is financially healthier when visible facts show enough real-money clarity, manageable obligations, active planning rhythm, timely decisions, recent tracking rhythm, and understandable resilience against shocks.

Health must never confuse resilience with spendable cash.

## Business Outcome

The expected business outcome is shared household understanding:

- Users know what Health can see.
- Users know which factors are creating the condition.
- Users know when the signal is partial.
- Users know that actions belong in other domains.
- Users are not misled into unsafe financial behavior.
