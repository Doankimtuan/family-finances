# Final Verdict

## Business Completeness

Completeness: High for approved and modified Phase 3 scope.

The blueprint defines Health's business purpose, lifecycle, assessment states, primary flows, money-flow boundaries, business rules, cross-domain interactions, decisions, exceptional scenarios, and responsibility boundaries.

Deferred and rejected capabilities are intentionally excluded from operational business behavior.

## Business Consistency

Consistency: Strong.

Health remains a read-only interpretation domain. It consumes source-domain facts, produces grounded condition and explanations, and stops before action, advice, or money movement.

## Risk Summary

Key remaining risks:

- Users may overtrust a strong signal.
- Missing facts may reduce accuracy.
- Medical and insurance language may be misunderstood.
- Partner rhythm may feel blaming if not kept neutral.
- Scenario language may be mistaken for recommendation.
- Future trend and provider-derived capabilities remain out of scope.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The business behavior is deterministic and well aligned with Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3. Confidence is below 0.90 because terminology, emotional response, partner dynamics, medical/insurance boundaries, and long-term trend comprehension still need validation before richer Health expansion.

## Readiness For Implementation Contract

Readiness: Ready with constraints.
