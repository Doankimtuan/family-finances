# Loans Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Loans domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared borrowing obligations in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a domain that shows what they owe, when they must pay, and how repayment affects future household cash flow?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Single adults, couples, newly married partners, and young families.
- Shared finance between partners, including partially shared and informally shared money.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household borrowing behavior.
- Monthly affordability thinking.
- Partner coordination.
- Emotional pressure and trust.
- Manual tracking tolerance.
- Confusion around rates, fees, and schedules.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Loans as scheduled borrowing obligations: principal, lender, repayment schedule, interest, payments, lifecycle, and boundaries with Accounts, Transactions, Debts, Cards, Inbox, Calendar, and providers.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
