# Emotional Journey

## Before Borrowing

Common feelings:

- Hope that a need can be solved.
- Pressure from urgency.
- Anxiety about affordability.
- Optimism if the monthly payment looks manageable.

Trust is gained when:

- Terms are understandable.
- Partner knows the obligation.
- Payment impact feels realistic.

Trust is lost when:

- Fees or future rates are hidden.
- One partner discovers the loan late.

## At Creation Or First Tracking

Common feelings:

- Relief from bringing the obligation into view.
- Confusion around rate, term, and total repayment.
- Mild shame if the loan feels irresponsible.

Trust is gained when:

- The record matches lender or family expectations.
- The next payment is clear.

Trust is lost when:

- Required details are unknown.
- The product appears more certain than the user feels.

## During Active Repayment

Common feelings:

- Routine when payments are predictable.
- Anxiety near due date.
- Stress when salary arrives late.
- Irritation when provider records lag.
- Pride when remaining amount decreases.

Trust is gained when:

- Payment history feels consistent.
- Partner can see progress.
- Changes are explainable.

Trust is lost when:

- The recorded remaining amount conflicts with provider app.
- A missed payment is not obvious.

## During Trouble

Common feelings:

- Stress.
- Regret.
- Embarrassment.
- Fear of penalties or credit-history impact.
- Conflict between partners.

Trust is gained when:

- The household can reconstruct what happened.
- Records help clarify, not judge.

Trust is lost when:

- The product hides uncertainty.
- Family or partner expectations are misrepresented.

## Completion

Common feelings:

- Relief.
- Achievement.
- Pride.
- Desire to remove the burden from everyday view.

Trust is gained when:

- Completion matches lender confirmation or family agreement.
- Payment history remains available.

Trust is lost when:

- A residual balance appears after assumed completion.
- The record disappears before the household is confident.
