# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Loans exists because households often use future income to repay present borrowing.

For ViNha, the business purpose is to make that future obligation visible, understandable, and separable from ordinary spending and virtual plans.

## Core Responsibility

Loans answers:

- What does the household owe?
- Who is owed?
- How much was borrowed?
- How much principal remains according to recorded product truth?
- What payment is expected next?
- What has actually been paid?
- Is the obligation active, completed, cancelled, defaulted, archived, or under review?

## Financial Principle

A loan is a liability.

Loan repayment is both:

- A real money movement from a payment source.
- A reduction or attempted reduction of an obligation.

Planned schedules, payoff estimates, and future due dates are not real money movements.

## Business Outcome

The desired business outcome is a trustworthy household loan record that helps partners understand repayment burden without confusing:

- Recorded principal with lender-confirmed outstanding balance.
- Loan repayment with ordinary spending.
- Future payoff intention with real payment.
- Credit-card revolving balances with scheduled loans.
