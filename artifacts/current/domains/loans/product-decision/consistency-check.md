# Consistency Check

No approved decision conflicts with Phase 1 or Phase 2.

- Visible obligation.
- Monthly repayment awareness.
- Partner relevance.
- Real repayment history.
- Caution around provider truth.

## No Duplicated Capabilities

No approved Loan capability duplicates another domain's ownership:

- Accounts own where money is.
- Transactions own real money movement.
- Cards own revolving credit and statement cycles.
- Planning/Jars/Goals own virtual intentions.
- Health owns read-only summary.

## No Contradictory Decisions

Related decisions are consistent:

- Remaining principal is approved with recorded-truth limits.
- Provider-confirmed import is deferred.
- Early payoff is approved only as estimate.
- Automatic repayment execution is rejected.
