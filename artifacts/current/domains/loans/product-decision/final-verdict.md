# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Loans is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear scope controls.

The domain solves a real household problem, maps to validated young-household behavior, and protects ViNha's long-term financial-safety principles when provider truth, automation, and advisory complexity are handled cautiously.

## Decision Summary

- Core loan tracking is approved.
- Several capabilities are approved with modifications around recorded truth, household relevance, informal loans, rate clarity, and payoff estimates.
- Legal-party complexity, collateral, provider integrations, reconciliation automation, advisory analysis, and regulatory normalization are deferred.
- Capabilities that blur Loans with Cards, Planning, Health, or automatic payments are rejected.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.83

Reason:

Core decisions are strongly supported by Phase 1 discovery and Phase 2 validation. Confidence is reduced by limited direct user interviews, unresolved Vietnamese terminology, provider statement variability, informal-loan sensitivity, and unknown tolerance for manual tracking of small installments.

## Readiness for Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat credit-card revolving balances as Loans.
- Present recorded remaining principal as lender-confirmed outstanding balance.
- Move virtual payoff planning into Loans.
- Let Health mutate loan state.
- Introduce automatic repayment execution.
- Introduce provider import or reconciliation automation before validation.
