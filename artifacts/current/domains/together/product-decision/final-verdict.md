# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Together is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear scope protections.

The domain solves a real young-household problem: defining who belongs to shared finance and which assumptions are visible to partners. The approved scope is simple, maintainable, and aligned with ViNha's household-first identity.

## Decision Summary

- The household trust boundary is approved.
- Invitations, membership, simple policies, household preferences, and household-scoped protection are approved.
- Roles, policy events, membership history, and attribution are approved only with simplification and safety modifications.
- Multi-household, family complexity, separation lifecycle, external consent, and data-governance complexity are deferred.
- Surveillance, granular permissions, legal registry behavior, dispute resolution, and Together-driven money movement are rejected.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Expand Together into granular permissions.
- Treat Admin as money owner.
- Let Together move or allocate money.
- Turn policy history into partner surveillance.
- Model legal marriage or relationship disputes.
- Add multi-household, separation, or provider-consent complexity before validation.
