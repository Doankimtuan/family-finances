# Roadmap Classification

This file classifies product decisions by broad phase. It is not a release plan.

## MVP

| Capabilities | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| TGT-PD-001 to TGT-PD-015 | Core household trust boundary, invitation, policies, scope, protection, and preferences are required for household-first MVP. |
| TGT-PD-003 with modifications | Simple Partner/Admin model is needed, but must stay non-hierarchical. |
| TGT-PD-016, TGT-PD-019, TGT-PD-020 with modifications | Recent material policy/invite attribution supports BR-13 trust without becoming heavy audit. |

## Version 1.x

| Capabilities | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| TGT-PD-017, TGT-PD-018 with modifications | Historical membership interpretation becomes more useful after real household usage accumulates. |
| TGT-PD-021 with modifications | Simple household meaning clarification may help after terminology validation. |
| TGT-PD-027 | Household export/deletion audit may be needed as data governance matures. |
| TGT-PD-031 | Broader access recovery should be informed by real onboarding failures. |

## Version 2.x

| Capabilities | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| TGT-PD-022 | Multi-household support requires deliberate privacy and switching model. |
| TGT-PD-023 | Complex family structures require research and careful boundary design. |
| TGT-PD-025 | Separation/split lifecycle is important but safety-sensitive. |
| TGT-PD-026, TGT-PD-028 | External consent depends on approved provider integrations. |
| TGT-PD-030 | Household leave/close lifecycle requires validated exit expectations. |

## Future

| Capabilities | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| TGT-PD-024 | Temporary access has low current validation and high permission complexity. |
| TGT-PD-029 | Cross-border settings are not core Vietnam-first scope. |

## Never

| Capabilities | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| TGT-PD-032 | Partner engagement summaries create surveillance risk. |
| TGT-PD-033 | Granular roles violate simple household product philosophy. |
| TGT-PD-034 | Legal registry is outside product responsibility. |
| TGT-PD-035 | Relationship dispute resolution is not a product capability. |
| TGT-PD-036 | Together must never move or allocate money. |
