# Modified Features

## Capabilities Approved With Modifications

| ID | Capability | Original idea | Required modification | Reason | Expected result |
|----|------------|---------------|----------------------|--------|-----------------|
| TGT-PD-003 | Distinguish roles | Roles distinguish household members. | Keep only simple Partner/Admin meaning; Admin is policy responsibility, not ownership. | Avoid hierarchy and enterprise permissions. | Clear responsibility without undermining equal daily participation. |
| TGT-PD-016 | Recent policy events | Show policy event history. | Limit to material recent policy context. | Users need clarity, not an audit-console experience. | Partner-visible change awareness without complexity. |
| TGT-PD-017 | Membership history | Preserve who belonged. | Keep history minimal and privacy-sensitive. | Former access and responsibility are sensitive. | Historical interpretation without building separation workflows. |
| TGT-PD-018 | Inactive member representation | Represent inactive members. | Represent only enough for historical clarity. | Avoid complex lifecycle/state-machine expansion. | Past records remain understandable. |
| TGT-PD-019 | Invitation attribution | Track who invited whom. | Use as accountability context only. | Avoid blame or social-pressure mechanics. | Household can understand invite origin. |
| TGT-PD-020 | Policy-change attribution | Track who changed policy. | Attribute material changes without turning into partner scoring. | Phase 2 validated trust need but warned about blame. | Better trust and memory. |
| TGT-PD-021 | Manual household meaning clarification | Clarify household meaning manually. | Keep as simple descriptive context; do not create agreement management. | Prevents Together from becoming a contract or dispute domain. | Users can understand household context in ordinary language. |

## Modification Principle

All modified capabilities must remain collaboration context. They must not become granular permission systems, legal contracts, partner performance analytics, or automated money behavior.
