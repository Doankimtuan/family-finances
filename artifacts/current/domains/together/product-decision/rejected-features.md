# Rejected Features

## Rejected Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why rejected | Conflict with product philosophy | Complexity | Financial risk | Maintenance cost |
|----|------------|--------------|----------------------------------|------------|----------------|------------------|
| TGT-PD-032 | Partner engagement summaries | Creates surveillance and blame risk. | Violates household trust and calm finance. | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| TGT-PD-033 | Granular custom roles and permissions | Over-engineered for young households. | Violates simple before powerful. | High | High | High |
| TGT-PD-034 | Legal family or marriage registry | Not needed to define shared finance. | Together is a household trust boundary, not legal infrastructure. | High | Medium | High |
| TGT-PD-035 | Relationship dispute resolution | Product cannot adjudicate fairness or conflict. | Facts before judgment; no relationship counseling. | High | Critical | High |
| TGT-PD-036 | Together-initiated money movement | Together must not move or allocate money. | Violates Real Ledger boundaries and no unnecessary automation. | High | Critical | High |

## Rejection Principle

Rejected capabilities either create unsafe power dynamics, blur money-domain ownership, or turn ViNha into enterprise, legal, or counseling software. These are permanently outside Together's product responsibility.
