# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Together exists because ViNha is household finance software, not only personal finance software.

A household cannot safely track money, plan spending, review decisions, or reflect on financial health until the product knows who belongs to the shared financial space.

## Core Responsibility

Together's core responsibility is to define and protect the household trust boundary.

It owns:

- Who the household is.
- Who belongs to the household.
- Which member responsibilities exist.
- How a partner joins.
- Which household-level policies are current.
- Which material policy changes are visible as household context.
- Which household preferences shape money and time interpretation.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is shared accountability without ownership confusion.

Together defines who can participate in household finance. It does not decide where money is, what money moved, what jars mean, or whether the household is healthy.

## Business Outcome

A successful Together outcome is:

- Every household-scoped financial record has a clear household boundary.
- Members understand who belongs.
- Partners share daily financial participation.
- Policy responsibility is simple and non-hierarchical.
- Material household policy changes are not hidden.
- Non-members cannot access household financial information.
