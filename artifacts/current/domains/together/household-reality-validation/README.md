# Together Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Together domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared finance in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a domain that defines who "we" are, what financial visibility is shared, and which household assumptions govern money behavior?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and households preparing for long-term financial management.
- Shared finance between partners, including partial pooling and informal shared money.

Validated dimensions:

- Household collaboration behavior.
- Partner trust and visibility.
- Role and responsibility expectations.
- Policy awareness.
- Emotional safety.
- Manual-work tolerance.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Together as the household trust boundary: membership, roles, shared policies, partner visibility, and household-level assumptions.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
