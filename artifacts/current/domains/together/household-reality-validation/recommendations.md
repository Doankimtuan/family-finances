# Recommendations

This document provides validation observations only. It does not redesign business, create product decisions, or propose implementation.

## Validated

- Together solves a real household problem: defining who belongs in shared finance.
- Young households naturally understand shared money as "ours," even when accounts are individually held.
- One active money manager and one less-engaged partner is a realistic pattern.
- Partner-visible material changes are behaviorally important.
- Together should remain separate from money movement and financial calculation.
- A small role model is more realistic than a large permissions system for young couples.
- Long-term household finance requires a stable trust boundary.

## Needs Review

- Whether "Admin" language feels hierarchical.
- Whether two-member assumptions cover enough Vietnam-first households.
- Whether policy terminology is concrete enough for users with low financial confidence.
- Whether family support should remain outside Together while still being acknowledged elsewhere.
- Whether users understand shared visibility as different from legal authority.

## Needs Research

- Vietnamese-language terminology validation.
- Couple interviews across pooling styles: full pool, fixed contribution, separate accounts, single income, variable income.
- Less-engaged partner comprehension and comfort.
- Privacy boundaries for personal accounts, e-wallets, hidden debt, and family obligations.
- Sensitive lifecycle cases: breakup, divorce, unsafe partner access, death, and account deletion.
- Actual household usage of policy history over time.

## Future Opportunity

- Multi-household life may become relevant as users support parents while managing their own household.
- Consent and external-data sharing may grow in importance as financial integrations mature.
- Former-member and household-closure interpretation may matter over 5-10 years.
- Responsibility mapping may matter as children, parents, and property obligations increase.

These observations are not roadmap priorities.
