# Navigation Review

## Assessment

The approved navigation is sound:

Home | Money | Plan | Inbox | Together

Health via Home is correct. Categories should not be primary navigation. Assets/Wealth should not appear as current primary navigation.

## Discoverability

| Destination | Current Placement | Discoverability | Recommendation |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Savings | Money Hub section | Medium | Surface urgent maturity in Home/Inbox |
| Investments | Money future/product surface | Medium-low | Keep within Money when present; avoid Wealth primary nav |
| Loans | Money Debts | Medium | Label as "Loans" where scheduled obligation matters |
| Cards | Money Hub section | Medium | Surface due pressure in Money Hub strip |
| Inbox | Primary tab and Home CTA | Strong | Badge only for decision-bearing items |
| Planning | Primary Plan tab | Strong | Use "Plan" in nav, "Jars" inside |
| Goals | Plan section | Medium | Surface active goal progress on Plan Hub |
| Health | Home chip | Medium | Keep chip visible but not alarmist |

## Recommendations

### PX-NV-01: Add Money Hub urgency strip

Problem: Product domains are discoverable but urgent product events can hide below the fold.

User Impact: Users miss due card payments, installments, or savings maturity.

Affected Screens: Money Hub, Home, Inbox Queue.

Frequency: Weekly/Event-based.

Business Impact: Medium-high.

Recommended UX: Add one compact "Upcoming" strip in Money Hub for cards, installments, savings maturity, and stale investment facts. Items link to their exact domain or Inbox item.

Implementation Cost: Medium.

Priority: P1.

### PX-NV-02: Preserve five-tab muscle memory

Problem: Additional domains may tempt future teams to add primary navigation.

User Impact: The product becomes harder to use one-handed and harder to remember.

Affected Screens: Global shell.

Frequency: Daily.

Business Impact: High long-term simplicity impact.

Recommended UX: Keep all new money products under Money, all intentions under Plan, all decisions under Inbox, all household boundaries under Together, and all interpretation under Health via Home.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P0.
