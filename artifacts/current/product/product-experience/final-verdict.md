# Final Verdict

## Verdict

APPROVED WITH UX IMPROVEMENTS

ViNha is product-experience viable as a Household Money Operating System for ordinary young families, provided UX hardening is completed before broad release.

## Answer To Mission Question

Can an ordinary young family naturally manage household finances without confusion?

Yes, with improvements. The approved architecture and business model can support natural household finance management, but the current experience contract needs stronger concept teaching, navigation cues, inbox discipline, mobile form reduction, and emotionally neutral language.

## Approval Basis

- The approved IA is simple enough: Home, Money, Plan, Inbox, Together, with Health behind Home.
- The daily loop is coherent: check Home, capture in Money, resolve Inbox only when needed.
- The monthly loop is coherent: clear decisions, review, lock, reflect, then Health.
- Household collaboration is visible without becoming surveillance or legal relationship tooling.
- Financial safety is preserved because real money, virtual intention, Inbox decisions, Together scope, and Health interpretation remain separated.

## Conditions

1. Do not ship GA without a first-time concept pass for Account, Jar, Goal, Saving, Investment, Loan, Inbox, and Health.
2. Do not allow Inbox to accept awareness-only items.
3. Do not show Health as a command center, optimizer, or action executor.
4. Do not make investment or goal progress appear spendable.
5. Do not make Month Ritual feel like an audit, blame session, or accounting exam.

## Final Declaration

The Product Experience Board approves ViNha with UX improvements. These improvements are experience-level implementation guidance only and do not redesign business rules, financial correctness, or architecture.
