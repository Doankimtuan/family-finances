# Product Experience Board - ViNha

Status: CURRENT

Board: Product Experience Board

Scope: Complete user experience validation for ViNha as a Household Money Operating System.

## Mission

Validate whether an ordinary young family, ages 20-35, can naturally manage household finances without confusion.

This board does not review financial correctness, redesign business rules, redesign architecture, or modify frozen SoT. Financial System Integration is treated as approved.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- Preserve BR-01: Real Ledger is not Virtual Jars.
- Preserve BR-24: Health is read-only.
- Preserve the approved five-tab IA: Home, Money, Plan, Inbox, Together; Health via Home.
- Do not make Categories, Decision Tools, Assets, Wealth, or Health a primary navigation item.
- Optimize for young families, calmness, trust, long-term use, financial discipline, and low cognitive load.
