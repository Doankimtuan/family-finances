# Long-Term Retention

## Assessment

ViNha can stay useful for years if it respects cadence. Users should not feel the product demands the same intensity every day.

## Cadence Review

| Cadence | User Need | Product Role |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Daily | Record important movement, see next decision | Home, Money, Inbox |
| Weekly | Clear queue, glance upcoming pressure | Inbox, Plan, Health chip |
| Monthly | Close and reflect | Month Ritual, Health |
| Quarterly | Review goals, savings, debt pressure | Plan, Savings, Loans, Health |
| Yearly | Understand household trend and update policies | Health, Together, Goals |

## Recommendations

### PX-LT-01: Shift from daily nagging to cadence-based attention

Problem: Finance apps become annoying when everything is urgent.

User Impact: Users mute or abandon the product.

Affected Screens: Home, Inbox, Health, Plan.

Frequency: Ongoing.

Business Impact: High retention impact.

Recommended UX: Daily surfaces show only current action. Weekly/monthly surfaces summarize patterns. Quarterly/yearly surfaces support review without daily interruption.

Implementation Cost: Medium.

Priority: P1.

### PX-LT-02: Make stale data honest but not accusatory

Problem: Long-term users will have gaps.

User Impact: They may feel they failed and never return.

Affected Screens: Home, Health, Inbox, Money.

Frequency: Weekly/Monthly.

Business Impact: Medium-high.

Recommended UX: Show "Some facts are stale" with specific source and next action. Avoid degrading the whole experience into warnings.

Implementation Cost: Medium.

Priority: P1.
