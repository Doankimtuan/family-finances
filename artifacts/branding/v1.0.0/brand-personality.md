# Brand Personality

Inherits and extends the product personality already frozen in [Design-Vision.md](../../design-foundation/v1.0.0/Design-Vision.md) (Calm · Adult · Collaborative · Precise · Honest · Grounded). This document adds the brand-expression layer on top: how personality shows up in mark, color, motion, and voice specifically.

## Communicates

| Trait | Visual expression | Verbal expression |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Enclosed, grounded silhouette; warm neutral surfaces | "Home · Money · Plan · Inbox · Together" navigation, never "Dashboard" |
| Trust | Single flat color, no gloss/gradient tricks, generous whitespace | Calm adult voice, concrete nouns (Jar, Inbox, Month Ritual) |
| Growth | Soft, rising arch geometry — never a chart arrow | "steady," "on track," never "moon," "10x," "gains" |
| Clarity | One mark, one hue family, one radius language | Short sentences, one idea per line |
| Partnership | Twin, equal-weight forms in the mark itself | "you and your partner," never "user" |
| Financial confidence | Icon stays legible and calm even at 16px — never busy | No shame-based copy; sparse celebration only at real milestones |
| Long-term planning | Grounded flat base on the mark (not floating/tilted) | Plan language distinguishes real vs. intention (`BR-01`) |
| Peace of mind | Negative-space "gap" at the top of the mark reads as breathing room, not a gap in trust | Errors are honest and recoverable, never silent |

## Never communicates

- **Luxury** — no gold, no serif wordmark, no ornament for its own sake.
- **Corporate / Enterprise** — no dashboard-grid brand system, no navy-and-gray "B2B SaaS" palette.
- **Aggressive fintech** — no neon gradients, no glow, no rocket/arrow-up iconography.
- **Trading / Speculation** — no candlestick shapes, no ticker motifs, no currency glyphs baked into the mark.

## Voice pairing (brand ↔ product)

The brand mark's calm-but-warm geometry must match the product's already-defined "calm adult voice" (see [Design-Vision.md § Product Voice](../../design-foundation/v1.0.0/Design-Vision.md)). If a marketing surface ever needs headline copy, it follows the same rule as in-product copy: concrete nouns over marketing verbs, one idea per line, never cute.

## Personality guardrails for future contributors

1. If a proposed asset (icon, illustration, motion) could belong to a bank, a budgeting spreadsheet SaaS, or a crypto wallet without changes, it fails the brand test.
2. If it needs a gradient or a shadow to look "premium," it fails — premium here means restraint, not effects.
3. If it would look dated in a screenshot from 2031, it fails the timelessness test in [design-rationale.md](./design-rationale.md).
