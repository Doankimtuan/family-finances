# Brand Strategy

## What ViNha is

A household finance operating system for couples and families: record expenses, manage income, run savings goals and installments, review spending together, and improve financial health over time. See [Executive-Summary.md](../../product-definition/CURRENT/Executive-Summary.md) for the product definition this brand serves.

## What ViNha is not

Not crypto, not stock trading, not a bank, not accounting software. The brand must never borrow the visual grammar of trading terminals, speculative fintech, or enterprise SaaS dashboards.

## The single promise

**Calm shared confidence.** Two people (or a family) always know what they have, what they meant to do with it, and what's next — together, without shame or spreadsheet archaeology. This is already the product's emotional goal in [Design-Vision.md](../../design-foundation/v1.0.0/Design-Vision.md) ("oriented, respected, capable together, never shamed"); the brand's job is to make that promise legible in three seconds, at 16px, before a single screen loads.

## Positioning by philosophy, not by imitation

We extract *principles* from category leaders, never their forms:

| Reference | Philosophy extracted | Explicitly not copied |
|---|---|---|
| Monzo / Revolut | Flat, single-mass icon silhouettes; one confident brand hue | Their coral/blue hue, their exact glyphs |
| Apple Wallet / Health | Icon reads as a *category*, not a literal object; generous safe space | Skeuomorphic card/heart shapes |
| Notion / Linear | Restraint — one glyph, no ornament, works equally at 16px and 1024px | Their monochrome/grayscale brand (ViNha needs warmth, not neutrality) |
| Things 3 | Soft geometric mark that feels handmade but is built on a strict grid | Checklist/task iconography |
| Copilot Money / YNAB | Trustworthy-but-friendly tone, never intimidating | Literal chart/graph marks |

## Brand pillars

1. **Home** — the app is where a household's money *lives*, not a ledger bolted onto a bank.
2. **Trust** — every visual decision favors legibility and honesty over cleverness (mirrors `BR-01` real≠virtual honesty already codified in product SoT).
3. **Growth** — quiet, steady, never a stock-chart arrow or a rocket.
4. **Clarity** — one glyph, one hue, no competing signals.
5. **Partnership** — the mark itself is built from two equal forms, not one dominant + one subordinate.

## Symbol thesis (locked)

Blend **Home** (protected shared space, cradle) with **Harmony/Balance** (supporting a single shared seed/coin). See [icon-decision.md](./icon-decision.md) for the resulting Cradle & Seed mark. Explicitly rejected: houses drawn literally, piggy banks, wallets, currency symbols, letters/text, upward arrows/charts — all listed as clichés to avoid and all counter to "iconic rather than descriptive."

## Timelessness test

The mark must still read as "a modern household finance app I can trust" in 2031. It is validated against trend-cycles (skeuomorphism, neumorphism, gradient-mesh branding, 3D-render icons) by containing zero decoration that depends on a rendering trend — flat single-color fill only, works identically whether iOS ships glass, flat, or whatever comes next.
