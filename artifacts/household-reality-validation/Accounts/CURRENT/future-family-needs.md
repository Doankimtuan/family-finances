# Future Family Needs

## Marriage

Likely needs:

- Shared visibility over previously personal accounts.
- Agreement on which accounts are household-relevant.
- Tracking wedding spending, gifts, and family contributions.
- Trust around income, debt, and savings transparency.

## Children

Likely needs:

- More recurring payments.
- Healthcare and childcare expenses.
- Education savings.
- Emergency liquidity.
- More cash and transfer activity involving relatives.

## Buying a Home

Likely needs:

- Down payment tracking.
- Mortgage-linked payment account.
- Long-term debt visibility.
- Distinction between liquid money and committed savings.
- Family contribution tracking.

## Buying a Car or Motorbike

Likely needs:

- Loan or installment payment account.
- Insurance and maintenance cash flow.
- Down payment source clarity.

## Growing Income

Likely needs:

- Multiple salary or freelance accounts.
- Higher savings balances.
- Investment or brokerage accounts.
- Stronger reconciliation expectations.

## Multiple Accounts

Likely needs:

- More banks for salary, savings, cards, loans, and promotions.
- Account closure and migration history.
- Better recognition of which account serves which purpose.

## Family Expansion

Likely needs:

- Support for parents or siblings.
- Money temporarily held outside the couple.
- Shared obligations across households.
- Greater sensitivity around privacy and transparency.

## Long-Term Stability

The concept of Accounts remains stable. The number and variety of containers will grow, but the household question stays the same: where is our money, where are our obligations, and what can we trust?
