# Accounts Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Accounts domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared money in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a domain that shows where real money and financial obligations live?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and households preparing for long-term financial management.
- Shared finance between partners, including partially shared and informally shared money.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household behavior.
- Financial habits.
- Emotional trust.
- Manual-work tolerance.
- Account understanding.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Accounts as real-world financial containers: bank accounts, cash, e-wallets, savings accounts, credit obligations, and similar places where money or financial position is held.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
