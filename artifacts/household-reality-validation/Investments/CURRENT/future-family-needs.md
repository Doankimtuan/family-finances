# Future Family Needs

## Marriage

Likely needs:

- Clarify which investments are personal and which are household assets.
- Build trust around risk-taking.
- Explain previous investments brought into marriage.
- Align on what surplus can be invested.

## Children

Likely needs:

- Separate emergency cash from long-term investments.
- Understand whether education-related money is exposed to market risk.
- Reduce avoidable surprises during childbirth, childcare, healthcare, and school expenses.

## Buying A Home

Likely needs:

- Know which investments can be liquidated for down payment.
- Understand potential loss or delay before relying on investment value.
- Compare long-term holding preference with near-term cash need.

## Buying A Car Or Motorbike

Likely needs:

- Decide whether to sell investments, use savings, or borrow.
- Understand opportunity cost and liquidity impact.

## Growing Income

Likely needs:

- More surplus may create more investment experimentation.
- Multiple platforms and assets may appear.
- Partner alignment becomes more important as amounts grow.

## Multiple Accounts

Likely needs:

- Broker accounts, fund apps, bank accounts, and gold holdings may multiply.
- Household may need clearer memory of where assets are held.

## Family Expansion

Likely needs:

- Parents or relatives may influence investment choices.
- Family business, land, gold, or informal deals may become financially significant.
- Inheritance and gifted assets may enter the household picture.

## 5-10 Year Stability

Likely needs:

- Keep investment truth understandable after provider changes, life events, and asset exits.
- Preserve distinction between cash, savings, investment value, and planning intention.
- Support more mature conversations about long-term wealth without requiring professional investor behavior from ordinary households.
