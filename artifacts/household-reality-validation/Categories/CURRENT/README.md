# Categories Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Categories domain matches how young Vietnamese households classify shared money activity in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand category labels as meaning for transactions, without confusing categories with accounts, jars, budgets, merchants, or real balances?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Shared finance between partners.
- Single people preparing for shared household finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and families with children.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Natural category language.
- Daily and monthly categorization behavior.
- Shared partner comprehension.
- Manual work tolerance.
- Confusion risk between Categories, Transactions, Jars, Accounts, and provider labels.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Categories as household classification labels for money events. It identified their core purpose as meaning, not money storage or planning capacity.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against realistic young-household behavior. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
