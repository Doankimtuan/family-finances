# Health Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Health domain matches how young Vietnamese households understand and manage shared financial condition in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need a read-only financial health mirror that explains condition, pressure, and resilience without becoming advice, automation, or a money-moving domain?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Shared finance between partners.
- Single people preparing for shared household finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and families with children.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real financial-health concerns.
- Household understanding of scores, levels, and signals.
- Partner decision behavior.
- Emotional response to financial condition feedback.
- Medical-expense and emergency-pressure relevance.
- Manual work tolerance.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Health as a read-only household financial condition mirror. It identified Health's responsibility as interpreting source-domain facts into stability, pressure, resilience, and attention signals.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against young-household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
