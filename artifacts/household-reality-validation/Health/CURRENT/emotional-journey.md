# Emotional Journey

## First Encounter

Likely emotions:

- Curiosity.
- Skepticism.
- Hope for reassurance.
- Concern about being judged.

Trust is gained when Health explains itself using facts users recognize.

Trust is lost if Health feels like a black-box score or generic lecture.

## Stable Period

Likely emotions:

- Confidence.
- Relief.
- Pride.
- Reduced need to inspect every detail.

Trust is gained when the signal remains calm and consistent with lived experience.

Trust is lost if Health overreacts to minor noise.

## Pressure Period

Likely emotions:

- Anxiety.
- Defensiveness.
- Urgency.
- Uncertainty.

Trust is gained when Health names visible pressure without shame.

Trust is lost if it appears to blame one partner or oversimplify the cause.

## Emergency Period

Likely emotions:

- Stress.
- Fear.
- Tunnel vision.
- Need for clarity.

Trust is gained when Health separates financial impact from medical, insurance, or moral judgment.

Trust is lost if it sounds advisory, clinical, or falsely certain.

## Recovery Period

Likely emotions:

- Regret.
- Relief.
- Cautious optimism.
- Desire to regain control.

Trust is gained when Health supports re-entry after avoidance or shock.

Trust is lost if prior weakness follows users as a permanent label.

## Long-Term Household Period

Likely emotions:

- Achievement.
- Fatigue.
- Ambition.
- Concern for children and parents.

Trust is gained when Health still makes sense as the family becomes more complex.

Trust is lost if it remains shallow while household responsibilities grow.
