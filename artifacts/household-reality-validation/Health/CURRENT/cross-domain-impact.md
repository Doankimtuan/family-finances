# Cross-Domain Impact

This document describes natural interactions only. It does not redesign boundaries.

## Accounts

Health depends on Accounts for real money location and account visibility. Accounts own balances; Health interprets condition.

## Transactions

Health reads transaction rhythm, spending evidence, income evidence, and medical or unusual expense events. Transactions own the money movement record.

## Planning

Health reads plan presence, allocation pressure, and intention rhythm. Planning owns jars, capacity, and intended use of money.

## Goals

Health may interpret future-goal pressure, emergency-goal presence, or progress patterns. Goals own named future intentions.

## Inbox

Health reads unresolved decision burden and decision rhythm. Inbox owns review items, approvals, rejections, and decision closure.

## Cards

Health may interpret utilization, repayment pressure, fees, and credit reliance. Cards own card product truth and statement/payment state.

## Loans

Health may interpret debt burden, payment pressure, delinquency risk, or payoff progress. Loans own loan product truth and repayment lifecycle.

## Categories

Health may read category-level patterns such as medical spending, family support, or recurring pressure. Categories do not become Health-owned rules.

## Together

Health depends on household membership, permissions, and shared visibility. Together owns who belongs to the household and what they can see or do.

## Month Close

Health may use closed periods for more stable comparison. Month Close owns monthly ritual and period finality.

## Insurance / External Providers

Health may read coverage, premium, claim, or reimbursement facts if available elsewhere. Insurance providers or future insurance domains own policy truth.
