# Recommendations

This document contains classified observations only. It does not redesign the business, create Product Decisions, or define implementation.

## Validated

- Health solves a real household problem: users need to know whether the household is financially okay.
- A read-only mirror matches user trust needs better than an action-taking domain.
- Young households understand condition through pressure, liquidity, debt, decisions, and recent rhythm.
- Partner-shared finance needs neutral language for condition.
- Medical expense exposure is a real Vietnam-first financial-health concern.
- Health remains relevant over 5-10 years as households gain children, debt, assets, and obligations.
- The distinction between Health observation and source-domain action is essential.

## Needs Review

- Whether "Health" is too easily confused with medical health.
- Whether scores, levels, or pulse language feel motivating or judgmental.
- Whether Health can explain incomplete data without losing trust.
- Whether partner-facing Health creates blame or alignment.
- Whether current Health understanding underweights debt, cards, income stability, and emergency buffer.
- Whether medical-cost context can stay financial rather than clinical or advisory.

## Needs Research

- Vietnam-first terminology testing for `suc khoe tai chinh`, pulse, score, signal, pressure, and resilience.
- Interviews with singles, newly married couples, couples with children, and variable-income households.
- Observation of real payday, bill-payment, and month-end conversations.
- Medical emergency and insurance reimbursement stories from young households.
- Manual-work tolerance after missing data, transaction corrections, or partner disagreement.
- Trust response to good, neutral, and bad Health signals.

## Future Opportunity

- Deeper validation of Health across family growth, home purchase, car purchase, childcare, elder care, and higher income.
- Better understanding of emotional resilience and avoidance behavior.
- More nuanced long-term validation of shared financial-health rituals.
- International comparison only after Vietnam-first behavior is validated.

These are observations only, not roadmap priorities.
