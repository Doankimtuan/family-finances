# Recommendations

This document contains classified observations only. It does not redesign the business, create Product Decisions, define implementation, or prioritize roadmap.

## Validated

- Cards are a real household finance concern for young Vietnamese households.
- Credit-card use creates a real obligation that affects monthly planning.
- Users naturally care about due date, amount owed, limit, repayment, refunds, and shared responsibility.
- Credit-card outstanding must remain separate from real cash balance.
- Debit cards are usually understood as access to a bank account rather than a separate household asset.
- Card repayment must be distinguishable from original card purchase.
- Shared finance makes card visibility and purchase attribution emotionally important.
- The domain can remain meaningful for 5-10 years because cards, online payments, subscriptions, installments, and family expenses tend to increase with household maturity.

## Needs Review

- Whether the domain should foreground all card types or focus household attention mainly on credit-card obligations.
- Whether card infrastructure terminology is too distant from ordinary household language.
- Whether current terminology cleanly separates statement balance, outstanding balance, due amount, and available credit.
- Whether cashback should be treated as reward, discount, income, or statement credit in future product decisions.
- Whether card installments belong primarily to Cards, Loans, or a shared interpretation layer.
- Whether supplementary-card concepts matter for the initial target segment.

## Needs Research

- How target users describe card concepts in Vietnamese without prompting.
- How often young households use credit cards compared with debit cards, QR, e-wallets, and transfers.
- How many households pay full balance versus partial or minimum payments.
- How users handle refunds, disputes, and unfamiliar charges.
- How much manual transaction tracking is acceptable.
- Whether users can reliably obtain statement date, due date, minimum payment, and fees from issuer apps.
- How partners negotiate personal versus shared card spending.
- How users react when household records differ from issuer app records.

## Future Opportunity

- Better understanding of card-on-file subscriptions.
- Better understanding of virtual cards and tokenized wallet cards.
- Better understanding of children-related card controls and supplementary-card behavior.
- Better understanding of cross-border e-commerce, travel, FX, and dispute behavior.
- Better understanding of credit-health awareness as income and household obligations grow.
- Better understanding of rewards optimization without encouraging overspending.

These are observations only and do not create product scope.
