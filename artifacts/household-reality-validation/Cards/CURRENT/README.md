# Cards Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Cards domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared card spending, repayment obligations, and card-related uncertainty in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a domain that separates card payment convenience from real money, credit obligation, billing timing, and household trust?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Singles, couples, newly married partners, young families, and families preparing for long-term financial management.
- Shared finance between partners, including partially shared money, one-person-operated finances, and informal household repayment responsibility.

Validated dimensions:

- Real card usage behavior.
- Credit-card and debit-card understanding.
- Household coordination.
- Emotional trust.
- Manual-work tolerance.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Cards as payment instruments and, for credit cards, short-term revolving credit obligations with issuer-defined billing cycles, limits, fees, interest, refunds, disputes, and repayment.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
