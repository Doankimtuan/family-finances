# Final Verdict

## Does This Domain Accurately Reflect Real Household Financial Behavior?

Yes.

Goals accurately reflect a real young-household need: families need named future intentions that help them protect priorities before money is spent elsewhere. Vietnamese households may not use formal product language, but they naturally think in goals such as emergency fund, wedding, baby, travel, school, motorbike, home setup, family support, and healthcare.

## Strengths

- Solves a real and frequent problem: future priorities are easily swallowed by daily spending.
- Uses a natural household mental model: "we are saving for this."
- Supports shared finance between partners.
- Carries emotional motivation, not just accounting structure.
- Fits both short-term and long-term household life.
- Preserves the critical distinction between intention and real money truth.

## Weaknesses

- Goal progress can easily be mistaken for real available money.
- Contribution language can imply a real transfer even when it is only an intention record.
- Goal, jar, savings account, and savings product language may overlap.
- Partner contribution visibility can create fairness and blame concerns.
- Manual progress maintenance may decay over time.
- Completion semantics are ambiguous in real life.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.83

Reason:

The core domain is strongly validated by realistic household behavior and Phase 1 discovery. Confidence is reduced by unvalidated Vietnamese terminology, manual maintenance tolerance, partner dynamics, and the risk that progress is misunderstood as real money.

## Validation Completeness

Completeness: Medium-high.

Validated from:

- Phase 1 Goals discovery.
- Existing household-reality validation patterns.
- Current product artifacts and Plan domain boundaries.
- Realistic Vietnam-first young household behavior.

Still incomplete:

- Direct user interviews.
- Vietnamese-language terminology testing.
- Observed payday and goal-funding behavior.
- Household artifact review.
- Longitudinal manual maintenance evidence.
- Partner trust, blame, and privacy research.

## Readiness For Product Decision Phase

Readiness: Conditionally ready.

The domain is ready for Product Decision discussion at the principle and boundary level. Before final decisions, the team needs validation of terminology, contribution meaning, funded-progress interpretation, goal-versus-jar understanding, goal-versus-savings understanding, partner dynamics, and manual-maintenance tolerance.
