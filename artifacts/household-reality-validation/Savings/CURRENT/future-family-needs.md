# Future Family Needs

## Years 1-2: Stability

Needs:

- Salary-day discipline.
- Small emergency fund.
- Rent deposit safety.
- Wedding-cost recovery.
- Shared visibility.

Savings behavior: Short terms, flexible savings, manual tracking, occasional missed months.

## Years 3-4: Family Formation

Needs:

- Pregnancy and birth reserve.
- Medical buffer.
- Childcare cushion.
- Household appliance replacement.

Savings behavior: More liquidity, fewer long lock-ups, emergency withdrawal becomes more plausible.

## Years 5-6: Housing and Debt

Needs:

- Apartment down payment.
- Mortgage safety buffer.
- Interest-rate shock protection.
- Debt versus savings trade-off.

Savings behavior: Larger balances, laddered maturities, more partner decisions.

## Years 7-8: Education and Parent Support

Needs:

- Education reserve.
- Parent healthcare support.
- Family remittance planning.
- Stronger emergency fund.

Savings behavior: Multiple purposes and providers; more fragmentation risk.

## Years 9-10: Wealth Boundary

Needs:

- Separate emergency savings from long-term investment.
- Insurance and provider risk awareness.
- Retirement conversations.

Savings behavior: Savings remains safety/liquidity; investment belongs elsewhere.
