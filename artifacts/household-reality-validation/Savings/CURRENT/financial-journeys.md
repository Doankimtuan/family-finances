# Financial Journeys

## Journey A: Marriage to First Child

Marriage

Savings role: Rebuild cash after wedding costs.

First rental home

Savings role: Deposit, furniture, emergency cash.

Dual-income stability

Savings role: Monthly salary sweep into liquid or short-term product.

Pregnancy

Savings role: Medical reserve; avoid long lock-up.

Birth and childcare

Savings role: Buffer when income drops or expenses spike.

Education reserve begins

Savings role: Small recurring savings, usually not yet investment.

## Journey B: Renting to First Apartment

Renting

Savings role: Emergency reserve and down-payment seed.

Career growth

Savings role: Bonuses converted into fixed deposits.

Apartment search

Savings role: Laddered maturities around expected booking/down-payment dates.

Mortgage approval

Savings role: Proof of discipline and cash reserve.

Move-in

Savings role: Furniture and repair buffer; some savings depleted.

Mortgage life

Savings role: Installment safety reserve and prepayment option.

## Journey C: Irregular Income Family

Freelance income begins

Savings role: Smooth uneven cash flow.

Large project payment

Savings role: Split into tax, household buffer, and short-term savings.

Slow month

Savings role: Controlled withdrawal instead of panic borrowing.

Family emergency

Savings role: Early withdrawal may be rational despite lost interest.

Income stabilizes

Savings role: Longer terms become possible.

## Journey D: 10-Year Growth Path

Year 1: Couple stabilizes shared money routines.

Savings role: Emergency fund and salary discipline.

Year 2-3: Household takes on larger goals.

Savings role: Motorbike, wedding recovery, apartment deposit, baby reserve.

Year 4-5: Child or housing costs arrive.

Savings role: Liquidity buffer; long lock-up becomes riskier.

Year 6-7: Income improves.

Savings role: More product diversity and provider concentration risk.

Year 8-10: Wealth boundary emerges.

Savings role: Short-term safety; longer-term wealth may move to Investment domain. Savings must not become investment.
