# Decision Points

## Should We Save This Month?

Inputs: Salary received, bills due, debt due, family obligations, emergency buffer.

Trade-offs: Discipline versus liquidity.

Consequences: Stronger reserve or potential cash squeeze.

Who decides: Usually the more finance-active partner, ideally both.

Frequency: Monthly.

## How Much Should We Lock?

Inputs: Upcoming expenses, runway, income stability, term length, penalty.

Trade-offs: Higher yield versus access.

Consequences: Too much lock-up can force early withdrawal.

Who decides: Both for meaningful amounts.

Frequency: Monthly or when funding savings.

## Which Term Should We Choose?

Inputs: Known payment dates, interest rate, emergency fund level, family plans.

Trade-offs: Longer term rate versus flexibility.

Consequences: Better yield or worse liquidity.

Who decides: Finance-active partner, with partner approval for large sums.

Frequency: Every new deposit or renewal.

## Should We Renew?

Inputs: Rate, current liquidity, goals, upcoming expenses, alternative providers.

Trade-offs: Convenience versus reassessment.

Consequences: Good continuation or passive mistake.

Who decides: Usually both if shared money.

Frequency: At maturity.

## Should We Switch Provider?

Inputs: Rate difference, trust, deposit insurance exposure, convenience, app reliability.

Trade-offs: Yield versus friction and trust.

Consequences: Higher return or operational complexity.

Who decides: Finance-active partner researches; both approve.

Frequency: At maturity or when rates change.

## Should We Withdraw Early?

Inputs: Emergency severity, penalty, other cash, available borrowing, emotional/family pressure.

Trade-offs: Immediate relief versus lost interest and broken discipline.

Consequences: Solves urgent need but weakens future buffer.

Who decides: Both when possible; sometimes one partner acts under urgency.

Frequency: Rare but high impact.

## Should We Stop Saving Temporarily?

Inputs: Income reduction, new child, debt pressure, mortgage rate.

Trade-offs: Preserve cash flow versus falling behind goals.

Consequences: Avoids default but delays progress.

Who decides: Both.

Frequency: During life transitions.

## Should Savings Fund a Goal or Stay Emergency-Only?

Inputs: Household priorities, emergency fund size, goal deadline, partner comfort.

Trade-offs: Goal progress versus safety.

Consequences: Clarity or conflict.

Who decides: Both.

Frequency: Quarterly or around major events.
