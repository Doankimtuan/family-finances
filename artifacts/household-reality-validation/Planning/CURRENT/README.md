# Planning Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Planning domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage shared financial intention in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a shared place for expected income, planned allocation, goals, recurring obligations, upcoming pressure, and month review without confusing it with real account balances?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Shared finance between partners.
- Single people preparing for shared household finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and families with children.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household behavior.
- Planning awareness.
- Shared decision patterns.
- Manual-work tolerance.
- Emotional trust.
- Cross-domain interactions.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Planning as the household intention domain: jars, goals, recurring expectations, calendar pressure, month review, and the strict separation of planned money from real ledger truth.

Phase 2 validates that discovery against young-household reality. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
