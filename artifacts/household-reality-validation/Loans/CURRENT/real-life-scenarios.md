# Real-Life Scenarios

## Best Case

A newly married couple has a motorbike loan with a clear monthly payment and stable salary.

User expects:

- Know next due date.
- Know remaining amount.
- See progress toward completion.

Successful outcome:

- Payments are made on time.
- No partner is surprised.
- Loan completion is confirmed.

## Typical Case

A young household has one consumer finance installment and one informal family loan.

User expects:

- Track the formal payment reliably.
- Remember the family repayment expectation.
- Understand monthly pressure.

Successful outcome:

- Formal payment is not missed.
- Family repayment remains socially clear.
- Household can discuss the obligation without turning it into a fight.

## Worst Case

The household has multiple overlapping installments, one missed payment, and an upcoming floating-rate increase.

User expects:

- Understand what is overdue.
- Know whether cash this month is enough.
- Avoid further credit-history or relationship damage.

Successful outcome:

- The household knows the real repayment burden.
- The situation is no longer hidden or vague.
- The next required action is understood outside this validation scope.

## Unexpected Case

A lender changes the interest rate or schedule after a promotional period.

User expects:

- Notice that the monthly payment may change.
- Understand why the old plan no longer matches provider reality.
- Preserve trust in the household record.

Successful outcome:

- The household recognizes the change.
- Partner expectations adjust to the new repayment burden.
- The product's records are not mistaken for lender confirmation.

## Emergency Case

A medical expense or family crisis forces the household to borrow quickly from a finance company or relative.

User expects:

- Capture the obligation without perfect documentation.
- Know who must be repaid and when.
- Keep near-term cash flow visible.

Successful outcome:

- Emergency borrowing does not disappear from household awareness.
- The repayment obligation is visible enough to prevent accidental overspending.
- Emotional stress is reduced by clarity.
