# Cross-Domain Impact

Together naturally affects every household-scoped domain because it defines who can participate in the shared financial space.

## Accounts

Interactions:

- Together provides household scope for account visibility.
- Accounts may be legally individual but practically household-relevant.
- Partner sensitivity appears when personal accounts become shared context.

## Transactions

Interactions:

- Transactions rely on household membership for visibility.
- Transaction attribution may affect partner trust.
- Shared transaction review can expose hidden habits or forgotten agreements.

## Planning

Interactions:

- Household policies influence planning assumptions.
- Partner agreement affects whether plans feel legitimate.
- Planning can reveal asymmetric expectations between partners.

## Goals

Interactions:

- Goals often represent shared aspirations.
- Partner buy-in affects commitment.
- Long-term goals make household identity more concrete.

## Inbox

Interactions:

- Inbox decisions are household attention items.
- Together defines who should see or participate in those decisions.
- Policy changes or partner-visible events can affect review behavior.

## Health

Interactions:

- Health reflects household data, not individual worth.
- Together controls who may see household reflection.
- Health interpretation can create emotional sensitivity between partners.

## Categories

Interactions:

- Categories may encode household meaning.
- Partners may disagree about classification.
- Shared vocabulary helps reduce ambiguity.

## Cards, Loans, And Savings

Interactions:

- Obligations may be legally individual but household-relevant.
- Due dates, maturity decisions, and payoff decisions often require partner awareness.
- Hidden liabilities can damage trust.

## Auth And Security

Interactions:

- Auth proves user identity.
- Together proves household membership.
- Security protects the trust boundary.

## External Providers

Interactions:

- Banks and wallets hold individual legal data.
- Together determines whether externally sourced information is part of household visibility.
- Consent and account-holder identity are important real-world constraints.

## Together Itself

Interactions:

- Together must remain the collaboration boundary.
- It should not absorb ledger, planning, inbox, or health responsibilities.
