# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Inbox depends on account context to make items recognizable. Account names, balances, and source containers help users understand where the event came from.

Inbox does not own real balances.

## Transactions

Transactions are a major source of Inbox items. Unclear, unmapped, corrected, refunded, or imported transaction facts naturally become review work.

Inbox does not own transaction truth.

## Cards

Card statements, due reminders, installments, refunds, disputes, and fees can create household attention.

Inbox does not own credit limits, statement cycles, or repayment obligations.

## Savings

Savings maturity, renewal, early withdrawal, rate change, and settlement decisions can surface through Inbox.

Inbox does not own product terms, bank rates, maturity calculations, or real withdrawal execution.

## Loans

Loan payment reminders, installment completion, rate changes, and repayment irregularities can create review needs.

Inbox does not own loan contracts or repayment truth.

## Planning

Inbox decisions may affect jars, goals, recurring obligations, or household intention.

Planning remains the owner of virtual intention. Inbox carries attention and decision state.

## Goals

Goals may be affected by spending, savings maturity, deposits, withdrawals, or unexpected obligations that appear in Inbox.

Inbox does not own goal progress truth.

## Health

Unresolved Inbox volume, staleness, and review habits may inform household financial confidence.

Inbox does not own scoring or interpretation of overall household health.

## Categories

Inbox often asks users to assign household meaning to a transaction.

Categories provide meaning; Inbox does not define the full taxonomy.

## Together

Inbox has strong shared-finance impact. It can expose unclear partner spending, missing context, emergency declarations, and decisions requiring mutual awareness.

Together or equivalent shared-household domains own relationship rules, consent norms, and partner policy decisions.

## Notifications

Notifications may tell users an item exists or changed.

Inbox is not the notification system. It is the queue of unresolved financial attention.
