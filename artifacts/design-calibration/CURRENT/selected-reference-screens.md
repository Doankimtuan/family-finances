# Selected Reference Screens

## Reference 1 - Home Overview

- Route: `/home`
- Purpose: household orientation and next actions.
- Validates: hierarchy, dominant information, summaries, progressive disclosure, quick action placement, Health read-only relationship, bottom navigation relationship.
- Page pattern: OverviewPage.

## Reference 2 - Money Activity Surface

- Routes: `/money`, `/money/transactions`
- Purpose: show real ledger position, money containers, recent activity, and full activity list.
- Validates: transaction rows, amount signs, financial status, list density, search/filter entry, readable long-list structure.
- Page pattern: OverviewPage plus ListPage.

## Reference 3 - Daily Money Capture

- Route: `/money/transactions/new`
- Purpose: capture income or expense quickly without changing IA or business behavior.
- Validates: amount field, direction selection, account/category/jar selection, inline validation, money movement preview, sticky bottom action.
- Page pattern: CreateFlow.

## Selection Notes

No additional surfaces were selected. Health, Plan, Inbox, Together, product details, loans, savings, and settings remain outside this calibration pass.
