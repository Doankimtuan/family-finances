# Cross-Domain Contract

## Accounts

Producer:

- Account context for source recognition.

Consumer:

- Inbox reads context.

Shared responsibility:

- Keep account context recognizable without changing balances.

Expected result:

- Inbox item may display account context; Accounts remains authoritative.

## Transactions

Producer:

- Transaction review needs.

Consumer:

- Transactions may consume valid resolution meaning.

Shared responsibility:

- Inbox resolves attention; Transactions owns ledger facts.

Expected result:

- No transaction fact changes unless Transactions accepts an outcome under its own contract.

## Cards

Producer:

- Card decision-bearing attention.

Consumer:

- Cards may consume acknowledgement or valid outcome.

Shared responsibility:

- Inbox state must not imply repayment.

Expected result:

- Card truth remains owned by Cards.

## Loans

Producer:

- Loan or installment attention.

Consumer:

- Loans may consume valid review outcome.

Shared responsibility:

- Inbox does not own payment or balance truth.

Expected result:

- Loan lifecycle remains in Loans.

## Savings

Producer:

- Maturity, renewal, withdrawal, or rate attention.

Consumer:

- Savings may consume valid decisions.

Shared responsibility:

- Inbox does not renew, withdraw, or settle money by itself.

Expected result:

- Savings owns all savings product effects.

## Planning

Producer:

- Planning review attention.

Consumer:

- Planning may consume valid virtual-intention decisions.

Shared responsibility:

- BR-01 must remain explicit.

Expected result:

- Planning changes, if any, are virtual and owned by Planning.

## Goals

Producer:

- Goal-related attention.

Consumer:

- Goals may read outcome context.

Shared responsibility:

- Inbox does not own goal progress.

Expected result:

- Goal interpretation remains in Goals.

## Inbox

Producer:

- Queue state and outcome.

Consumer:

- Inbox consumes source attention.

Shared responsibility:

- No circular ownership.

Expected result:

- Inbox owns attention only.

## Health

Producer:

- None for mutation.

Consumer:

- Health reads workload/staleness context.

Shared responsibility:

- BR-24.

Expected result:

- Health never writes Inbox.

## Categories

Producer:

- Valid meaning vocabulary.

Consumer:

- Inbox may use category options for decision outcomes.

Shared responsibility:

- Category validity remains outside Inbox.

Expected result:

- Inbox can route category meaning; Categories owns vocabulary.

## Together

Producer:

- Visibility and household policy.

Consumer:

- Together may read shared attention context.

Shared responsibility:

- No partner analytics or surveillance.

Expected result:

- Partner norms remain outside Inbox.
