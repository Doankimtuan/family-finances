Semantic Disambiguation & Silhouette Independence Audit (Group K Batch 11)
cancel vs. close

cancel (07) is enclosed in an optical boundary ring, denoting destructive or abortive modal termination. In contrast, close (08) uses an open diagonal cross without container boundaries for standard window/sheet dismissal.

confirm vs. check

confirm (09) embeds an affirmative tick within a verified circular seal for high-confidence financial authorizations. check (10) is an unencumbered checkmark glyph for lightweight row selection and list reconciliation.

back vs. chevron-left

back (17) incorporates a horizontal stem shaft indicating directional spatial navigation history. chevron-left (19) is an acute shaftless caret tuned strictly for numerical pagination and calendar month stepping.
