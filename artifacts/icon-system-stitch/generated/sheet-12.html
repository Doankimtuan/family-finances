Semantic Disambiguation & Meaning Differentiation Audit (Batch 12)

refresh vs. retry vs. restore

refresh (07) employs balanced dual orbital arcs communicating reciprocal ledger synchronization. retry (08) features a single counter-clockwise loop returning to the baseline for re-executing failed calls. In contrast, restore (Atlas 11) models a pure reversal arrow.

download vs. upload vs. deposit

download (11) & upload (12) isolate external data transport into/out of a standardized three-sided file receptacle tray. In contrast, financial deposit (Atlas 02 #14) directs capital inflow into an enclosed ledger safe basin.

clear vs. close vs. cancel

clear (19) is strictly scaled as a 12px pill token inside an input field to reset query text. close (Atlas 11 #08) is an unencumbered diagonal cross for dismissing modals, and cancel (Atlas 11 #07) is a stroked boundary button.
