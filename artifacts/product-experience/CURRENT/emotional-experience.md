# Emotional Experience

## Assessment

ViNha has the right emotional philosophy: calm, adult, shared, and non-punitive. The experience should reduce anxiety by making facts clear and next steps finite. It must never create guilt, shame, or pressure loops.

## Emotional States

| Moment | Desired Feeling | UX Guidance |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Before salary | Prepared, not judged | Show upcoming pressure and planned commitments |
| After salary | Clear and grounded | Show income as real first, allocation as intention second |
| Emergency | Supported | Allow emergency declaration and defer reflection to Month Ritual |
| Debt | Capable | Show next due and progress; avoid shame language |
| Goal achieved | Proud and steady | Celebrate quietly, then suggest responsible next step |
| Savings maturity | Attentive | Show options and consequences without urgency theater |
| Investment loss | Grounded | Show value freshness and uncertainty; no advice |
| Investment profit | Calm | Show unrealized vs realized distinction |
| Month Close | Closure | Make review finite and reflective |
| Health review | Informed | Explain factors and completeness, not blame |

## Recommendations

### PX-EM-01: Replace judgment with observable facts

Problem: Finance products often imply users failed when spending, debt, or Health worsens.

User Impact: Users avoid the app during stressful moments.

Affected Screens: Health, Month Ritual, Debts/Loans, Cards, Emergency flows.

Frequency: Weekly/Monthly/Event-based.

Business Impact: High retention impact.

Recommended UX: Use fact language: "This month has more pressure from..." instead of "You overspent badly". Show next review step, not moral scoring.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P0.

### PX-EM-02: Use celebration only for discipline-supporting milestones

Problem: Celebration can become gamification.

User Impact: Users optimize for rewards rather than financial clarity.

Affected Screens: Goals, Savings, Debt payoff, Month Ritual, Health.

Frequency: Event-based.

Business Impact: Medium.

Recommended UX: Use restrained motion and plain acknowledgment for goal completion, debt payoff, savings maturity handled, and yearly review completed.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P2.
