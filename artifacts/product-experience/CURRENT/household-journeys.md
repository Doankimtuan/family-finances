# Household Journeys

## Overall Assessment

ViNha naturally supports single users, couples, married families, and families with children when the experience stays household-first and calm. The product is strongest for shared visibility, planning, monthly review, savings maturity, debt payoff, and emergency adaptation.

The product is intentionally not a legal family registry, relationship fairness judge, or money authority. That boundary should be visible in the UX.

## Journey Fit

| Household Scenario | Fit | Notes |
|--------------------|-----|-------|
| Single user | Strong | Together can be quiet; Home/Money/Plan still useful |
| Couple | Strong | Equal daily Money/Plan/Inbox access supports collaboration |
| Married family | Strong | Policies and Month Ritual support shared assumptions |
| Family with children | Strong | Goals and recurring pressure support school, health, and childcare |
| Shared expenses | Strong | Transactions, categories, jars, and Inbox cover review |
| Joint savings | Strong | Savings product plus goal/intention context covers clarity |
| Emergency | Strong | Emergency Declaration bypasses warning and surfaces reflection |
| Vacation planning | Strong | Goal plus jar support is natural |
| Buying a house | Moderate | Goal is strong, investment/loan complexity needs careful disclosure |
| Preparing for childbirth | Strong | Goal, recurring, emergency buffer, and Health factors support it |
| Unexpected unemployment | Moderate | Health can reflect pressure, Plan can adapt, but language must avoid advice |

## Recommendations

### PX-HJ-01: Let single users skip collaboration weight

Problem: Together is essential but can feel irrelevant to single users.

User Impact: A single user may feel the app is not for them.

Affected Screens: Onboarding, Together Members, Home.

Frequency: First session and rare.

Business Impact: Medium activation impact.

Recommended UX: Allow "Just me for now" as a household state while keeping the Together tab available for later invitation.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P1.

### PX-HJ-02: Make emergencies calm, not exceptional theater

Problem: Emergency Declaration bypasses warnings, but emotional UX can still feel alarming.

User Impact: Families under stress may avoid recording emergency spending.

Affected Screens: Plan Jars, Transaction Add, Month Ritual, Home, Health.

Frequency: Event-based.

Business Impact: High trust impact.

Recommended UX: Use calm copy: "Mark as emergency" and "Review this together at month close". Do not add guilt-heavy banners or scary score changes.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P0.

### PX-HJ-03: Support life-event bundles through existing primitives

Problem: Major family moments require several domains but should not become new business features.

User Impact: Users may not know how to use Goals, Recurring, Jars, and Health together.

Affected Screens: Plan Hub, Goals, Recurring, Home.

Frequency: Event-based.

Business Impact: Medium.

Recommended UX: Offer existing starter plan presets for childbirth, vacation, house purchase, and unemployment buffer using Jars, Goals, and Recurring only.

Implementation Cost: Medium.

Priority: P2.
