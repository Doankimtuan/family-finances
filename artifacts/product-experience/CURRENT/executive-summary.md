# Executive Summary

## Verdict

APPROVED WITH UX IMPROVEMENTS

ViNha can become a natural Household Money Operating System for young families without changing the approved business model. The core experience direction is strong: Home answers what matters now, Money owns real movement, Plan owns intention, Inbox owns decisions, Together owns household participation, and Health reflects condition without acting.

The product experience is coherent, but it is not yet self-explanatory enough for first-time and emotionally stressed households. The main UX risk is not financial correctness. The main risk is concept translation: ordinary users may confuse Accounts, Jars, Goals, Savings, and Health unless every screen repeats the same simple mental model in interface structure, labels, and action order.

## What Works

- The five-tab IA is understandable and suitable for mobile daily use.
- Home's "real, intention, next decision" purpose is the right product center.
- Money and Plan separation protects BR-01 while matching how households think.
- Inbox as a typed decision queue can reduce anxiety if it stays selective.
- Together gives shared finance a visible boundary without becoming relationship management.
- Health can reduce anxiety if it remains a calm mirror with source factors and completeness.
- Month Ritual creates the right monthly cadence for long-term discipline.

## Primary UX Risks

| Risk | Impact |
|------|--------|
| Real money vs intention is too abstract | Users may treat jars or goals as bank money. |
| Money Hub may hide product domains behind too many section links | Savings, cards, and loans may feel secondary even when urgent. |
| Inbox may become noisy as more domains emit ReviewItems | Users stop trusting attention items. |
| Health score may feel judgmental or over-authoritative | Users may feel blamed or misled. |
| Month Ritual can become a chore | Long-term retention suffers. |
| Product-domain setup wizards may be too long on mobile | Users defer important setup. |

## Board Recommendation

Proceed with implementation, but add a UX hardening layer before GA:

- First-run home coach with minimal, inline concept framing.
- Screen-level microcopy that names source truth without lectures.
- "What happened / what it means / what to do next" structure for major journeys.
- Inbox value rules and bundling rules.
- Mobile form simplification and progressive disclosure.
- Calm emotional language for debt, emergencies, losses, and Health.
- Consistent terminology across screens.

No recommendation requires changing BR-01, BR-24, business rules, domain ownership, or the approved five-tab navigation.
