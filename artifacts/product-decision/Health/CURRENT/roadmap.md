# Roadmap Classification

This roadmap classifies decisions only. It is not a delivery commitment.

| Capabilities | Why |

| HLT-PD-001, HLT-PD-004, HLT-PD-005, HLT-PD-007, HLT-PD-009, HLT-PD-010, HLT-PD-019, HLT-PD-022 | These protect the core Health mirror: condition, explainability, read-only behavior, visible decisions, plan rhythm, activity rhythm, completeness, and light scenarios. |
