# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Transactions is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear guardrails.

The domain solves a real household problem, maps to familiar bank/wallet history behavior, supports shared finance, and protects ViNha's core distinction between real ledger facts and virtual planning.

## Decision Summary

- The factual transaction backbone is approved.
- Category, refund/correction, cross-domain consumption, transfer, reconciliation, and AI assistance require scope protections.
- Provider import, merchant enrichment, pending states, split categorization, receipts, cash reconciliation, review history, and multi-currency contexts are deferred.
- No discovered transaction capability is permanently rejected, but several unsafe interpretations are disallowed by product guardrails.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat virtual planning movement as a transaction.
- Count transfers as income or expense by default.
- Let Health write transaction facts.
- Let AI or providers override ledger truth autonomously.
- Require heavy reconciliation or categorization before core household value is proven.
