# Approved Features

## TX-PD-001: Record Real Money Movement

Why approved: This is the core of the domain and directly solves the validated household problem of forgotten money movement.

Expected product value: Creates truthful financial memory.

Dependencies: Accounts as real containers.

Scope: Real money events only.

## TX-PD-002: Income and Expense Distinction

Why approved: Young households naturally understand money coming in and money going out.

Expected product value: Prevents direction ambiguity.

Dependencies: Transaction amount and account context.

Scope: Basic cash-flow direction.

## TX-PD-003: Preserve Amount, Currency, Date, Account, Direction

Why approved: These are the minimum facts needed for trust.

Expected product value: Makes activity auditable and explainable.

Dependencies: Account existence and household access.

Scope: Factual transaction anchors.

## TX-PD-004: Chronological Activity

Why approved: Mirrors bank/wallet history and household memory.

Expected product value: Lets users scan what happened recently and over time.

Dependencies: Transaction date and creation evidence.

Scope: Activity history, not analytics ownership.

## TX-PD-005: Link to Real Account/Container

Why approved: Users need to know where money moved.

Expected product value: Protects recorded balances and financial trust.

Dependencies: Accounts domain.

Scope: Real container association only.

## TX-PD-006: Household Note or Description

Why approved: Notes are low-complexity memory cues for ordinary households.

Expected product value: Helps users recognize transactions later.

Dependencies: None beyond transaction record.

Scope: Plain household explanation.

## TX-PD-008: Review Unresolved Activity

Why approved: Phase 2 validated that unclear transactions naturally become later review work.

Expected product value: Reduces pressure to classify everything immediately.

Dependencies: Inbox interaction for review items.

Scope: Unresolved transaction facts.

## TX-PD-009: Search and Filtering

Why approved: Users search when balances feel wrong or memory is incomplete.

Expected product value: Answers practical questions quickly.

Dependencies: Transaction text, category, account, and date facts.

Scope: Transaction retrieval and review support.

## TX-PD-011: Evidence for Account Balance Changes

Why approved: Account balances need transaction evidence to be trusted.

Expected product value: Connects "what we have" to "how we got here."

Dependencies: Accounts and Transactions.

Scope: Explanatory ledger evidence.
