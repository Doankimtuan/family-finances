# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Accounts continue to own cash containers. Investments may reference where holdings are custodied, but investment holdings and market value must not become ordinary account balances.

Brokerage account cash remains a deferred distinction.

## Transactions

Transactions own real cash movement: contributions, withdrawals, dividends, coupons, sale proceeds, fees, and corrections.

Investments own the holding/value meaning behind those movements.

## Cards

Cards may reveal risky behavior if card debt is used to fund investment. Approved decisions do not allow card-funded investment automation or advice.

## Loans

Loans remain obligations. Investment proceeds may interact with repayment only through real cash movement. Borrowing to invest is a risk context, not a recommended behavior.

## Planning

Planning may express intended contributions, but approved decisions do not let investment value become plan capacity by default.

## Goals

Goals may express why the household invests. Investments do not own goal progress or guarantee goal funding.

## Inbox

Inbox may receive review-worthy investment uncertainty in later phases: stale value, missing statement, unclear sale proceeds, partner question, or risk exposure. Inbox does not decide investment strategy.

## Health

Health may read investment exposure only if/when investment data exists. Health must not mutate holdings, values, decisions, or recommendations.

## Categories

Categories may label investment-related transactions. Categories do not own holding truth, performance, or risk interpretation.

## Together

Together governs household membership, visibility, and partner context. Investments introduce higher privacy and trust sensitivity than ordinary accounts because losses and speculative behavior may be emotionally charged.
