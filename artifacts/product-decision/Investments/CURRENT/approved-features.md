# Approved Features

Approved capabilities are product-valid for a future Investments domain. Approval does not imply MVP inclusion or immediate implementation.

## INV-PD-001 Investment Domain Separation

Why approved:

- Strongly validated by Phase 1 and Phase 2.
- Protects users from confusing cash, savings products, plans, and risk-bearing assets.

Expected product value:

- Keeps ViNha financially coherent as Wealth/Investments matures.

Dependencies:

- BR-01 protection.
- Clear terminology in later phases.

Scope:

- Investments owns risk-bearing holding truth, not cash containers, transaction facts, savings-product contracts, goals, or plans.

## INV-PD-003 Holding Identification

Why approved:

- Users need to know what asset is actually owned.

Expected product value:

- Enables basic household understanding of exposure.

Dependencies:

- Asset-class classification.
- Ownership/visibility scope.

Scope:

- Name and identity of the investment holding at household-understandable level.

## INV-PD-006 Contribution Amount

Why approved:

- Phase 2 shows users remember and discuss "how much we put in" first.

Expected product value:

- Supports trust, loss discussion, and household memory.

Dependencies:

- Transaction interaction for real cash movement.

Scope:

- Amount contributed or paid into investment exposure.

## INV-PD-009 Valuation Date

Why approved:

- Stale values are a core household misunderstanding risk.

Expected product value:

- Reduces false confidence in old or manual values.

Dependencies:

- Estimated value and valuation source.

Scope:

- Date/time or period when value was known.

## INV-PD-012 Realized Gain/Loss

Why approved:

- Once an investment exits, the household needs factual outcome memory.

Expected product value:

- Separates actual proceeds from past expectations.

Dependencies:

- Contribution amount.
- Sale/redemption/exit cash movement.

Scope:

- Household-level realized outcome after sale, redemption, maturity, or write-off.

## INV-PD-014 Cash Movement Distinction

Why approved:

- Essential to avoid double counting and BR-01 violations.

Expected product value:

- Keeps market value changes separate from actual money movement.

Dependencies:

- Transactions owns cash movement truth.
- Accounts owns cash containers.

Scope:

- Product-level distinction between transfer, buy/sell, income, fee, and value change.

## INV-PD-017 Historical Preservation After Exit

Why approved:

- Households need to understand past investments after sale, closure, or write-off.

Expected product value:

- Preserves trust and audit memory.

Dependencies:

- Holding identification.
- Exit interpretation.

Scope:

- Historical investment facts remain understandable after active ownership ends.
