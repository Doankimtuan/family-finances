# Roadmap Classification

This classification records product-decision timing only. It does not create a roadmap commitment.

## MVP

None.

Reason:

- Product Definition currently places Wealth assets in Future scope.
- Phase 2 concluded the domain is conceptually valid but not fully ready for Product Decision implementation without more validation.

## Version 1.x

None.

Reason:

- Core ViNha scope already centers Money, Plan, Inbox, Health, and Together.
- Investments would add terminology, risk, and valuation complexity before the current product needs it.

## Version 2.x

Possible candidates after research:

- INV-PD-001 Investment domain separation.
- INV-PD-003 Holding identification.
- INV-PD-006 Contribution amount.
- INV-PD-008 Estimated current value with modifications.
- INV-PD-009 Valuation date.
- INV-PD-010 Valuation source with modifications.
- INV-PD-012 Realized gain/loss.
- INV-PD-014 Cash movement distinction.
- INV-PD-015 Liquidity distinction with modifications.
- INV-PD-017 Historical preservation after exit.

Reason:

- These form a simple, household-first Investments foundation if user research validates terminology, asset priorities, and manual tracking tolerance.

## Future

Future candidates:

- Provider refresh/import.
- Portfolio allocation.
- Fee and tax detail.
- Corporate actions.
- Foreign currency.
- Net-worth contribution.
- Diversification visibility.
- Long-horizon portfolio framing.
- Private/family asset depth.
- Health read-only investment signals.

Reason:

- These require product maturity, provider research, governance, or more advanced household financial behavior.

## Never

Rejected permanently under current philosophy:

- Investment recommendations.
- Automated trading or rebalancing.
- Treating unrealized value as spendable plan capacity.
- Health write-back to investment data.
- Gamified trading or market-timing prompts.
- Detailed tax optimization.
- Crypto trading depth.

Reason:

- They violate financial safety, household control, BR-01, BR-24, or simple-first maintainability.
