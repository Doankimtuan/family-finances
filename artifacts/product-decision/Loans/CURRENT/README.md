# Loans Product Decision Board

## Overview

This board records Product Decisions for the Loans domain in ViNha, based on Phase 1 Domain Discovery and Phase 2 Household Reality Validation.

Loans are treated as household borrowing obligations that affect future cash flow, partner trust, and long-term financial safety.

## Decision Process

Each discovered and validated capability was reviewed by the Product Decision Board lenses:

- Principal Product Manager: product value and scope discipline.
- Household Finance Expert: real household usefulness.
- Senior UX Strategist: comprehension, emotional load, and manual-work tolerance.
- Principal Software Architect: maintainability and domain boundaries.
- Financial Risk Consultant: accuracy, risk, and user misunderstanding.

## Decision Criteria

Capabilities were evaluated against:

- Simple before powerful.
- Household-first value.
- Real Ledger is not Virtual Planning (BR-01).
- Health is read-only (BR-24).
- No unnecessary automation.
- User always understands where money is.
- Financial safety over convenience.
- Maintainability over 5-10 years.

Decision labels:

- APPROVED: suitable for product scope.
- APPROVED WITH MODIFICATIONS: suitable only with scope limits.
- DEFERRED: real but not now.
- REJECTED: conflicts with philosophy, safety, value, or maintainability.
