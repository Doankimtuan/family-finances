# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Accounts is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with targeted cautions.

The domain solves a real household problem, maps to natural user behavior, and protects ViNha's core principle that real money and virtual planning are different.

## Decision Summary

- The core Accounts reality layer is approved.
- Several capabilities are approved with scope protections around terminology, credit, liquidity, reconciliation, ownership, and transfer neutrality.
- Provider integrations, multi-currency, advanced reconciliation, detailed balance concepts, and wealth-adjacent capabilities are deferred.
- Capabilities that blur Accounts with Jars, Health, analytics, or automation are rejected.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.82

Reason:

Core decisions are strongly supported by Phase 1 and Phase 2. Confidence is reduced by unresolved Vietnam-first terminology, credit-card mental model, savings-product language, e-wallet tracking tolerance, and personal/shared account sensitivity.

## Readiness for Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Map jars to accounts.
- Count credit limits as owned money.
- Let Health mutate account data.
- Introduce provider automation before validation.
- Expand account taxonomy beyond simple household comprehension.
