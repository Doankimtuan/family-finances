# Rejected Features

## IB-PD-027 Partner Coordination Analytics

Why rejected:

- Measuring partner contribution or review behavior creates surveillance and blame risk.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Conflicts with household-first trust and calm financial management.

Complexity:

- High behavioral and privacy complexity.

Low value:

- Does not directly improve financial truth or household decision quality.

Financial risk:

- May discourage honest shared-finance participation.

Maintenance cost:

- High due to sensitive interpretation, permissions, and edge cases.

## IB-PD-029 General Notification-Center Behavior

Why rejected:

- Inbox must remain a decision queue, not a feed for alerts, marketing, or awareness-only messages.

Conflict with product philosophy:

- Violates simple before powerful and creates unnecessary noise.

Complexity:

- Medium technical scope but high product-boundary cost.

Low value:

- Generic notifications do not reliably improve household financial decisions.

Financial risk:

- Important decisions may be buried among low-value messages.

Maintenance cost:

- High long-term taxonomy and filtering burden.
