# Modified Features

## IB-PD-006 Dismissal Or Acknowledgement

Original idea:

- Support dismissal or acknowledgement when no further action is needed.

Required modification:

- Dismissal, acknowledgement, resolution, expiration, and archive must remain visibly distinct business meanings.

Reason:

- Phase 2 identified misunderstanding risk around "seen", "done", and "financially complete."

Expected result:

- Users can exit active attention without believing money moved or an obligation disappeared.

## IB-PD-007 Decision History

Original idea:

- Preserve history of resolved review items.

Required modification:

- Keep history focused on decision outcome and attribution; do not turn it into chat, blame, or heavy audit tooling.

Reason:

- Young households need memory and trust, not administrative overhead.

Expected result:

- Useful accountability with low emotional weight.

## IB-PD-009 Context For Owning Domain

Original idea:

- Carry enough context for the owning domain to act.

Required modification:

- Inbox may carry context and outcome, but execution and financial truth remain with the owning domain.

Reason:

- Protects BR-01 and maintainable domain boundaries.

Expected result:

- Inbox stays a bridge, not an executor.

## IB-PD-011 Defer An Item

Original idea:

- Allow items to be postponed.

Required modification:

- Deferral must represent intentional unresolved attention and must not become silent hiding.

Reason:

- Phase 2 validates missing-context behavior but warns against backlog avoidance.

Expected result:

- Households can wait for context without losing visibility.

## IB-PD-013 Suggested Resolution

Original idea:

- Suggest likely resolution based on prior behavior.

Required modification:

- Suggestions must be explainable, optional, and subordinate to user decision.

Reason:

- Financial safety is more important than convenience.

Expected result:

- Lower manual effort without hiding judgment.

## IB-PD-016 Staleness Tracking

Original idea:

- Track items that age.

Required modification:

- Staleness must be calm context, not punishment, score, or shame.

Reason:

- Phase 2 identifies backlog anxiety as a major emotional risk.

Expected result:

- Users understand urgency without avoidance pressure.

## IB-PD-017 Expire Time-Bound Reminders

Original idea:

- Expire reminders after they are no longer active.

Required modification:

- Expiration must apply only to time-bound Inbox attention and must not imply the underlying bill or obligation was paid.

Reason:

- Payment reminders risk blurring Inbox with notification and liability domains.

Expected result:

- Active queue stays clean without corrupting financial meaning.

## IB-PD-021 Pattern-Based Auto-Resolution

Original idea:

- Resolve repeated items automatically based on accepted patterns.

Required modification:

- Must be constrained to low-risk repeated review decisions, explainable, historically visible, and never move real money.

Reason:

- No unnecessary automation; users must understand where money is.

Expected result:

- Reduced repeated manual work without autonomous financial action.

## IB-PD-026 Review Workload Metrics

Original idea:

- Measure queue size, item age, or processing burden.

Required modification:

- Metrics must be operational context only. Health may read them, but Inbox does not score the household.

Reason:

- Protects BR-24 and avoids shame-based UX.

Expected result:

- Families can understand queue burden without turning Inbox into judgment.
