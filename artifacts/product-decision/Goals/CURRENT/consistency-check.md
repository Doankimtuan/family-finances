# Consistency Check

## No Conflicts

- Approved Goals capabilities preserve the Phase 1 definition: Goals are named future intentions, not money containers.
- Modified capabilities explicitly protect BR-01.
- Deferred capabilities are not needed for the simple goal core.
- No discovered capability is left undecided.

## No Duplicated Capabilities

- Goals own purpose, target, progress interpretation, status, and simple context.
- Accounts own balances.
- Transactions own real money movement.
- Savings owns savings product truth.
- Planning owns broader allocation and review.
- Health remains read-only.

## No Contradictory Decisions

- Goal progress is approved only as intention progress.
- Evidence comparison is approved only as read-only factual context.
- Savings association is approved only as context.
- Goal-jar association is deferred because it risks confusing two intention concepts.
- AI explanation is deferred because the required safeguards are not yet mature.

## No BR Violations

- BR-01 is protected by rejecting any interpretation of goals as real balances.
- BR-24 is protected by keeping Health read-only.
- AI non-invention is protected by deferring AI explanations.
- No unnecessary automation is protected by deferring recurring contributions, provider matching, forecasting, confidence, and AI.

## No Architecture Violations

- No decision requires Goals to mutate Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Savings, Inbox, Health, Categories, or Together.
- Cross-domain facts remain source-owned.
- Product decisions do not define architecture, database, API, or implementation.

## Open Cautions

- Vietnamese terminology remains unvalidated.
- Partner visibility can create blame.
- Manual progress can become stale.
- Completion semantics need careful future treatment.
