# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Goals is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear guardrails.

The domain solves a validated young-household problem: future priorities are easy to lose inside daily spending. Goals give households a shared intention layer for future outcomes without owning real money truth.

## Decision Summary

- The simple Goals core is approved: named purpose, target amount, lifecycle state, intention-versus-balance distinction, purpose explanation, and lightweight notes.
- Progress, contributions, timing, visibility, completion, savings association, account/transaction evidence, deadline pressure, partial/overfunded progress, and evidence comparison are approved only with strict modifications.
- Goal-jar association, partner-specific contribution context, priority, recurring contributions, non-cash support, trade-off analysis, life-event modeling, education, home deposit, forecasting, provider matching, inflation review, negotiation history, confidence, and AI explanations are deferred.
- No discovered Goals capability is permanently rejected at this stage.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The core decisions are strongly supported by Phase 1 discovery and Phase 2 household validation. Confidence is reduced by Vietnamese terminology risk, manual progress maintenance, partner trust dynamics, and the difficulty of keeping progress separate from real money in users' minds.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat goal progress as account, wallet, cash, investment, or savings product balance.
- Treat goal contributions as real transfers unless Transactions owns the movement.
- Treat completed goals as proof of payment or purchase.
- Let Goals own Savings product truth.
- Let Goals mutate source-domain facts.
- Let Health mutate Goals.
- Introduce automation, forecasting, confidence scoring, or AI explanations before user comprehension and safety are validated.
