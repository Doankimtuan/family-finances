# Final Verdict

Savings is approved as a core ViNha domain capability with strict scope control.

## Product Decision

APPROVED WITH DOMAIN GUARDRAILS.

Savings should exist because it matches real Vietnamese household behavior and strengthens ViNha's Household Money OS identity. It should not become a bank product marketplace, investment surface, or automation engine.

## MVP Verdict

MVP should include only the high-frequency, high-correctness core:

- Real savings product tracking.
- Funding and settlement accounts.
- Principal, provider, term, rate, maturity.
- Expected and posted interest separation.
- Maturity Inbox decision.
- Renewal review.
- Full withdrawal.
- Early withdrawal preview and confirmation.

## v1 Verdict

v1 should harden the household and accounting reality:

- Legal depositor.
- Purpose reference.
- Partner decision memory.
- Pending/failed states.
- Provider-confirmed outcomes.
- Package switching.
- Settlement mismatch review.

## Future Verdict

Future releases may add advanced resilience:

- Deposit insurance exposure.
- Liquidity ladder.
- Multi-currency.
- Legal exception states.
- Provider statement evidence.

## Never Build in Savings

- Provider marketplace.
- Investment advice.
- Business accounting.
- Automatic recurring saving transfers.

Final board assessment: the Savings domain is directionally correct and strategically valuable, but its long-term quality depends on disciplined subtraction. Build the household safety product, not the banking product.
