# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Planning exists because households make money decisions before money moves. A bank balance alone cannot show rent already committed, school fees coming, debt pressure, emergency reserves, family support, or savings goals.

Young households need shared clarity over intention so daily spending does not accidentally consume money meant for important obligations.

## Core Responsibility

Planning's core responsibility is to maintain household intention:

- Expected income.
- Purpose-based allocation.
- Jars and envelopes.
- Goals.
- Recurring expectations.
- Expected due pressure.
- Period review.
- Planning correction.

Planning must keep intention understandable and separate from real money facts.

## Financial Principle

The governing financial principle is BR-01: Real Ledger is not Virtual Planning.

Planning may describe what money is meant to do. It may not claim that money exists, moved, cleared, settled, matured, or was paid unless the owning real-money domain provides that fact.

## Business Outcome

Successful Planning gives the household:

- Confidence about committed money.
- Shared agreement about priorities.
- Visibility into upcoming pressure.
- A safe way to adjust after mistakes or emergencies.
- Long-term continuity as income, family, debt, goals, and accounts become more complex.

The outcome is better household understanding, not automatic financial execution.
