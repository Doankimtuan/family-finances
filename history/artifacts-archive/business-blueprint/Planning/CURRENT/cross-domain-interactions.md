# Cross-Domain Interactions

## Accounts

Producer: Accounts.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Accounts own real containers and recorded position.

Responsibilities:

- Accounts provide where money is.
- Planning uses account context to understand available reality.
- Planning must never create accounts or treat jars as accounts.

## Transactions

Producer: Transactions.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Transactions own factual money movement.

Responsibilities:

- Transactions provide actual income, expense, transfer, refund, and correction facts.
- Planning compares intention against transaction evidence.
- Planning cannot create paid status without transaction truth.

## Cards

Producer: Cards.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Cards own credit limit, statement, outstanding obligation, and due truth.

Responsibilities:

- Planning may show repayment pressure.
- Planning may hold payoff intention.
- Planning cannot own card balance or payment status.

## Loans

Producer: Loans.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Loans own contract, repayment schedule, rate, payoff, and obligation truth.

Responsibilities:

- Planning may show expected repayment pressure.
- Planning may hold payoff preparation intention.
- Planning cannot own loan balance or contract terms.

## Savings

Producer: Savings.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Savings owns product balance, rate, tenor, maturity, renewal, withdrawal, and settlement truth.

Responsibilities:

- Planning may hold savings goal intention.
- Planning may read maturity or product context.
- Planning cannot own savings product truth.

## Planning

Producer: Planning.

Consumer: Home, Health, Inbox, household members.

Ownership: Planning owns intention, allocation, review, and correction.

Responsibilities:

- Planning provides plan pulse and household intent.
- Planning keeps virtual state understandable.
- Planning does not own real money truth.

## Goals

Producer: Planning.

Consumer: Household, Home, Health.

Ownership: Goals are Planning intention unless another domain owns real product facts.

Responsibilities:

- Goals express target purpose.
- Goal progress remains planning interpretation.
- Goal is not a savings account.

## Inbox

Producer: Planning or adjacent domains.

Consumer: Household.

Ownership: Inbox owns discrete decision work.

Responsibilities:

- Inbox may carry planning clarification or review decisions.
- Inbox resolution may clarify Planning.
- Inbox does not own Planning state or real money movement.

## Health

Producer: Planning and source facts.

Consumer: Health.

Ownership: Health owns read-only interpretation.

Responsibilities:

- Health may interpret Planning in context.
- Health cannot mutate Planning.
- Health cannot provide advisory-grade planning.

## Categories

Producer: Transactions or household meaning.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Categories own meaning labels, not money movement.

Responsibilities:

- Categories may inform spending purpose.
- Planning may compare category evidence with intention.
- Categories are not jars by default.

## Together

Producer: Together.

Consumer: Planning.

Ownership: Together owns membership, partner rights, and shared policy.

Responsibilities:

- Together determines who may view or change household Planning.
- Planning changes may depend on shared policy.
- Planning must not bypass partner-visible assumption rules.
