# Final Verdict

## Business Completeness

Completeness: High.

The blueprint covers the complete approved Inbox business behavior: creation, review, resolution, acknowledgement, dismissal, deferral, expiration, archival, suggestion, constrained auto-resolution, recovery, money-flow boundaries, cross-domain interactions, and exceptional situations.

## Business Consistency

Consistency: High.

The domain has one stable responsibility: unresolved financial attention. Every flow preserves that responsibility and avoids taking ownership from Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Savings, Planning, Goals, Health, Categories, or Together.

## Risk Summary

Primary risks:

- Users may confuse acknowledgement, resolution, dismissal, and expiration.
- Payment reminders may drift toward notification-center behavior.
- Auto-resolution may reduce trust if not explainable.
- Backlog can create anxiety.
- Cross-domain outcomes can blur ownership if business boundaries are not respected.

Risk posture:

- Acceptable for Business Blueprint.
- Requires careful Implementation Contract discipline.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.86

Reason:

The business behavior is strongly traceable to Phase 1 discovery, Phase 2 validation, and Phase 3 product decisions. Confidence is reduced by unresolved terminology, partner privacy expectations, and future provider/evidence complexity.

## Readiness For Implementation Contract

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Implementation Contract work may proceed if it preserves:

- Inbox as decision queue only.
- Source-domain ownership.
- BR-01 real-versus-virtual separation.
- BR-24 Health read-only behavior.
- No generic notification-center behavior.
- No partner performance analytics.
- No autonomous real-money movement.
