# Cross-Domain Interactions

## Accounts

Producer:

- Together produces household scope and membership validity.

Consumer:

- Accounts consumes household scope.

Ownership:

- Accounts owns account identity, balance context, and real-position eligibility.
- Together owns who may see household account context.

## Transactions

Producer:

- Together produces household scope.

Consumer:

- Transactions consumes household scope for money events.

Ownership:

- Transactions owns money movement truth.
- Together owns member access context.

## Cards

Producer:

- Together produces membership and household policy context where relevant.

Consumer:

- Cards consumes household scope.

Ownership:

- Cards owns card obligations, billing, limits, and payment status.
- Together does not own card authority or legal holder facts.

## Loans

Producer:

- Together produces household visibility boundary.

Consumer:

- Loans consumes household scope.

Ownership:

- Loans owns debt terms, repayment status, and completion.
- Together does not adjudicate whether a personal loan is fair.

## Savings

Producer:

- Together produces household scope and policy visibility.

Consumer:

- Savings consumes household scope for household-visible savings products.

Ownership:

- Savings owns maturity, renewal, withdrawal, and settlement behavior.
- Together does not decide savings actions.

## Planning

Producer:

- Together produces current household policies.

Consumer:

- Planning consumes policies such as overspend behavior, income placement default, and month ritual mode.

Ownership:

- Planning owns jars, allocations, recurring patterns, and month behavior.
- Together owns policy state and partner-visible policy context.

## Goals

Producer:

- Together produces household scope.

Consumer:

- Goals consumes household scope.

Ownership:

- Goals owns aspiration target, progress, and related planning meaning.
- Together does not own goal priority or funding.

## Inbox

Producer:

- Together may produce material policy-change context.

Consumer:

- Inbox may consume household scope and partner-visible context.

Ownership:

- Inbox owns attention item lifecycle and resolution.
- Together owns whether members belong and whether policy context is material.

## Health

Producer:

- Together produces household scope.

Consumer:

- Health consumes household scope for read-only reflection.

Ownership:

- Health owns read-only interpretation.
- Together does not calculate health or permit Health write-back.

## Categories

Producer:

- Together provides household scope only.

Consumer:

- Categories consumes household scope where classification is household-specific.

Ownership:

- Categories owns vocabulary, mappings, and classification behavior.
- Together does not own categories.

## Together

Producer and consumer:

- Together produces and consumes its own household, membership, invitation, policy, preference, and historical context facts.

Ownership:

- Together owns collaboration context only.
