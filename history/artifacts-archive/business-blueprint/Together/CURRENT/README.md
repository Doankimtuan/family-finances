# Together Business Blueprint

## Overview

This blueprint defines deterministic business behavior for the Together domain in ViNha.

Together is the household trust boundary. It answers one business question: who is included in shared household finance, and which shared assumptions govern household behavior?

## Scope

In scope:

- Household identity.
- Active household membership.
- Partner/Admin responsibility distinction.
- Partner participation.
- Invitation lifecycle.
- Household policy state.
- Material policy-change attribution.
- Household scope for other domains.
- Household data protection from non-members.
- Active household status.
- Household preferences.
- Member recognition identity.
- Limited recent policy events.
- Limited historical and inactive-member interpretation.
- Simple household meaning clarification.

Out of scope:

- Multi-household membership.
- Complex family structures.
- Temporary access.
- Separation or household split workflows.
- External provider consent records.
- Provider consent renewal.
- Cross-border household complexity.
- Partner engagement summaries.
- Granular custom roles or permissions.
- Legal family or marriage registry.
- Relationship dispute resolution.
- Together-initiated money movement.

## Business Responsibility

Together owns household collaboration context. It does not own real money, virtual planning amounts, transaction truth, health interpretation, external account consent, legal relationship status, or relationship fairness.

## Relationship With Previous Phases

- Phase 1 discovered Together as household membership, trust, visibility, roles, policy awareness, and consent.
- Phase 2 validated that young Vietnamese households need a clear shared financial boundary.
- Phase 3 approved a simple Together scope and rejected enterprise, legal, surveillance, and money-movement responsibilities.
- Phase 4 converts approved and modified decisions into a deterministic business contract.
