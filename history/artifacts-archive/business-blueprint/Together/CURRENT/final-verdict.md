# Final Verdict

## Business Completeness

Completeness: High for approved Phase 3 scope.

The blueprint fully defines:

- Household creation and active household state.
- Membership and partner participation.
- Invitation lifecycle.
- Role responsibility.
- Household policy behavior.
- Policy visibility and attribution.
- Household preferences.
- Household scope for other domains.
- Non-member protection.
- Limited historical member interpretation.
- Exceptional and recovery behavior.

Deferred capabilities remain explicitly outside this blueprint.

## Business Consistency

Consistency: High.

Together remains a collaboration boundary and does not absorb ledger, planning, health, legal, relationship, or security-infrastructure responsibilities.

## Risk Summary

Key remaining risks:

- Admin terminology may be misunderstood as ownership.
- Inactive member context is sensitive and must remain limited.
- Policy attribution can become blame-oriented if over-expanded.
- One-household scope may constrain future family-support use cases.
- Separation, safety, deletion, and external consent remain deferred high-risk areas.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.83

Reason:

The approved Together scope is simple, validated, and consistent with ViNha principles. Confidence is reduced by unresolved terminology validation and deferred safety-sensitive lifecycle needs.

## Readiness For Implementation Contract

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Implementation Contract may proceed for the approved and modified Together scope if it preserves these business constraints:

- Together does not move money.
- Partner/Admin remains simple and non-hierarchical.
- Policy attribution remains material and non-surveillance.
- Inactive member context remains historical only.
- Deferred lifecycle, consent, multi-household, and complex family features are not introduced by implementation.
