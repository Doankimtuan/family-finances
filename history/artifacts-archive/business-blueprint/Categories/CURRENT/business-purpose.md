# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

ViNha needs Categories because real money history without meaning is not useful enough for household management.

A transaction can say that 250,000 VND left an account. A category says the household understood that event as groceries, transport, health, family support, salary, or another shared purpose.

## Core Responsibility

Categories provides a stable household vocabulary for classifying transaction meaning.

The domain:

- Defines reusable household labels.
- Applies labels to transaction facts.
- Preserves unknown meaning when needed.
- Supports correction when meaning is wrong.
- Allows read-only category interpretation by other domains.
- Keeps classification separate from money state.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is purpose-based classification.

Classification explains money movement but does not create, move, reserve, allocate, or spend money.

## Business Outcome

The successful business outcome is that a household can understand and discuss where income came from and what expenses were for, while always knowing that categories are labels and not financial containers.

## Business Success Conditions

Categories is successful when:

- Category meaning is understandable to both partners.
- Transactions can be reviewed by purpose.
- Unknown meaning is not guessed.
- Wrong meaning can be corrected.
- Historical meaning remains understandable.
- Other domains can consume category meaning without Categories owning their decisions.
- No user can mistake a category for money location, available balance, or spending limit.
