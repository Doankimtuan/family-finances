# Exceptional Scenarios

## Cancellation

Business expectation:

- Cancellation ends ordinary pursuit.
- Cancellation does not erase prior progress meaning.
- Cancellation does not move money.

## Correction

Business expectation:

- Wrong target, date, note, progress, or status is corrected as goal intention.
- Correction cannot alter Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, or Savings truth.
- Prior valid state remains if correction is invalid.

## Recovery

Business expectation:

- Household can recover from stale progress, mistaken completion, mistaken cancellation, or changed life reality.
- Recovery must be explicit enough that terminal meanings are not silently reversed.
- Recovery does not create real money movement.

## Emergency

Business expectation:

- Emergency may pause or cancel a goal.
- Emergency spending, withdrawal, transfer, or debt use belongs to source domains.
- Goal language must avoid shame.

## Conflict

Business expectation:

- Partner disagreement does not silently change a goal.
- Prior valid state remains until the household resolves meaning.
- Together governs household access and partner context.

## Expired Data

Business expectation:

- Target date may pass without automatic completion or cancellation.
- Stale progress is not treated as factual balance.
- Missing evidence stays uncertain.

## Invalid State

Business expectation:

- Forbidden transition produces Invalid Attempt.
- Prior valid goal state remains.
- Invalid Attempt does not become a persisted business goal.

## Unexpected User Behavior

Examples:

- User treats goal as bank account.
- User treats contribution as transfer.
- User completes goal to imply purchase happened.
- User associates savings product and expects Goals to own maturity.

Business expectation:

- Boundary-breaking action is invalid or must be reframed as intention.
- Source-domain ownership is preserved.

## System Interruption

Business expectation:

- If a goal change cannot be safely completed, prior valid business state remains.
- No partial goal action may imply real money movement.
- Any source-domain evidence remains owned by the source domain.

## Source-Domain Conflict

Business expectation:

- If account, transaction, savings, card, or loan facts conflict with goal progress, source-domain truth wins for real money.
- Goal progress may be corrected as intention.
- Goals must not overwrite source facts.
