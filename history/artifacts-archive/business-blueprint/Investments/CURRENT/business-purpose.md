# Business Purpose

## Why This Business Exists

Investments exists because households sometimes convert cash into assets that may rise or fall in value. Once that happens, the household needs a truthful way to understand the asset without treating it as ordinary cash or a guaranteed savings product.

Young households may not need professional portfolio tooling, but they do need clarity when invested money affects trust, liquidity, long-term planning, or emergency decisions.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility is to describe investment holdings as risk-bearing assets.

Investments answers:

- What investment does the household hold?
- What broad type of asset is it?
- How much was contributed?
- What is its last-known estimated value?
- When and how was that value known?
- Is the value realized or unrealized?
- Can the value realistically become cash?
- Who treats the holding as household-visible, personal, partner-managed, family-held, or uncertain?
- What happened when the investment exited?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is risk-aware wealth tracking.

Investments are not guaranteed cash. Their value can change without money moving. The business must preserve this distinction so users understand where money is and what remains uncertain.

## Business Outcome

Successful Investments behavior means:

- Households understand invested assets without receiving advice.
- Investment value is never confused with bank cash.
- Real cash movement remains explainable through Accounts and Transactions.
- Long-term household memory survives market changes, exits, corrections, and family uncertainty.
- Financial safety outranks convenience, automation, or trading power.
