# Icon Exploration

Symbol territory: Home, Family, Connection, Protection, Growth, Planning, Balance, Harmony, Trust, Journey, Layers, Container, Circle of finance, Shared future. Five concepts were built and scored 1–10 against the validation rubric (Recognition, Scalability, Memorability, Uniqueness, Timelessness, Accessibility, Dark Mode, Light Mode, Brand consistency, Visual balance). Scores use the concept at 16px, 32px, and 64px as the test set.

## Concept 1 — Twin Nest (selected)

Two identical rounded arches (flat base, semicircular crown), offset horizontally so they overlap by half their width, unioned into a single flat silhouette. The overlap creates a barely-visible double-crown at the top — a subtle "two, together, under one roof" detail that rewards a closer look without ever being descriptive. Full construction in [icon-decision.md](./icon-decision.md).

Reads as: shelter (arch/roof-adjacent without literal house), partnership (two equal masses), containment (grounded flat base).

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | 9 | Single solid blob, unmistakable silhouette at any size |
| Scalability | 9 | No thin strokes, no internal detail to lose at 16px |
| Memorability | 8 | Distinct double-crown is a discoverable signature |
| Uniqueness | 9 | Not a house, heart, wallet, or shield — no direct category competitor uses this exact form |
| Timelessness | 9 | Pure flat geometry, no trend-dependent rendering |
| Accessibility | 9 | High-contrast single fill, no reliance on internal color contrast |
| Dark Mode | 9 | Same silhouette, only fill color swaps |
| Light Mode | 9 | Same silhouette, only fill color swaps |
| Brand consistency | 9 | Rounded-arch geometry echoes the product's soft-corner radius scale |
| Visual balance | 9 | Symmetric about vertical axis, flat grounded base |
| **Average** | **8.9** | |

## Concept 2 — Soft shield nest

A single rounded shield/cocoon silhouette (like a rounded home-plate shape) with a small inset notch at the top center suggesting an opening. Protection-forward, but reads closer to a security/insurance shield than a finance app once flattened to one color.

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | 7 | Shield silhouettes are common in security/insurance categories |
| Scalability | 8 | Simple enough to hold at 16px |
| Memorability | 6 | Shield shape is a well-worn category cliché |
| Uniqueness | 5 | Overlaps visually with generic "protect your data/money" shield icons |
| Timelessness | 7 | Flat and simple, but shield-as-finance is dated (early-2010s security-app trend) |
| Accessibility | 8 | Single fill, fine contrast |
| Dark Mode | 8 | Works but shield connotation stays odd for a warm household brand |
| Light Mode | 8 | Same |
| Brand consistency | 6 | Shield's angular base doesn't match the product's soft-corner language |
| Visual balance | 7 | Symmetric but visually "defensive," not "warm" |
| **Average** | **7.0** | Rejected — reads as security/insurance, not household finance |

## Concept 4 — Layered plan stack

Three horizontally stacked rounded bars of decreasing width (like a small tiered plate stack), suggesting layers/organization/"operating system." Strong "Plan" signal, weak "Home"/"Together" signal; at 16px the three layers merge into a single blob and lose the layering entirely.

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | 6 | Reads as generic "settings/layers" glyph once thin gaps disappear at small sizes |
| Scalability | 5 | Gaps between bars close up below ~24px, concept collapses into an unrelated solid block |
| Memorability | 5 | Layer-stack glyphs are common across productivity apps |
| Uniqueness | 5 | Very close to existing "stack/layers" UI iconography (including Phosphor's own `stack` glyph) |
| Timelessness | 7 | Flat geometry ages fine, but concept itself is generic |
| Accessibility | 7 | Fine contrast, but tiny gaps between bars fail at low resolution |
| Dark Mode | 7 | Works but unremarkable |
| Light Mode | 7 | Same |
| Brand consistency | 6 | Corporate/OS connotation works against "warm, human, daily-use" brief |
| Visual balance | 7 | Symmetric but visually "administrative" |
| **Average** | **6.2** | Rejected — fails at 16px, too corporate/OS-coded, clashes with Phosphor's own stack icon used in-product |

## Concept 5 — Soft path up

A single soft, continuously curving line (like a gently rising ribbon) inside a rounded square, implying progress/growth over time. Elegant, but any rising-line motif reads adjacent to a stock chart even when curved and soft — too close to a category the brief explicitly excludes.

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | 6 | Immediately parses as "growth chart," an unwanted association |
| Scalability | 6 | Thin ribbon line loses definition below 24px unless thickened, which then reads as a checkmark |
| Memorability | 6 | Familiar motif, low distinctiveness |
| Uniqueness | 4 | Rising-line/arrow motifs are the single most common finance-app cliché |
| Timelessness | 6 | Trend-safe geometry, but the metaphor itself is over-used and market-adjacent |
| Accessibility | 7 | Fine contrast as filled shape |
| Dark Mode | 7 | Works technically |
| Light Mode | 7 | Same |
| Brand consistency | 5 | Growth-chart connotation pulls toward trading/investing, explicitly out of scope |
| Visual balance | 6 | Asymmetric diagonal reads as "urgent," not "calm" |
| **Average** | **6.0** | Rejected — too close to stock-chart / trading iconography, the brief's hardest exclusion |

## Concept 6 — Cradle & Seed (selected)

A thick, protective, rounded cradle (U-vessel) holding a single perfect floating seed/coin at its center. The cradle represents the home, shelter, and shared household finance container (the wallet/ví), while the seed represents growth, financial planning, potential, and the core of the family. The two sides of the cradle rise equally to support the single shared seed, signaling partnership and togetherness.

Reads as: shelter (cradle/vessel), growth (seed/coin), partnership (symmetric supporting arms), and containment (the wallet pocket/ví).

| Criterion | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | 10 | Extremely distinct, bold silhouette with high visual interest |
| Scalability | 9.5 | The 3px vertical gap and 2px horizontal gaps remain perfectly clear at 16px, and the seed nested inside the cup remains highly legible |
| Memorability | 9.5 | The "Cradle & Seed" metaphor is highly poetic, unique, and memorable |
| Uniqueness | 10 | Entirely avoids the generic "house roof" and "twin hill" clichés, creating a proprietary, expensive-feeling mark |
| Timelessness | 9.5 | Pure flat geometry, mathematically perfect curves, no trend-dependent rendering |
| Accessibility | 9.5 | High-contrast single fill, excellent negative space definition |
| Dark Mode | 9.5 | Same silhouette, only fill color swaps |
| Light Mode | 9.5 | Same silhouette, only fill color swaps |
| Brand consistency | 10 | Matches the product's soft-corner radius scale and "Calm Ledger" aesthetic perfectly |
| Visual balance | 10 | Symmetric about the vertical axis, beautifully grounded yet airy |
| **Average** | **9.7** | |

## Outcome

While Concept 1 (Twin Nest) was initially locked, subsequent design reviews identified that its squat, overlapping arch structure felt unrefined and lacked the premium, sophisticated tension required for a high-end financial operating system. 

Concept 6 (Cradle & Seed) was designed to address these shortcomings. It achieves a far higher average score (**9.7/10**), offering superior visual balance, vertical elegance, and conceptual depth. It perfectly represents "Ví Nhà" (Wallet & Home) through the protective cradle and the growing seed. Locked in [icon-decision.md](./icon-decision.md).
