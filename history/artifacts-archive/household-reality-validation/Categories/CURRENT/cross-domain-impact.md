# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Accounts hold real balances and show where money is. Categories explain what account-linked transactions were for.

Interaction:

- Category summaries depend on transactions from accounts.
- Account truth remains separate from category meaning.

## Transactions

Transactions are the primary domain Categories classify.

Interaction:

- A transaction may have a category.
- Category meaning helps users understand transaction history.
- Corrections, refunds, and unclear transactions can affect category interpretation.

## Planning

Planning interprets future intent and available capacity. Categories provide evidence about actual behavior.

Interaction:

- Categorized transactions can inform planning conversations.
- Category-to-jar mapping can connect actual spending meaning to virtual planning context.
- Categories do not own planned limits or available-to-spend amounts.

## Goals

Goals represent named future outcomes. Categories may show spending or income patterns that affect those outcomes.

Interaction:

- Goal-related expenses may be categorized for later understanding.
- Goal progress remains separate from category totals.

## Inbox

Inbox carries unresolved decisions.

Interaction:

- Uncategorized or unmapped transactions may require review.
- Category uncertainty can become household work.
- Inbox owns review state; Categories own the vocabulary.

## Health

Health interprets financial condition.

Interaction:

- Health may read categorized spending patterns.
- Category data can signal pressure areas such as medical, debt-related spending, family support, or discretionary spending.
- Health conclusions remain outside Categories.

## Cards

Cards may provide merchant category, MCC, reward, billing, refund, and statement context.

Interaction:

- Card merchant-category evidence can inform classification.
- Household category may differ from card-network merchant category.
- Card repayment should not automatically equal the original purchase category.

## Loans

Loans own liability and repayment truth.

Interaction:

- Loan repayment transactions may require category clarity.
- Category should not confuse repayment with the original financed consumption.

## Savings

Savings owns product and goal-linked savings behavior.

Interaction:

- Savings contributions or withdrawals may appear in transaction history.
- Category language must not make savings movement look like ordinary spending.

## Together

Together owns household membership and shared decision context.

Interaction:

- Categories require shared vocabulary between partners.
- Category visibility can support or strain trust.
- Household roles and permissions influence who can classify or change shared meaning.

## Provider Integrations

Providers may supply descriptors, merchant names, MCC, wallet biller types, or suggested categories.

Interaction:

- Provider data can inform but does not own household meaning.
- Imported category evidence can conflict with household interpretation.
