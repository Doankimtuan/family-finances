# Pain Points

## Current Frustrations

- Loan information is scattered across contracts, provider apps, bank statements, SMS, and memory.
- Payment due date and payment posting date may differ.
- Promotional-rate changes can create surprise.
- Early payoff rules are unclear.
- Informal family loans carry emotional ambiguity.
- Small installments can accumulate unnoticed.
- Partner visibility can be incomplete or delayed.

## Confusing Concepts

- Principal versus outstanding balance.
- Interest rate versus total interest.
- Fixed monthly payment versus reducing balance.
- Promotional rate versus full-term fixed rate.
- Interest-free versus fee-free.
- Payment made versus payment received by lender.
- Credit card balance versus scheduled loan.
- Open-ended debt versus scheduled loan.

## Unnecessary Complexity

- Detailed amortization can exceed ordinary users' natural mental model.
- Legal contract terms can be too dense for daily household use.
- Rate formulas may be too technical unless payment impact is obvious.
- Fee taxonomy can overwhelm users when the immediate concern is payment due.

## Missing Visibility

- True lender-confirmed balance may not be visible from household records.
- Fees and penalties may be invisible until charged.
- Partner-held or family-held obligations may not be visible.
- Credit-history impact is often abstract until future borrowing is denied.
- Future payment shock after rate change may be underestimated.

## Potential Misunderstandings

- User may believe the product guarantees lender accuracy.
- User may believe a loan is complete before lender confirmation.
- User may interpret family loan as optional while partner treats it as binding.
- User may undercount total monthly obligation across multiple loans.
- User may treat loan repayment as normal expense and lose sight of liability reduction.

No solutions are proposed in this validation phase.
