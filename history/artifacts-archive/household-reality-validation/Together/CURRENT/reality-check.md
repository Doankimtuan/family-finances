# Reality Check

## Real-World Behavior

Young Vietnamese households commonly:

- Use individual accounts and wallets for shared money.
- Coordinate through chat, screenshots, transfers, and memory.
- Let one partner manage more detail.
- Expect the other partner to remain meaningfully informed.
- Treat family support as part of household reality.
- Need trust and privacy at the same time.
- Make major money decisions together but delegate daily actions.

## Current Domain Understanding

Phase 1 understands Together as:

- Household trust boundary.
- Membership and roles.
- Shared policies.
- Partner-visible material changes.
- Collaboration infrastructure.
- Not a ledger, planner, bank connector, auth system, or relationship dispute solver.

## Already Validated

- The domain solves a real problem: defining who "we" means in household finance.
- Shared visibility is a natural need for partner finance.
- Asymmetric engagement is realistic.
- Policy visibility matters because household agreements are easy to forget.
- Together should mostly act as quiet infrastructure.
- The boundary with ledger and planning domains is behaviorally sound.
- Overly granular permissions would likely be over-engineered for young couples.

## Assumptions Remaining

- Two-person household membership fits most target use cases in the near term.
- "Household" is the right product term for Vietnamese young users.
- "Admin" is understood as responsibility, not ownership.
- Users want policy event visibility often enough to value it.
- Partners are comfortable with broad shared visibility once invited.
- Non-member family influence can remain outside Together's membership model.

## Still Needs Confirmation

- Direct interviews with Vietnamese couples using different contribution models.
- Language testing for Household, Partner, Admin, Policy, and Together.
- Privacy testing around personal accounts, e-wallets, debt, and family support.
- Safety-sensitive research around coercive control, separation, and former access.
- Longitudinal validation of whether policy visibility is used after setup.
- Validation of whether less-engaged partners feel included rather than monitored.

## Validation Judgment

The core domain is behaviorally accurate. The main uncertainty is not whether Together should exist, but how target households interpret membership, consent, roles, and shared visibility.
