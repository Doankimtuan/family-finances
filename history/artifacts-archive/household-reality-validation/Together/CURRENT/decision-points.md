# Decision Points

## Create Household

Why it matters:

- Establishes the shared financial unit.
- Signals that the product is household-first, not only personal tracking.

## Name Household

Why it matters:

- Gives emotional identity to the shared space.
- Helps users recognize whether this is "mine" or "ours."

## Invite Partner

Why it matters:

- Converts private tracking into shared finance.
- Requires trust, timing, and consent.

## Accept Invitation

Why it matters:

- The invitee agrees to enter the household financial boundary.
- It changes what the person may see and participate in.

## Decline Invitation

Why it matters:

- Preserves consent.
- May carry emotional meaning beyond product state.

## Revoke Invitation

Why it matters:

- Corrects wrong recipient, changed mind, or changed relationship context.
- Prevents unwanted future access.

## Review Members

Why it matters:

- Confirms who currently belongs.
- Supports trust in household-scoped data.

## Change Role

Why it matters:

- Alters policy responsibility.
- Can be misunderstood as hierarchy if not handled carefully.

## Edit Household Policy

Why it matters:

- Changes assumptions that other domains depend on.
- Can affect spending, planning, and review expectations.

## Review Policy Events

Why it matters:

- Helps partners understand what changed and when.
- Supports memory during disagreement or stress.

## Edit Household Preferences

Why it matters:

- Locale, timezone, and currency shape interpretation of dates and money.
- Incorrect preferences can confuse household records.

## Leave Or Close Household

Why it matters:

- Changes trust boundary and historical responsibility.
- Often happens in emotionally sensitive situations.

## Recover Access

Why it matters:

- Household finance depends on continuity.
- Lost access can prevent review, trust, or urgent action.

## Reject Shared Visibility

Why it matters:

- Some personal financial information may be too sensitive or unsafe to share.
- Consent is central to the domain's legitimacy.
