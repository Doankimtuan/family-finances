# Final Verdict

## Does This Domain Reflect Real Household Financial Behavior?

Yes.

Together accurately reflects a real household finance need: young partners need a shared trust boundary before shared money, planning, review, or financial health can make sense.

The domain is especially valid for Vietnam-first households because formal joint banking is not the only way partners share money. Many households rely on individual accounts, e-wallets, QR transfers, cash, and informal agreements. Together captures the human boundary around those facts.

## Strengths

- Solves a real problem: who is included in shared finance.
- Uses a natural mental model: "our household."
- Supports asymmetric partner engagement.
- Protects the distinction between collaboration context and financial truth.
- Helps reduce surprise around shared policies.
- Remains relevant as households move through marriage, children, property, and family support.

## Weaknesses

- Role language may be misunderstood as hierarchy.
- Broad shared visibility can feel unsafe or intrusive in some relationships.
- Two-person assumptions may not cover all financially relevant family situations.
- Non-member family influence is real and remains conceptually delicate.
- Exit, separation, and former-access cases are high-risk and under-validated.
- Policy history may be valuable in principle but unused in calm periods.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.80

Reason:

The core domain strongly matches young-household behavior. Confidence is reduced by terminology uncertainty, privacy sensitivity, family-of-origin complexity, and lack of direct field research with Vietnamese couples.

## Validation Completeness

Completeness: Medium.

Validated from:

- Phase 1 Together discovery.
- Existing product and domain artifacts.
- Realistic Vietnam-first household behavior patterns.
- Cross-domain household finance reasoning.

Still incomplete:

- Direct target-user interviews.
- Vietnamese-language comprehension testing.
- Longitudinal observation.
- Safety-sensitive research.
- Validation of partner comfort with shared visibility.

## Readiness For Product Decision Phase

Readiness: Conditionally ready.

The core Together domain is ready for Product Decision discussion. Before final product decisions, the team should validate terminology, role interpretation, shared-visibility comfort, two-person household assumptions, non-member family influence, and sensitive exit scenarios with target households.
