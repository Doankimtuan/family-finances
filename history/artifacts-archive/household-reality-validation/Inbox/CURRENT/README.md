# Inbox Household Reality Validation

## Overview

This board validates whether the Inbox domain matches how young Vietnamese households manage unresolved financial attention in real life.

The core validation question is: do young households naturally need and understand a shared place for financial decisions that have not yet been made?

## Validation Scope

Target users:

- Age 20-35.
- Vietnam first.
- Single people preparing for shared finance.
- Couples, newly married partners, young families, and households with children.
- Shared finance between partners, including partially shared and informally shared money.
- Long-term household financial management.

Validated dimensions:

- Real household decision behavior.
- Review habits and backlog tolerance.
- Manual-work acceptance.
- Emotional load.
- Partner accountability.
- Cross-domain effects.
- Long-term fit over 5-10 years.

Excluded:

- Business redesign.
- UX redesign.
- Architecture, database, API, or implementation detail.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Product decisions.

## Relationship With Phase 1

Phase 1 discovered Inbox as the household's decision queue: unresolved financial attention linked to transactions, reminders, maturities, provider evidence, or internal financial events.

Phase 2 validates that understanding against real young-household behavior. It does not replace Phase 1 and does not modify any Source of Truth. It classifies what appears validated, what needs review, what needs research, and what may become relevant later.
