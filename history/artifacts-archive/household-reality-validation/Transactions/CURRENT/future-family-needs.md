# Future Family Needs

## Marriage

Marriage increases shared obligations and makes transaction visibility more important. Salary, rent, family support, insurance, and joint purchases become recurring discussion points.

## Children

Children increase transaction volume and urgency. School, milk, medicine, childcare, clothes, transport, and emergency healthcare create more complex spending patterns.

## Buying A Home

Home purchase introduces deposits, mortgage payments, renovation costs, furniture, legal fees, family contributions, and long-term obligation tracking.

## Buying A Car Or Motorbike

Vehicle purchase introduces down payments, loans, insurance, repairs, fuel, parking, registration, and maintenance.

## Growing Income

Higher income may increase accounts, card use, subscriptions, investments, travel, lifestyle spending, and expectations for clearer review.

## Multiple Accounts

Households are likely to accumulate more bank accounts, wallets, cards, savings products, and provider relationships over time.

## Family Expansion

Support for parents, siblings, in-laws, children, and extended family may increase transfer ambiguity. Some transfers are gifts, some are loans, some are shared obligations.

## Career Changes

Job change, freelancing, remote work, maternity leave, or business income creates income irregularity and reimbursement complexity.

## Geographic Mobility

Travel, study abroad, migration, remote income, and remittances can introduce foreign currency, transfer fees, and settlement differences.

## Long-Term Stability

The need to know what happened to money does not fade. It becomes more valuable as household life becomes more complex.
