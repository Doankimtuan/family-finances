# Future Family Needs

These needs are likely to appear over 5-10 years. They are observations only.

## Marriage

- Wedding cost preparation.
- Rent deposit or home setup.
- Shared emergency fund.
- Negotiation of personal versus shared goals.

## Children

- Pregnancy and birth expenses.
- Childcare.
- School fees.
- Healthcare buffer.
- Education preparation.
- Larger emergency reserve.

## Buying A Home

- Down payment preparation.
- Furniture and renovation.
- Mortgage affordability pressure.
- Family contribution or support complexity.

## Buying A Vehicle

- Motorbike or car purchase.
- Loan or installment pressure.
- Insurance, maintenance, and registration costs.

## Growing Income

- More capacity for multiple goals.
- Bonus allocation decisions.
- Lifestyle creep risk.
- Greater need to distinguish wants, safety, and long-term stability.

## Multiple Accounts

- Separate personal accounts.
- Shared household account.
- Savings products for specific purposes.
- Wallet balances.
- Cash and informal holdings.

## Family Expansion

- Support for parents.
- Support for siblings.
- Extended-family ceremonies, illness, education, and housing pressure.
- Goals that cross household boundaries.

## Longer Time Horizon

- Education.
- Housing.
- Health protection.
- Career transitions.
- Retirement-adjacent thinking.
- Wealth-building intentions that may exceed the simple Goals domain.
