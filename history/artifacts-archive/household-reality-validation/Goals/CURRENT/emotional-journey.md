# Emotional Journey

## Beginning

Common feelings:

- Hope.
- Motivation.
- Anxiety about affordability.
- Partner excitement.
- Fear of committing too much.

Trust is gained when the goal feels understandable and realistic.

Trust is lost when users feel pushed into formal planning before they are ready.

## Early Progress

Common feelings:

- Confidence.
- Momentum.
- Pride after first contribution.
- Relief that the goal is visible.

Trust is gained when progress matches the user's memory of real effort.

Trust is lost when progress feels abstract or disconnected from money reality.

## Interruption

Common feelings:

- Stress.
- Guilt.
- Frustration.
- Resentment if one partner feels responsible.
- Uncertainty about whether to continue.

Trust is gained when the goal can absorb real-life interruption without judgment.

Trust is lost when the product language implies failure or hides the reason progress changed.

## Review

Common feelings:

- Clarity.
- Anxiety before seeing the gap.
- Relief if the goal is on track.
- Discomfort if the household overestimated progress.

Trust is gained when the domain helps users distinguish intention from reality.

Trust is lost when review exposes a mismatch that users feel should have been obvious earlier.

## Completion

Common feelings:

- Achievement.
- Relief.
- Shared pride.
- Motivation for the next goal.

Trust is gained when completion matches the household's real-world understanding of "done."

Trust is lost if completion only means a number was reached while the actual purchase or payment remains unresolved.

## Cancellation Or Abandonment

Common feelings:

- Regret.
- Shame.
- Practical acceptance.
- Relief if the goal was unrealistic.

Trust is gained when cancellation feels like a valid life decision.

Trust is lost when cancellation feels like erasing effort.
