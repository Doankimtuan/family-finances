# Cross-Domain Analysis

## Savings and Accounts

Why it exists: Accounts hold salary, spending money, and settlement destinations. Savings funding and settlement must move through real accounts.

Missing relationship: Clear pending/failed funding and provider-confirmed settlement relationship.

Unnecessary relationship: Savings should not duplicate generic account balance ownership.

## Savings and Transactions

Why it exists: Funding, interest posting, settlement, and early withdrawal must be ledger-visible.

Missing relationship: Distinguish projected interest from posted transactions.

Unnecessary relationship: Daily savings projections should not create fake transactions.

## Savings and Inbox

Why it exists: Maturity and early withdrawal are real household decisions.

Missing relationship: Emergency early-withdrawal and failed-settlement review should be explicit decision types when needed.

Unnecessary relationship: Generic low-value reminders should not crowd Inbox.

## Savings and Goals

Why it exists: Families save for apartment, baby, education, emergency, travel, and motorbike.

Missing relationship: Purpose reference without violating BR-01.

Unnecessary relationship: A savings account should not equal a goal.

## Savings and Planning

Why it exists: Monthly plans decide whether money can be saved or must remain liquid.

Missing relationship: Saving pause/resume behavior during income shocks.

Unnecessary relationship: Plan must not move real savings principal.

## Savings and Health

Why it exists: Health can read liquidity, maturity concentration, provider concentration, and emergency runway.

Missing relationship: Read-only deposit insurance and lock-up risk insights.

Unnecessary relationship: Health must not trigger settlement or renewal.

## Savings and Together

Why it exists: Savings often represents shared sacrifice, but legal ownership may sit with one partner.

Missing relationship: Partner visibility, legal owner distinction, and decision consent for large withdrawals.

Unnecessary relationship: Together should not override legal depositor reality.

## Savings and Loans/Cards

Why it exists: Families decide between using savings, borrowing, paying off debt, or preserving cash.

Missing relationship: Trade-off visibility for early debt payoff versus preserving emergency fund.

Unnecessary relationship: Savings should not become debt optimization.
