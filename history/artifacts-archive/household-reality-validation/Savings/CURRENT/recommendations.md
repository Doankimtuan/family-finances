# Recommendations

## Keep

- Keep Savings in Real Ledger.
- Keep BR-10 maturity-to-Inbox.
- Keep Health read-only.
- Keep product lifecycle thinking.
- Keep early withdrawal support.
- Keep renewal as decision support, not silent household money movement.

## Clarify in Later Business Design

- Legal depositor versus household relevance.
- Liquid reserve versus locked savings.
- Purpose reference without jar/balance conflation.
- Provider-confirmed settlement truth.
- Pending and failed money movement states.
- Deposit insurance concentration.
- Grace period and passive provider auto-renewal.

## Avoid

- Do not turn Savings into investment advice.
- Do not make provider comparison the core product.
- Do not shame withdrawals.
- Do not over-model tiny savings products.
- Do not let Health prescribe actions.
- Do not treat every reminder as Inbox-worthy.

## Product Strategy Direction

Savings should feel like household safety and future readiness. It should not feel like a bank account admin screen. The product should help a couple answer:

- Are we protected?
- What is locked?
- What matures soon?
- What decision do we need to make?
- What happens if we break this savings?
- Does my partner understand this money the same way I do?
