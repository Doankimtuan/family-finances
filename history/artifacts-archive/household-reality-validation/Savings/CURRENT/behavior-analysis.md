# Behavior Analysis

## Save Immediately After Salary

Frequency: Common among disciplined salaried households.

Reason: Prevents lifestyle spending and preserves goals.

Risk: Over-locking money before bills and family obligations are clear.

Long-term impact: Strong habit if liquidity buffer is maintained.

## Save Irregularly

Frequency: Very common for young families, freelancers, and households with family obligations.

Reason: Income and expenses are uneven.

Risk: Savings becomes inconsistent and hard to plan.

Long-term impact: Still valuable if ViNha supports lump-sum behavior rather than assuming perfect monthly routines.

## Keep Emergency Funds

Frequency: Aspirational and increasingly common, but often underfunded.

Reason: Health, job loss, family support, and housing shocks.

Risk: Emergency fund may be locked in term deposits.

Long-term impact: Core resilience behavior.

## Split Income Into Purposes

Frequency: Common, whether done mentally, with bank accounts, e-wallet pockets, jars, envelopes, or spreadsheets.

Reason: Makes money feel controlled.

Risk: Real money and intention can be confused.

Long-term impact: ViNha must preserve BR-01 while still acknowledging purpose.

## Keep Multiple Savings Accounts

Frequency: Common as income grows and banks offer easy digital deposits.

Reason: Different terms, goals, providers, rates, and mental buckets.

Risk: Fragmentation, forgotten maturity dates, provider concentration.

Long-term impact: Savings maturity calendar becomes more important over time.

## Roll Over Savings

Frequency: Common for term deposits.

Reason: Convenience and habit.

Risk: Passive renewal at poor rate or wrong term.

Long-term impact: Inbox maturity review is highly relevant.

## Withdraw Early

Frequency: Occasional but important.

Reason: Emergency, family support, housing payment, job loss.

Risk: Lost interest and regret.

Long-term impact: Must be supported without shame.

## Borrow From Relatives Instead of Breaking Savings

Frequency: Common in family-centered financial culture.

Reason: Preserve interest or avoid losing disciplined savings.

Risk: Emotional debt, unclear repayment, partner conflict.

Long-term impact: Savings decisions cannot be separated from informal debt and family obligations.

## Ignore Reminders

Frequency: Common when reminders feel like noise or money is stressful.

Reason: Avoidance, uncertainty, low confidence, busy family life.

Risk: Passive renewal or idle money.

Long-term impact: Inbox should prioritize true decisions, not generic notifications.

## Keep Cash or Bank Balance Because It Feels Safer

Frequency: Common among lower-income or single-income households.

Reason: Liquidity and fear of unexpected events.

Risk: Low yield and inflation erosion.

Long-term impact: ViNha should not over-push term products.
