# Real-Life Scenarios

## Salary Received

What happened: One or both partners receive monthly salary.

Decision: Save immediately, wait until bills clear, or leave money liquid.

Money flow: Salary account -> savings product or remains in account.

Domains: Accounts, Savings, Plan, Inbox, Health, Together.

## Bonus Received

What happened: Tet bonus, annual bonus, sales bonus, or project payout arrives.

Decision: Spend, repay debt, support family, fund emergency reserve, or lock into term deposit.

Money flow: Income account -> debt repayment, family transfer, savings product, or jar intention.

Domains: Accounts, Transactions, Savings, Goals, Plan, Together.

## Wedding or Post-Wedding Recovery

What happened: Wedding gifts and wedding costs create irregular inflow/outflow.

Decision: Preserve gift surplus, repay informal debt, or set up new household fund.

Money flow: Cash/bank gifts -> shared account -> savings product or debt repayment.

Domains: Accounts, Savings, Loans, Together, Plan.

## Pregnancy

What happened: Medical expenses and income uncertainty increase.

Decision: Avoid long-term deposits; keep emergency money liquid.

Money flow: Salary -> liquid account/flexible savings; possible early withdrawal from term product.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Health, Plan, Inbox.

## First Child

What happened: Expenses become frequent and less predictable.

Decision: Reduce fixed-term savings; build flexible buffer.

Money flow: Savings may settle into checking for childcare and medical needs.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Plan, Goals, Together.

## Medical Emergency

What happened: Household or parent needs urgent care.

Decision: Withdraw early, borrow from relatives, use credit card, or combine sources.

Money flow: Savings product -> settlement account -> hospital/family transfer.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Inbox, Cards, Loans, Together, Health.

## Buying a Motorbike

What happened: Household needs transport for work or childcare.

Decision: Pay cash from savings, installment loan, or delay purchase.

Money flow: Savings -> checking -> merchant, or checking -> loan down payment.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Loans, Plan, Inbox.

## Buying First Apartment

What happened: Down payment deadline approaches.

Decision: Align maturities, withdraw early if needed, accept family support, or delay.

Money flow: Savings products -> settlement account -> developer/bank.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Loans, Goals, Plan, Together.

## Job Change or Unemployment

What happened: Income pauses or becomes uncertain.

Decision: Stop new savings, preserve cash, break term only if runway is low.

Money flow: Savings may remain untouched or settle into living-expense account.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Plan, Health, Together.

## Family Support Request

What happened: Parents or siblings need money.

Decision: Help now, lend formally/informally, withdraw savings, or set boundary.

Money flow: Savings or salary account -> family transfer.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Loans, Together, Plan.

## Interest Rate Changes

What happened: Bank product rates change before renewal.

Decision: Renew same, switch package/provider, shorten term, or keep liquid.

Money flow: Matured savings -> renewed product or settlement account.

Domains: Savings, Inbox, Accounts, Health.

## Emergency Withdrawal

What happened: A locked product must be broken before maturity.

Decision: Accept lost interest, use another source, or delay payment.

Money flow: Savings product -> settlement account minus forfeited/penalized interest.

Domains: Savings, Inbox, Accounts, Together.

## Provider Migration or Merger

What happened: Bank product, account number, or provider identity changes.

Decision: Confirm product still exists and update household record.

Money flow: Usually none; possibly product migration.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Inbox.

## Provider Bankruptcy or Resolution

What happened: Institution fails or is restricted.

Decision: Track insured claim, household liquidity response, emergency plan.

Money flow: Provider/insurance payout -> settlement account, possibly delayed.

Domains: Savings, Accounts, Inbox, Health, Together.
