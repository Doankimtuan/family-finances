# Reality Check

## Real-World Behavior vs Current Domain Understanding

The current domain understanding says Planning owns household intention, not real money truth. Young-household behavior supports this strongly.

Households already plan before money moves:

- Salary is mentally assigned.
- Bills are remembered before payment.
- Goals are discussed before saving.
- Emergency reserves are protected before use.
- Partners negotiate large expenses before spending.

The mental model is real. The main validation challenge is terminology and complexity.

## Already Validated

- Planning solves a real problem: account balances do not show committed money.
- Users naturally understand expected bills, goals, due dates, and salary allocation.
- Shared planning is relevant for couples and young families.
- Real-versus-virtual separation is financially necessary.
- Recurring obligations are central to household planning.
- Goals are natural and emotionally meaningful.
- Month or period review is behaviorally useful when framed as learning.
- The domain can survive 5-10 years because obligations grow with family life.

## Assumptions Remaining

- Whether "jar" is the best Vietnam-first household term.
- Whether users understand planned amount versus real balance without repeated explanation.
- Whether Month Ritual feels natural or too ceremonial.
- Whether users want strict allocation or rough guidance.
- Whether irregular income is common enough in the target group to affect early decisions.
- Whether partner review reduces conflict or creates surveillance anxiety.
- Whether users will maintain recurring assumptions over months.

## Still Needs Confirmation

- Interviews with Vietnamese couples aged 20-35.
- Observation of payday planning behavior.
- Review of actual household spreadsheets, notes, chats, and app screenshots.
- Vietnamese terminology testing for jars, goals, recurring, planned amount, reallocation, and month review.
- Longitudinal evidence of manual maintenance tolerance.
- Research on partner privacy, blame, and shared-control dynamics.
- Research on seasonal planning around Tet, school fees, travel, insurance, and family support.
- Validation of how users interpret current Plan, Jars, Goals, Recurring, Calendar, and Month Ritual concepts.
