# Approved Features

## Approved Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why approved | Expected product value | Dependencies | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GOAL-PD-001 | Named future purpose | Young households naturally save for concrete outcomes. | Gives shared meaning to future money preparation. | Planning domain context. | Name and purpose only. |
| GOAL-PD-002 | Target amount | A goal needs an estimated amount to be useful. | Makes progress understandable. | Household base currency and simple amount language. | Estimated target, not contract or guarantee. |
| GOAL-PD-006 | Goal lifecycle state | Active, paused, completed, and cancelled match real life. | Keeps goals aligned with changing household reality. | Goal terminology. | Intention state only. |
| GOAL-PD-007 | Intention versus real balance distinction | Protects BR-01 and user trust. | Prevents progress from being read as bank balance. | Accounts, Transactions, Savings boundaries. | Always part of goal meaning. |
| GOAL-PD-008 | Goal purpose explanation | Purpose improves motivation and partner understanding. | Helps households remember why the goal exists. | Goal name and optional context. | Human meaning, not advice. |
| GOAL-PD-015 | Notes or reason | Simple context supports household memory with low complexity. | Helps explain changes and contributions without heavy process. | Shared household visibility policy. | Lightweight notes only. |

## Product Value

Approved capabilities form the minimum safe Goals core:

- What the household is preparing for.
- How much it estimates is needed.
- Whether the goal is active or no longer active.
- Why the goal matters.
- How to keep goal intention separate from real money.

They are approved because they are simple, validated, emotionally meaningful, and maintainable for 5-10 years.
