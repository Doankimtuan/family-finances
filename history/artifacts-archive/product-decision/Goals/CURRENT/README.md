# Goals Product Decision Board

## Overview

This board makes product decisions for the Goals domain in ViNha based on:

- Phase 1 Domain Discovery for Goals.
- Phase 2 Household Reality Validation for Goals.
- ViNha product principles: simple before powerful, household-first, Real Ledger != Virtual Planning, Health read-only, no unnecessary automation, financial safety, and 5-10 year maintainability.

Goals are evaluated as named household future intentions, not money containers.

## Decision Process

The board reviewed every discovered Goals capability and classified each as:

- APPROVED.
- APPROVED WITH MODIFICATIONS.
- DEFERRED.
- REJECTED.

No capability remains undecided. Decisions are product decisions only and do not define UX, architecture, database, API, tests, or implementation.

## Decision Criteria

Capabilities were evaluated against:

- Real household need for young Vietnamese households.
- User comprehension.
- Risk of confusing goal progress with real money.
- Partner trust and blame sensitivity.
- Simplicity before power.
- Cross-domain ownership.
- Financial safety.
- Long-term maintainability.
