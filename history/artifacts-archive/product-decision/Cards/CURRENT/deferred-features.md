# Deferred Features

## Deferred Capabilities

| Capability ID | Capability | Reason | Conditions to reconsider | Suggested future phase |
|---------------|------------|--------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| CARD-PD-020 | Authorization / pending concepts | Too complex for core manual tracking. | Provider feeds or strong user evidence of pending-state pain. | Version 2.x |
| CARD-PD-021 | Supplementary-card spending separation | Prevalence and privacy expectations unvalidated. | Research shows common supplementary-card household use. | Version 1.x |
| CARD-PD-024 | Detailed issuer charge types | Provider-specific and high taxonomy burden. | Users frequently encounter fees and can identify them reliably. | Version 1.x |
| CARD-PD-025 | Points, miles, vouchers | Low safety value and high manual effort. | Rewards become material household decision factor. | Future |
| CARD-PD-027 | Disputes / chargebacks | Important but rare and provider-specific. | Users report frequent dispute pain or provider data becomes reliable. | Version 2.x |
| CARD-PD-028 | Statement imports / uploads | Adds document and reconciliation complexity. | Manual tracking proves insufficient; statement evidence is common. | Version 2.x |
| CARD-PD-029 | Tokenized card / card-on-file identity | Future-relevant but not core obligation clarity. | Subscriptions and virtual cards become a primary pain point. | Future |
| CARD-PD-030 | Replacement and masked identity | Low-frequency lifecycle complexity. | Card replacement creates history or refund confusion. | Version 2.x |
| CARD-PD-032 | Autopay behavior | Payment automation and posting reliability need validation. | Users commonly rely on autopay and can verify outcomes. | Future |
| CARD-PD-033 | Provider-verified card feeds | Provider access and consent are uncertain. | Stable feed provider exists with reliable Vietnam card coverage. | Future |
| CARD-PD-034 | Statement parsing and reconciliation | Too much automation before core model trust. | Statement uploads/imports become validated and manageable. | Future |
| CARD-PD-035 | Fraud / unusual-spend detection | Risk-sensitive and provider-dependent. | Reliable near-real-time data exists and false-positive handling is researched. | Future |
| CARD-PD-036 | Subscription visibility | Strong future need but data-dependent. | Card-on-file subscriptions are confirmed as recurring household pain. | Version 2.x |
| CARD-PD-037 | Multi-currency card cost analysis | Useful mainly for travel/cross-border users. | Cross-border card usage becomes common in target segment. | Future |
| CARD-PD-039 | Reward optimization analysis | Can encourage overspending and distract from safety. | Strong evidence shows users need neutral reward visibility without behavioral harm. | Future |
| CARD-PD-040 | Household card-use policies | Risks surveillance and household conflict. | Partner/dependent-card research shows safe, consent-based need. | Future |
| CARD-PD-041 | Country-specific card rule packs | Vietnam-first is sufficient now. | International expansion is approved elsewhere. | Future |

## Deferral Rationale

Deferred capabilities are real but not currently necessary for safe household card management. Most require provider reliability, direct household research, privacy decisions, or stronger evidence that manual effort is worth the value.
