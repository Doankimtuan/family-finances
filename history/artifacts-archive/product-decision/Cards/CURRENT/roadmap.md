# Roadmap Classification

This roadmap classifies decisions by product phase. It is not an implementation plan.

## MVP

| Capability IDs | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| CARD-PD-001, CARD-PD-003, CARD-PD-007, CARD-PD-009, CARD-PD-010, CARD-PD-011, CARD-PD-012, CARD-PD-013, CARD-PD-014, CARD-PD-015, CARD-PD-016, CARD-PD-022, CARD-PD-023 | Minimum safe credit-card reality: instrument, issuer, limit, dates, amounts, billing period, purchase/repayment separation, refunds, repayment source, and history. |

## Version 1.x

| Capability IDs | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| CARD-PD-002, CARD-PD-004, CARD-PD-005, CARD-PD-006, CARD-PD-008, CARD-PD-017, CARD-PD-018, CARD-PD-019, CARD-PD-021, CARD-PD-024, CARD-PD-026, CARD-PD-031 | These improve card identity, household responsibility, fees, interest, cashback, card-origin installments, and repayment interpretation after core card obligation is stable. Deferred items in this group need validation before activation. |

## Version 2.x

| Capability IDs | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| CARD-PD-020, CARD-PD-027, CARD-PD-028, CARD-PD-030, CARD-PD-036 | These support pending states, disputes, statements, replacements, and subscriptions. They are real but require stronger data or user evidence. |
