# Executive Summary

Savings is approved as a core Money-domain product capability for ViNha.

The board decision is deliberately conservative: Savings should not become a digital-bank clone, provider marketplace, investment optimizer, or rate-comparison product. It should help young Vietnamese households answer practical household questions: what is protected, what is locked, what matures soon, what decision is needed, and what happens if we break it.

The final scope prioritizes:

- Real Ledger correctness.
- Term and maturity awareness.
- Household decision moments through Inbox.
- Early withdrawal clarity.
- Provider-confirmed truth over estimates.
- Partner visibility and decision memory for meaningful savings decisions.

The board rejects or defers capabilities that add banking-app complexity without strong household value, especially deep tax tooling, investment-like advice, automatic recurring transfers, provider marketplaces, and exposed provider rule catalogs.

MVP should include only the thin, high-value core: create/manual-track savings, funding source, settlement destination, principal, provider, term, maturity, expected interest, maturity Inbox decision, renewal/withdraw choices, and early-withdrawal preview. v1 should harden pending/failed states, provider confirmation, purpose reference, legal depositor, and partner decision memory. Future releases may add deposit insurance exposure, multi-currency, partial withdrawal, product evidence, and provider risk handling.
