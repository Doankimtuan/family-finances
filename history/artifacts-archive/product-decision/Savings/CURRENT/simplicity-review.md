# Simplicity Review

## Remove or Avoid

Provider marketplace/rate shopping.

Reason: High complexity, stale-data risk, and weak fit with Household Money OS.

Automatic recurring saving transfer inside Savings.

Reason: Belongs to Planning as intention and Ledger as actual movement.

Full provider rule catalog exposed to users.

Reason: Households need understandable consequences, not bank configuration.

Personal/business distinction inside Savings.

Reason: Scope creep beyond young household finance.

Regulated advice language.

Reason: Legal and trust risk.

## Keep Simple

Product type.

Rule: Distinguish only what affects household decisions: locked/flexible/manual and regulated/non-obvious provider class.

Interest.

Rule: Show expected versus posted truth. Hide formulas unless needed.

Maturity.

Rule: One decision moment, not a notification storm.

Renewal.

Rule: Pre-fill preference, require decision.

Early withdrawal.

Rule: Show cost and confirmation. Do not shame.

Provider data.

Rule: Use as support data, not as a product browsing experience.

## Complexity Worth Paying For

- Separation of estimated and posted interest.
- Maturity Inbox decision.
- Early withdrawal preview and confirmation.
- Immutable cycle snapshot.
- Pending/failed settlement in v1.
- Legal depositor in v1.
