# Deferred Features

## Deferred Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Reason | Conditions to reconsider | Suggested future phase |
|----|------------|--------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| TGT-PD-022 | Multiple households per user | Adds switching, privacy, and data-leak risk. | Strong evidence of parent-support or multi-home use cases. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-023 | Complex family structures | Real but too broad for young-couple MVP. | Research shows dependents/non-members require first-class modeling. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-024 | Temporary access | Low validated need and high permission complexity. | Clear demand for advisor/helper access with safety model. | Future |
| TGT-PD-025 | Separation or split workflows | High sensitivity and insufficient research. | Safety, legal, and user research validate expected outcomes. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-026 | External account consent records | Depends on bank/wallet data sharing maturity. | Provider integrations become approved and legal review confirms consent needs. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-027 | Household export/deletion audit | Important data-governance need but outside core collaboration MVP. | Data portability/deletion policy work begins. | Version 1.x |
| TGT-PD-028 | Provider consent renewal | No provider connection scope now. | External provider feeds are approved. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-029 | Cross-border household settings | Not a common Vietnam-first core case. | Material number of users have foreign income or cross-border households. | Future |
| TGT-PD-030 | Household leave/close lifecycle | Real but emotionally and legally sensitive. | Research validates safe exit expectations. | Version 2.x |
| TGT-PD-031 | Household access recovery context | Broader recovery is partly Auth, partly Together. | Repeated failure cases appear in onboarding/support evidence. | Version 1.x |

## Deferred Principle

Deferred items are not rejected. They are valid pressures that should wait until core household collaboration is stable and user research justifies added complexity.
