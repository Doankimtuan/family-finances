# Cross-Domain Impact

## Accounts

Together provides household membership and access context. Accounts remain owner of real money containers and account facts. Together must not decide account balances or account inclusion in real position.

## Transactions

Transactions use Together for household scope. Transaction truth, attribution, correction, refunds, and transfers remain in Transactions.

## Cards

Cards may be household-visible based on membership. Card balances, limits, statements, due dates, and payment obligations remain in Cards.

## Loans

Loans may be household-relevant even when legally individual. Together provides visibility boundary only; loan terms and repayment status remain in Loans.

## Planning

Together holds simple household policy assumptions used by Planning. Planning owns jars, recurring patterns, allocations, and month behavior.

## Goals

Goals may reflect shared household aspirations. Together does not own target amounts, progress, funding, or priority.

## Inbox

Inbox may surface decisions to household members. Together defines who belongs to the household and which policy changes are partner-visible; Inbox owns review-item lifecycle.

## Health

Health reads household-scoped data and remains read-only. Together may define who can see Health, but Health must not change Together policies or money records.

## Categories

Categories provide household meaning vocabulary. Together does not own category taxonomy or mappings.

## Together

Approved decisions keep Together focused on membership, invitation, policy visibility, household preferences, and household scope. Deferred and rejected decisions protect Together from absorbing unrelated domain responsibilities.
