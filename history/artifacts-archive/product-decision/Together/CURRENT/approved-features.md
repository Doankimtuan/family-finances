# Approved Features

## Approved Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why approved | Expected product value | Dependencies | Scope |
|----|------------|--------------|------------------------|--------------|-------|
| TGT-PD-001 | Define household | Core household-first foundation. | Establishes the shared financial unit. | Authenticated user. | One household context. |
| TGT-PD-002 | Identify active members | Users need to know who belongs. | Builds trust and prevents ambiguity. | Household membership. | Active members only. |
| TGT-PD-004 | Represent partner participation | Partners are real participants even with unequal engagement. | Supports shared responsibility. | Membership. | Partner participation, not performance tracking. |
| TGT-PD-005 | Invite person | Natural way to add a partner. | Converts personal setup into shared finance. | Household and authenticated inviter. | Partner invite only. |
| TGT-PD-006 | Accept or decline invitation | Preserves consent. | Makes joining voluntary and clear. | Pending invite and authenticated invitee. | Accept/decline decision. |
| TGT-PD-007 | Invitation lifecycle state | Prevents unclear access states. | Improves recovery and safety. | Invitation flow. | Pending, accepted, revoked, expired, declined. |
| TGT-PD-008 | Define household policies | Shared assumptions affect planning and review. | Reduces forgotten agreements. | Household membership. | Small set of household policies. |
| TGT-PD-009 | Expose policy state | Partners need current assumptions. | Reduces surprise. | Household policies. | Current policy visibility. |
| TGT-PD-010 | Attribute material policy changes | Trust depends on knowing material changes happened. | Supports accountability without dispute resolution. | Policy changes. | Material changes only. |
| TGT-PD-011 | Provide household scope | All household domains need boundary. | Enables household-first product behavior. | Membership resolution. | Scope provider only. |
| TGT-PD-012 | Protect records from non-members | Financial privacy is essential. | Prevents data leakage. | Auth and membership. | Household-scoped access protection. |
| TGT-PD-013 | Identify active household status | Needed for onboarding and gates. | Keeps users in correct product state. | Auth session. | Has active household or not. |
| TGT-PD-014 | Household preferences | Shared date, currency, and language context matter. | Prevents interpretation errors. | Household record. | Locale, timezone, base currency. |
| TGT-PD-015 | Member display/contact identity | Users need to recognize household people. | Makes membership understandable. | User identity. | Minimal recognition identity. |

## Product Value

The approved set makes Together a stable collaboration foundation while keeping it simple. It supports ViNha's household-first premise without turning Together into money movement, planning, legal status, or relationship management.
