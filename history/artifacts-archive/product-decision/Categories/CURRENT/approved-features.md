# Approved Features

## Approved Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why approved | Expected product value | Dependencies | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CAT-PD-001 | Define household category | Households need reusable labels for recurring money meaning. | Establishes shared vocabulary. | Household membership and category terminology. | Label definition only. |
| CAT-PD-002 | Distinguish income and expense categories | Income and expense meaning differ in real life. | Reduces salary, refund, transfer, and spending confusion. | Transaction direction context. | Classification kind only. |
| CAT-PD-003 | Categorize transaction | Category assignment is the core validated behavior. | Makes transaction history understandable. | Transactions. | One household meaning label per transaction in simple-first scope. |
| CAT-PD-004 | Leave uncategorized | Unknown meaning is common and should not be guessed. | Preserves trust and avoids false certainty. | Transactions and Inbox context. | Explicit unknown state. |
| CAT-PD-005 | Correct category assignment | Mistakes are normal and recoverable. | Improves report trust and household memory. | Transaction correction semantics. | Change category meaning, not money fact. |
| CAT-PD-006 | Filter transaction history by category | Users naturally ask where spending went. | Fast answer to household questions. | Categorized transactions. | Filtering of real transaction facts. |
| CAT-PD-008 | Maintain household-specific vocabulary | Vietnamese households vary by life stage and family obligations. | Makes categories feel like household language. | Household tenancy. | Household-owned category names and use. |
| CAT-PD-010 | Separate category from account, merchant, payment method, and plan | Protects financial correctness and BR-01. | Prevents users from misunderstanding where money is. | Accounts, Transactions, Planning boundaries. | Category remains purpose label only. |

## Product Value

Approved capabilities form the minimum safe Categories core:

- Define the household's classification vocabulary.
- Attach meaning to transaction facts.
- Preserve uncertainty when meaning is unknown.
- Correct wrong meaning.
- Use categories to understand history.
- Keep purpose labels separate from money containers and planning capacity.

They are approved because they are simple, validated, financially safe, and maintainable for 5-10 years.
