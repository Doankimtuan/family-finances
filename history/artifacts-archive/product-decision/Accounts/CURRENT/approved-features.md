# Approved Features

## Approved Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why approved | Expected product value | Dependencies | Scope |
|----|------------|--------------|------------------------|--------------|-------|
| ACC-PD-001 | Real-world money containers | Foundation of household money reality. | Users understand where money is. | Tenancy, Money surface. | Bank/cash/wallet-like real containers. |
| ACC-PD-003 | Household-language account names | Matches how young households talk. | Faster recognition and partner understanding. | None beyond Accounts. | User-recognizable labels. |
| ACC-PD-004 | Opening or starting balance | Required to begin recorded tracking. | Establishes first real position. | Accounts. | Starting recorded amount only. |
| ACC-PD-005 | Current recorded balance | Core account value. | Gives users practical money truth. | Transactions, reconciliation behavior. | Recorded account amount. |
| ACC-PD-007 | Active versus historical distinction | Real accounts change over years. | Keeps current totals clean and history intact. | Transactions. | Active/historical status. |
| ACC-PD-008 | Account context for transactions | Transactions need a money container. | Prevents ambiguous spending and income records. | Transactions. | Account reference/context only. |
| ACC-PD-010 | Preserve history after closure | Closed accounts still explain past money. | Trustworthy long-term record. | Transactions. | Historical interpretation. |
| ACC-PD-012 | Real money versus virtual planning distinction | Protects product foundation. | Prevents account/jar confusion. | Planning, Jars, Home, Health. | Absolute product boundary. |
| ACC-PD-024 | Export account records | Supports data portability. | Users can retain account facts outside product. | Shared export capability. | Account facts only. |
