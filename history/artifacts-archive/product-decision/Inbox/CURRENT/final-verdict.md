# Final Verdict

## Overall Product Health

Inbox is product-healthy and ready to move toward Business Blueprint work with clear cautions.

The domain solves a validated household problem: young families accumulate unresolved financial attention across transactions, reminders, savings events, and partner context. The approved scope keeps Inbox simple, household-first, and financially safe.

## Decision Summary

- The core decision queue is approved.
- Source linkage, reason-for-attention, state, resolution, active/history separation, and real-versus-virtual separation are approved.
- Dismissal, acknowledgement, history, deferral, suggestions, staleness, reminder expiration, auto-resolution, and workload metrics are approved only with modifications.
- Partner targeting, grouping, duplicate detection, evidence surfacing, dispute notes, exception-only review, provider classification, learning, invoice review, and tax evidence are deferred.
- Partner coordination analytics and general notification-center behavior are rejected.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.84

Reason:

The core decisions are strongly supported by Phase 1 discovery and Phase 2 household validation. Confidence is reduced by unresolved Vietnamese terminology, review-volume tolerance, partner privacy norms, reminder boundary tension, and trust in suggestions/automation.

## Readiness For Business Blueprint

Readiness: Ready with cautions.

Business Blueprint may proceed for approved and modified capabilities, provided the blueprint does not:

- Treat Inbox as a transaction ledger.
- Treat Inbox as a notification center.
- Treat Inbox as a task manager or chat system.
- Let Inbox execute payments or own real-money movement.
- Let Inbox mutate Health.
- Let suggestions or auto-resolution hide financial judgment.
- Introduce partner analytics or surveillance behavior.
