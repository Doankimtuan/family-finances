# Approved Features

## Approved Capabilities

| ID | Capability | Why approved | Expected product value | Dependencies | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PL-PD-001 | Household income intention | Salary-cycle planning is validated for young households. | Helps households start planning before spending. | Accounts and Transactions as factual confirmation. | Expected income only, not confirmed money. |
| PL-PD-003 | Fixed and variable allocation targets | Matches how households allocate fixed bills and flexible spending. | Makes salary assignment understandable. | BR-04 income placement; active jars. | Fixed amount and proportion-style intention. |
| PL-PD-004 | Intention lifecycle states | Households pause, resume, finish, and abandon intentions. | Keeps active planning clean over time. | Planning terminology. | State of intention only. |
| PL-PD-008 | Planned-versus-real distinction | Protects ViNha's central product principle. | Prevents users from mistaking plan for balance. | BR-01. | Always visible in Planning meaning. |
| PL-PD-011 | Corrections to intention | Mistakes are normal in household planning. | Repairs trust without rewriting facts. | Month review and audit language. | Planning corrections only. |
| PL-PD-013 | Read-only cross-domain facts | Planning needs real facts for context. | Keeps planning grounded while preserving ownership. | Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Savings, Inbox. | Read-only consumption; no source-domain mutation. |

## Product Value

Approved capabilities form the minimum safe Planning core:

- What income is expected.
- Where money is intended to go.
- Which intentions are active.
- What is planned versus real.
- How mistakes are corrected.
- Which external facts inform planning.

They are approved because they are validated, simple enough for early product use, and compatible with 5-10 year maintainability.
