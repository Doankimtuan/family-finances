## 1. Strategic Vision

ViNha is built for multi-generational household financial operating stability. Business Model v2 establishes a scalable core that can expand over the next 5 to 10 years without requiring a fundamental architectural redesign or violating the Product Constitution.

This roadmap outlines the long-term strategic horizon.

## 3. Constitutional Guardianship Over Time

As ViNha evolves across Horizons 2 and 3, all future product expansions MUST adhere to the **Immutable Constitutional Principles**:
1. **BR-01 Guard**: Multi-Jar goal funding and investment integrations MUST never merge real ledger balances with virtual plan allocations.
2. **BR-24 Guard**: Machine learning and intelligence engines MUST remain strictly READ-ONLY advisory services (`Health-RO`). They will suggest actions in Inbox but will NEVER execute money movement without explicit user confirmation.
3. **Identity Guard**: ViNha will NEVER evolve into a bloated expense tracker or ad-driven lead-generation portal. ViNha will remain an empowering Household Money Operating System.
