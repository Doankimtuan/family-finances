# Future Considerations

These observations may become useful in 3-5 years. They are not roadmap items.

- Multi-household membership for users who manage their own household and support parents.
- Explicit former-member lifecycle for separation, divorce, death, or household migration.
- Consent records for external financial data shared into a household.
- Household-level data export and deletion interpretation.
- Richer audit history for material household events beyond policy changes.
- Distinction between household member, dependent, and financially relevant non-member.
- Support for cross-border partners, foreign income, and multi-currency preferences.
- Safety-sensitive handling for financial coercion or unsafe shared visibility.
- Household responsibility mapping without creating hierarchy.
- Time-limited or purpose-limited access for advisors or family helpers.
- Member engagement signals that preserve dignity and avoid surveillance.
- Legal and compliance treatment for personal financial data under evolving Vietnam privacy law.
- Compatibility with open banking or bank-link consent standards if they mature in Vietnam.
- Household continuity when login providers, phone numbers, or official IDs change.
- Long-term archive semantics for closed households.
