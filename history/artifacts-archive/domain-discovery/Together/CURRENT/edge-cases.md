# Edge Cases

This document identifies real-world edge cases only. It does not propose solutions.

## Membership

- User creates a household alone and never invites a partner.
- Partner invitation is sent to the wrong email.
- Invitee uses a different email than the invited email.
- Invitation expires before acceptance.
- Invitation is revoked after the invitee saw it.
- Invitee declines and later changes mind.
- Existing user already belongs to another household.
- Household reaches its member limit.
- Member loses access to the email used for login.
- Member's display name or email changes.
- A member becomes inactive but historical actions remain relevant.

## Relationship And Household Changes

- Partners separate.
- Partners reconcile after separation.
- One partner moves away temporarily.
- A child, parent, or dependent starts affecting finances.
- A family member contributes money but should not see all household data.
- A household wants to split finances into personal and shared zones.
- Trust breaks after hidden spending or hidden debt.
- One partner wants to leave the app while the other wants historical records.

## Policies

- Policy changed by one partner without the other noticing.
- Policy change is corrected shortly after saving.
- Household disagrees whether a change was material.
- Policy was misunderstood due to wording.
- Household uses a verbal agreement that differs from product policy.
- Policy is changed during an emergency.
- Policy change interacts with an already-running plan or review item.

## Access And Consent

- Shared device exposes household data.
- Partner remains logged in on a lost phone.
- One partner authorizes external account data but later revokes consent.
- External data belongs legally to one person but is practically household-relevant.
- Personal financial information appears in a shared view unexpectedly.
- A member requests export or deletion.

## External Providers

- Bank or wallet account is blocked.
- KYC information expires.
- Provider changes API behavior.
- Provider notification is delayed.
- Account holder identity differs from household member name.
- Payment account or wallet rules change.

## Data Quality

- Member record lacks display name.
- Household has duplicate-looking members due to identity linking history.
- Old invitation remains pending after real-world relationship changes.
- Historical records reference a former member.
- Policy event lacks enough human context to be understood later.

## Unexpected Events

- Job loss creates urgent financial policy changes.
- Medical emergency changes who manages money.
- Death of a member.
- Divorce or legal dispute.
- Fraud or coercive financial control.
- Domestic conflict makes shared visibility unsafe.

These cases are important because Together touches trust, identity, and safety, not merely configuration.
