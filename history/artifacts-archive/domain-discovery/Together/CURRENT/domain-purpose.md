# Domain Purpose

Together exists because household finance is not only arithmetic. It is shared responsibility between people.

Before a household can decide whether money is available, where spending belongs, what needs review, or whether the household is healthy, the product must know who "we" means.

## Core Responsibility

Together defines the trusted household boundary.

It is responsible for:

- Household identity.
- Membership.
- Shared access expectations.
- Partner roles and responsibilities.
- Household-level policies and assumptions.
- Visibility of material household changes.

## Financial Principle

The financial principle behind Together is shared accountability.

In a household, financial truth has social context:

- A salary may be legally paid to one person but practically supports both partners.
- A bank account may be individually held but treated as household money.
- A spending rule may be agreed verbally but misunderstood later.
- One partner may manage daily detail while the other still needs meaningful visibility.

Together makes the human boundary of the financial system explicit.

## Value Provided

Together provides:

- Clarity about who belongs to the household.
- Trust that shared information is visible to the right people.
- A stable context for all household-scoped money records.
- Accountability for policy or assumption changes.
- Reduced ambiguity between personal money, shared money, and family obligations.

The value is collaboration clarity, not financial calculation. Together does not decide what money is, how much exists, or how it should be spent. It defines who participates in those decisions.
