# Terminology

## Glossary

| Preferred term | Definition |
|----------------|------------|
| Together | Domain and product surface for household collaboration. |
| Household | The shared financial unit recognized by the product. |
| Member | A person who belongs to a household. |
| Partner | A household member participating in daily shared finance. |
| Admin | A household member responsible for elevated household policy actions. |
| Invitation | A time-bound request for another person to join the household. |
| Invitee | The person receiving an invitation. |
| Inviter | The member who creates an invitation. |
| Membership | The relationship between a user and a household. |
| Role | A member's responsibility level inside the household. |
| Policy | A household-level rule or assumption that affects money behavior. |
| Policy event | A record that a policy changed or was reviewed. |
| Household preference | A shared setting such as base currency, locale, or timezone. |
| Trust boundary | The line between people who may access household financial information and people who may not. |
| Consent | Agreement by a person to participate in or expose information to the household financial space. |

## Forbidden Synonyms

| Avoid | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Owner | Implies hierarchy over shared household finance. |
| Primary user | Implies the other partner is secondary. |
| Secondary user | Undermines equal household participation. |
| Viewer | Implies a read-only role not established in the current domain language. |
| Guest | Makes a household member feel temporary or lesser. |
| Family account | Ambiguous between household product identity and bank account. |
| Permission matrix | Enterprise language that overstates household needs. |
| Subaccount | Confuses membership with financial accounts. |

## Business Definitions

Household:

- A financial collaboration unit, not necessarily a legal family, marriage, lease, or residence record.

Partner:

- A trusted participant in shared household finance, regardless of income level.

Admin:

- A member with responsibility for household policies, not superior ownership of household money.

Policy:

- A shared assumption that shapes behavior in other domains.

Partner-visible:

- Visible enough that a reasonable household partner can discover that a material assumption changed.
