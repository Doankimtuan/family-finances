# Together Domain Discovery

## Overview

Together is the household collaboration domain. It answers: who is included in the household financial space, what each person is responsible for, and which shared assumptions govern money behavior?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where young households often combine individual bank accounts, e-wallets, cash habits, family support, and informal partner agreements rather than relying only on formal joint banking.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Together before any business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is intentionally observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, or modify existing Sources of Truth.

## Scope

Included:

- Household membership and shared financial identity.
- Partner visibility, consent, responsibility, trust, and policy awareness.
- Vietnam household money collaboration patterns.
- Boundaries with Auth, Accounts, Transactions, Planning, Inbox, Health, and external providers.

Excluded:

- New product features.
- Implementation models.
- Database, API, or UX proposals.
- Official business-rule changes.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
