# Planning Domain Discovery

## Overview

Planning is the household's intentional view of money before, during, and after real money moves. It answers: where is money meant to go, what must be prepared for, what goals are being protected, and what should be reviewed together?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where young households commonly combine monthly salary cycles, cash, bank transfers, QR payments, e-wallets, term savings, debt obligations, family support, school or child expenses, and informal mental envelopes.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Planning before any business, UX, architecture, database, or API design begins.

It is intentionally observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, or modify existing Sources of Truth.

## Scope

Included:

- Household budgeting, allocation, goals, recurring obligations, calendar awareness, and month-end review.
- Virtual planning concepts that express intention without claiming real money moved.
- Vietnam salary, family, cashless payment, e-wallet, debt, and savings behaviors that influence planning.
- Boundaries with Accounts, Transactions, Cards, Loans, Savings, Inbox, Health, Together, and external providers.

Excluded:

- New product features.
- Implementation models.
- Database, API, or UX proposals.
- Official business-rule changes.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
