# Actors

## User

The person interacting with the planning record. They create, review, adjust, pause, or cancel intentions and interpret planning information for household decisions.

## Partner

Another household member who shares daily Money, Plan, and Inbox responsibility. The partner may co-own decisions, notice missing obligations, challenge assumptions, or rely on the plan for spending confidence.

## Household

The shared economic unit. The household is the real beneficiary of planning and the context in which income, obligations, goals, trade-offs, and review make sense.

## Family

Parents, siblings, children, relatives, or extended-family members who influence planning through support, gifts, loans, childcare, shared housing, school costs, medical events, Tet spending, weddings, funerals, or emergencies.

## Employer Or Income Source

The party that provides salary, freelance income, business income, bonus, commission, allowance, or reimbursement. It influences timing and reliability of inflows.

## Bank

Holds real accounts, executes transfers, confirms balances, and provides transaction history. The bank does not own household intention.

## E-Wallet Or Fintech Provider

Holds or facilitates wallet balances, QR payments, rewards, bill payment, and linked-bank transactions. It may provide useful facts but does not define household planning priorities.

## Card Provider

Defines credit limits, statements, due dates, and outstanding obligations. Planning may consider repayment pressure but does not own card truth.

## Lender

Defines loan principal, repayment schedule, rate terms, fees, and legal obligation. Planning may consider expected repayment pressure but does not own loan truth.

## Savings Provider

Defines savings product balance, rate, tenor, maturity, withdrawal rules, and settlement. Planning may associate intention or goals but does not own savings product truth.

## Merchant Or Biller

Creates expected or actual obligations such as rent, utility, phone, internet, subscription, school, insurance, medical, and household service payments.

## System

Maintains the planning record, displays household intention, reads factual inputs from other domains, and preserves the real-versus-virtual distinction.

## Background Worker

May update derived planning state, reminders, schedule projections, auto-lock timing, or review signals. It should not be interpreted as moving real money unless an external money execution domain explicitly confirms that fact.

## Third-Party Provider

Any external integration supplying account, transaction, bill, card, loan, savings, or calendar data. It can provide evidence or read-only information but does not own household intent.
