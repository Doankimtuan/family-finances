# Future Considerations

The following observations may become useful over a 3-5 year horizon. They are not roadmap items or implementation recommendations.

## Provider Coverage

- Bank, wallet, card, biller, invoice, and receipt providers may become more structured.
- Vietnam's cashless and QR payment ecosystem may create more real-time payment signals.
- Cross-border QR payment interoperability may increase foreign merchant and currency complexity.

## Household Complexity

- Young families may add children, school fees, insurance, mortgages, elder support, and household business activity.
- Partner-specific spending context may become more important over time.
- Family reimbursements and informal loans may need clearer review evidence.

## Review Volume

- As integrations increase, Inbox may shift from reviewing every item to reviewing exceptions.
- Pattern-based suggestions may become more important.
- Duplicate detection may become more important.

## Evidence

- Receipts, screenshots, e-invoices, warranties, and provider messages may become useful review context.
- Household business or side-income users may care about invoice retention and tax evidence.

## Decision Intelligence

- Household-specific patterns may improve suggested decisions.
- Item ordering may need to account for due date, amount, staleness, and financial impact.
- Auto-resolution may need stronger audit language as confidence improves.

## Long-Term Stability

The Inbox concept is stable if it remains a queue of unresolved financial attention. It is less stable if it expands into generic notifications, task management, messaging, or provider-statement storage.
