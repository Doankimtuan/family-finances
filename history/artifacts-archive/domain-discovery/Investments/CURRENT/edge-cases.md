# Edge Cases

This document identifies real-world edge cases only. It does not propose solutions.

## Cancellation

- Buy/sell order cancelled before execution.
- Fund subscription cancelled before NAV date.
- Recurring contribution cancelled.
- Insurance-linked policy cancelled during cooling-off or early period.

## Correction

- Wrong quantity recorded.
- Wrong price or NAV used.
- Wrong trade date versus settlement date.
- Wrong asset symbol.
- Wrong account/custodian.
- Wrong currency.
- Wrong fee treatment.
- Wrong cost basis after partial sale.

## Refund / Reversal

- Failed subscription returns cash.
- Failed trade settlement returns cash or reverses position.
- Broker fee rebate.
- Fund redemption rejected and units restored.
- Corporate action payment corrected.

## Expiration / Maturity

- Bond matures and principal returns.
- Rights issue entitlement expires.
- Warrant or option expires.
- Insurance policy reaches maturity.
- Promotional investment product term ends.

## Rate Changes

- Bond coupon reset.
- Fund yield changes.
- Margin interest rate changes.
- Gold buy/sell spread changes.
- Foreign exchange rate changes.

## Missing Information

- No statement available.
- Family investment only verbally described.
- Physical gold receipt lost.
- Provider app inaccessible.
- Historical purchase price unknown.
- Corporate action details unclear.
- Dividend/coupon received but source not identified.

## Manual Adjustment

- User updates estimated value.
- User writes down private investment.
- User changes cost basis after finding receipt.
- User corrects ownership share with partner/family.
- User marks asset as no longer household-owned.

## Unexpected Events

- Market crash.
- Trading halt.
- Fund redemption suspension.
- Broker outage.
- Issuer default.
- Delisting.
- Stock split, merger, acquisition, rights issue, or tender offer.
- Forced sale or liquidation.
- Account frozen due to KYC, legal, or regulatory issue.
- Fraud, scam, or unauthorized trade.
- Partner dispute over risk or secrecy.
- Death, inheritance, divorce, or household separation.

## Liquidity Mismatch

- Asset appears valuable but cannot be sold quickly.
- Sale proceeds are unsettled.
- Redemption has a cut-off date.
- Gold can be sold but at a lower bid price.
- Private investment has no active buyer.
- Insurance-linked value is lower than expected surrender value.
