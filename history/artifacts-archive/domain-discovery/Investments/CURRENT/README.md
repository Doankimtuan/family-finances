# Investments Domain Discovery

## Overview

Investments are household-owned financial assets bought with the expectation of future return and accepted risk. They answer: what assets does the household hold beyond cash and savings products, what are they worth, and what risk or liquidity do they carry?

This discovery focuses on Vietnam first, where ordinary young households may use securities accounts, listed stocks, ETFs, open-ended funds, bonds, gold, insurance-linked investment products, and informal family-led investments. International differences are mentioned only when they affect product interpretation.

## Purpose

This pack documents the real-world nature of Investments before any business, UX, architecture, database, API, or product design begins.

It is intentionally observational. It does not redesign ViNha, introduce requirements, create product decisions, or modify existing Sources of Truth.

## Scope

Included:

- Real-world investment assets and investment accounts.
- Risk, return, liquidity, valuation, ownership, custody, contributions, withdrawals, dividends, interest, and realized or unrealized gain/loss.
- Vietnam securities, fund, bond, gold, and household usage patterns.
- Boundaries with Accounts, Transactions, Savings, Goals, Planning, Health, Inbox, Together, and third-party providers.

Excluded:

- New product features.
- Implementation models.
- Database, API, or UX proposals.
- Official business-rule changes.
- Product decisions or roadmap commitments.
- Investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.
