# Boundaries

## What Belongs Here

Investments owns the factual understanding of investment assets.

Included:

- Securities holdings.
- Fund units.
- ETFs.
- Bonds and bond funds.
- Gold when treated as an investment asset.
- Insurance-linked investment value when treated as an asset.
- Private or informal investment claims.
- Investment account cash when it is part of an investment platform's portfolio context.
- Cost, quantity, value, income, realized gain/loss, unrealized gain/loss, and valuation freshness.

## What Belongs In Another Domain

Accounts:

- Bank accounts, cash wallets, e-wallets, and ordinary cash containers.
- The bank account used to fund or receive investment proceeds.

Transactions:

- Real money transfers into or out of investment accounts.
- Fees, dividends, coupons, withdrawals, sale proceeds, and cash movements.

Savings:

- Term deposits and fixed-return savings products with principal protection and maturity terms.

Goals:

- Aspirational targets such as "invest for education" or "build retirement fund."

Planning:

- Intended monthly contribution, allocation intention, and household rituals.

Health:

- Interpretation of whether investment exposure affects household resilience.

Cards / Loans:

- Borrowed money, repayment obligations, card-funded purchases, and loan-related investment risks.

Together:

- Household membership, partner visibility, permissions, and decision policies.

Inbox:

- Review-worthy uncertainty, stale data, missing statements, or partner questions.

## Where Integrations Happen

Integrations may occur at:

- Bank transfer rails for funding and withdrawals.
- Broker accounts for holdings, trades, cash, and statements.
- Fund distributors for NAV, units, subscriptions, and redemptions.
- Market data providers for prices.
- Gold price or manual receipt sources.
- Document storage for statements and confirmations.

## Where Ownership Changes

Ownership changes when:

- Cash leaves a bank account and becomes investment account cash.
- Investment account cash buys an asset.
- Asset is sold or redeemed into investment cash.
- Investment cash is withdrawn to bank cash.
- Asset is transferred, gifted, inherited, written off, or no longer belongs to the household.

Domain ownership changes across contexts:

- Cash movement truth belongs to Transactions.
- Container truth belongs to Accounts.
- Holding/value truth belongs to Investments.
- Intention belongs to Planning or Goals.
- Interpretation belongs to Health.
