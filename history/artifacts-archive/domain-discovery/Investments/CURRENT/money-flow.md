# Money Flow

## Real Money

Investment begins and ends with real cash movement.

```text
Income / cash account
  -> bank account
  -> securities / fund / investment account cash
  -> investment purchase
  -> investment holding
```

Sale or redemption:

```text
Investment holding
  -> sell / redeem
  -> investment account cash
  -> bank account
  -> household cash position
```

Income from investment:

```text
Issuer / fund / bond
  -> dividend / distribution / coupon
  -> investment account cash or bank account
```

Fees and taxes:

```text
Investment account cash / sale proceeds
  -> broker, fund, exchange, tax authority, or provider charge
```

Important distinction:

- Cash transferred into an investment account is real money movement.
- Buying an asset changes the form of wealth from cash to holding.
- Market price changes do not move money until sale, redemption, distribution, or fee.

## Virtual Planning

Planning may express intent to invest, but it does not create or change an investment holding.

```text
Monthly plan / goal / jar intention
  -> intended investment contribution
  -> no real investment exists until cash is transferred and asset is bought
```

Investment value may inform planning confidence, but planning allocation is not the same as portfolio value.

```text
Investment portfolio value
  -> may inform household planning discussion
  -> does not become available plan capacity by default
```

## Read-Only Information

Read-only investment information can come from providers or manual statements.

```text
Broker / fund / market data / statement
  -> holdings, prices, NAV, units, cash, fees, timestamps
  -> household view
```

Read-only price movement:

```text
Market price / NAV changes
  -> updated estimated value
  -> unrealized gain/loss
  -> no bank-account cash movement
```

Manual valuation:

```text
User statement / gold receipt / family update
  -> manually stated value
  -> stale or uncertain information risk
```
