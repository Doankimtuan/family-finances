# Future Considerations

These observations may become useful in 3-5 years. They are not roadmap commitments.

## Market And Product Evolution

- Vietnam market infrastructure may continue moving toward broader international standards.
- Fund products may become more common for young households than direct stock picking.
- Digital broker/fund APIs may become more accessible.
- Gold and alternative assets may remain culturally important even when formal securities adoption grows.
- Insurance-linked investment products may keep creating confusion between protection, savings, and investment.

## Household Behavior Evolution

- Young families may want a clearer total wealth picture as income rises.
- Dual-income households may need partner-visible risk context.
- Education, home purchase, migration, and retirement goals may create longer investment horizons.
- Households may want to separate short-term cash safety from long-term growth assets.
- Manual tracking may remain necessary for gold, family assets, and private investments.

## Domain Evolution Observations

- Investments may need a durable distinction between account cash, holdings, and valuation.
- Valuation freshness may become as important as value itself.
- Asset-class taxonomy may need to accommodate local and international instruments.
- A future implementation may need explicit "not advice" boundaries.
- Investment data may need confidence levels because some values are exact and others are estimated.
- Household visibility and consent may matter more for investments than for ordinary accounts.
- Long-term stability depends on modeling financial truths rather than individual provider features.
