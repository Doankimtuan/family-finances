# Actors

## User

Creates, names, updates, pauses, completes, or cancels personal or household goals. May also record perceived contributions.

## Partner

Shares household context, negotiates priority, contributes money, questions progress, or depends on the outcome.

## Household

The economic unit that benefits from goal preparation. The household is the context for shared priorities, trade-offs, and trust.

## Family

May be the reason for a goal, a contributor to a goal, or a competing claim on goal money. In Vietnam, parents, siblings, and extended family can materially affect goal behavior.

## Bank

Holds real money in current accounts, savings accounts, or term deposits. The bank owns provider balance, product rules, interest, maturity, withdrawal constraints, and settlement.

## E-Wallet Or Fintech

May hold small balances or support transfers related to a goal. Owns wallet balance and transaction facts, not goal intention.

## Cash Holder

The person physically holding cash. Cash may support a goal but can be hard to verify or reconcile.

## Employer Or Income Source

Provides salary, bonus, commission, or irregular income that may fund goals. Does not own household goal meaning.

## Third-Party Provider

May provide account aggregation, payment evidence, savings product data, investment data, or exchange rates. Owns only the data it provides.

## System

Records goal facts known to the product, shows progress interpretation, preserves status, and keeps goal intention separate from real ledger truth.

## Background Worker

May observe time passing, stale states, scheduled reminders, or provider-derived facts if such behavior exists. It does not decide household priorities.

## Advisor Or Consultant

May help a household think through goals in real life. In product terms, advisory-grade recommendations are outside Goals unless separately validated and governed.
