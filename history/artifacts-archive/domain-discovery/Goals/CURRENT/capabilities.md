# Capabilities

This document lists natural domain capabilities only. It does not approve product scope.

## Core Capabilities

- Represent a named future purpose.
- Represent a target amount.
- Represent optional timing pressure.
- Represent current perceived progress.
- Represent contribution history or progress updates.
- Represent lifecycle state.
- Distinguish intention from real balance.
- Explain what the goal is for.
- Support household-level visibility.
- Support pausing, completion, and cancellation as real-life states.

## Optional Capabilities

- Associate a goal with a jar.
- Associate a goal with a savings product for context.
- Associate a goal with account or transaction evidence.
- Record partner-specific contribution context.
- Record notes or reason.
- Track deadline pressure.
- Track overfunding or partial funding.
- Compare goal progress with real money evidence.
- Represent goal priority.
- Represent recurring planned contributions.
- Represent non-cash support or gifts as context.

## Future Capabilities

- Multi-goal trade-off analysis.
- Long-horizon life-event goal modeling.
- Education cost preparation.
- Home deposit preparation.
- Goal-backed cash-flow forecasting.
- Provider-assisted evidence matching.
- Inflation-aware target review.
- Household goal negotiation history.
- Goal confidence based on factual money coverage.
- AI explanations grounded only in existing household facts.
