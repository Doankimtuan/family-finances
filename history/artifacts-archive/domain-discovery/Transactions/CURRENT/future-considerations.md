# Future Considerations

These are observations about capabilities that may become useful in 3-5 years. They are not roadmap items or product decisions.

## Possible Future Usefulness

- Bank, wallet, and card provider imports if reliable access becomes available.
- Merchant normalization for cryptic statement descriptions.
- Structured counterparty identity.
- Transfer modeling across owned accounts and household members.
- Pending, authorized, posted, settled, disputed, and reversed provider states.
- Receipt and invoice attachment.
- Split categorization for mixed-purpose purchases.
- Duplicate detection across manual and imported records.
- Reconciliation workflows for cash, bank, wallet, and card statements.
- Refund matching when merchants return money days or months later.
- Chargeback and dispute context for card and wallet transactions.
- Reimbursement linking between an expense and later income.
- Household member attribution when multiple people create transactions.
- Multi-currency and exchange-rate metadata.
- Subscription and recurring pattern detection.
- Privacy controls for sensitive transaction details in multi-member households.
- AI-assisted classification and explanation that never invents money movement.

## Stability Observation

The core idea that a transaction is a factual money movement is stable over 5-10 years. Most likely evolution happens around metadata, import sources, review assistance, and reconciliation depth rather than the basic definition.
