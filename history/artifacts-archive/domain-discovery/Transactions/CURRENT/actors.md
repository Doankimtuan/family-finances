# Actors

## User

Records, reviews, searches, categorizes, corrects, or explains household transactions.

## Partner

Creates additional real-world activity and may verify, question, or classify shared transactions.

## Family

May send or receive transfers, allowances, shared bill contributions, emergency support, loan repayment, or informal reimbursements.

## Merchant

Provides goods or services and creates a charge, receipt, refund, cancellation, fee, or dispute context.

## Employer

Pays salary, bonus, allowance, reimbursement, or other income.

## Bank

Maintains account records, transfer records, card-linked account records, fees, interest, reversals, and official statements.

## Card Issuer

Records card authorizations, posted purchases, refunds, fees, interest, payment due dates, and statement lines.

## E-Wallet / Fintech

Maintains wallet balance, wallet payments, peer transfers, promotions, refunds, linked-bank activity, and notifications.

## Payment Network / Switch

Routes QR, card, bank, wallet, and interbank payment messages. Usually invisible to the household but important for timing and settlement differences.

## Third-Party Provider

May supply imported transactions, merchant enrichment, currency conversion, receipts, or classification metadata.

## System

Stores the household transaction record and preserves factual consistency between account, amount, direction, date, category, mapping, and review status.

## Background Worker

May ingest provider records, reconcile delayed updates, detect missing information, or surface review items. In discovery terms, it observes and prepares facts; it does not decide household intent.

## Household Reviewer

The person temporarily responsible for making sense of unresolved activity. This may be either partner and may change over time.

## Support / Administrator

May help investigate data access, sync, duplicate, or correction issues without becoming an owner of household financial decisions.
