# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

Natural core capabilities of the Transactions domain:

- Record real money movement.
- Distinguish income from expense.
- Preserve amount, currency, date, account, and direction.
- Show chronological activity.
- Link to a real account or container.
- Capture household note or description.
- Categorize by household meaning.
- Support review of unresolved activity.
- Support search and filtering.
- Support refunds, reversals, and corrections as audit concepts.
- Provide evidence for account balance changes.
- Feed planning, inbox, and health domains as factual input.

## Optional Capabilities

Capabilities that commonly exist in real transaction systems but are not always essential:

- Transfer representation between owned accounts.
- Pending vs posted distinction.
- Merchant normalization.
- Receipt attachment.
- Split categorization.
- Duplicate detection.
- Recurring pattern detection.
- Statement reconciliation.
- Provider import.
- Cash reconciliation.
- Foreign currency metadata.
- Fees and discounts as explicit context.
- Partner comments or explanations.

## Future Capabilities

Capabilities that may become natural as household finance matures:

- Open Banking or provider-sync enrichment.
- More reliable merchant identity.
- Confidence scoring for imported or classified transactions.
- Better audit trail for disputes, chargebacks, and provider corrections.
- Household-level review history.
- International remittance and currency conversion context.
- AI-assisted classification that remains subordinate to factual transaction data.

These are observations only, not roadmap commitments.
