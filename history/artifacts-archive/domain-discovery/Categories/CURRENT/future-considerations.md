# Future Considerations

These are observations only. They are not roadmap commitments or product recommendations.

## 3-5 Year Observations

- Vietnamese bank, card, wallet, VietQR, and merchant data may become more standardized and useful for category hints.
- Merchant normalization may matter more as transaction volume increases.
- Households may expect suggested categorization because modern finance tools increasingly reduce manual review.
- Family-life templates may become useful for categories such as baby, school, parents, Tet, home, health, and travel.
- Item-level receipt data may eventually make mixed purchases easier to understand.
- Provider categories, MCCs, and household categories may need clearer separation as card and wallet integrations grow.
- AI-assisted categorization may become normal, but household override and transparency will remain important.
- Category drift over years may become a reporting problem if households rename, merge, or abandon labels.
- Multi-language and accent-insensitive matching may matter for Vietnamese and English household category names.
- Cross-border QR, card, travel, and remittance contexts may create new category ambiguity.
- Financial-adviser, accountant, or export use cases may need categories to be understandable outside the app.

## Stability Observation

The core category model can survive 5-10 years if it remains a classification layer. The fragile parts are automation, provider interoperability, historical drift, and household vocabulary growth.
