# Edge Cases

## Data Completeness

- Account is missing.
- Cash is untracked.
- Wallet balance is unknown.
- Partner-controlled account is invisible.
- Transaction sync is delayed.
- Duplicate transactions distort activity.
- Manual transaction correction changes prior interpretation.

## Time And Periods

- Salary arrives early or late.
- Month boundary differs from household pay cycle.
- One-time income creates a temporary strong signal.
- Seasonal expenses distort trend.
- Historical data is too short for comparison.

## Debt And Credit

- Minimum card payment is made but debt burden remains high.
- Card utilization rises after statement close.
- Loan payment is deferred, restructured, prepaid, or missed.
- Informal family loan has no fixed schedule.
- Interest, fee, or penalty is unknown.

## Healthcare And Insurance

- Medical bill is paid before insurance reimbursement.
- Insurance reimbursement is partial, delayed, denied, or capped.
- Public insurance eligibility differs by facility, referral, benefit code, or covered item.
- Private insurance excludes a condition.
- Pregnancy, childbirth, child illness, elder care, or chronic illness creates recurring exposure.
- Emergency care occurs outside the planned provider network.

## Household Behavior

- Partner disagreement hides real pressure.
- Family support is expected but uncertain.
- Household supports relatives unexpectedly.
- User avoids updating records during stress.
- User interprets a score as advice or guarantee.

## Interpretation

- Strong setup creates a good signal while money is still tight.
- Low setup creates a weak signal even when the household has external reserves.
- A single unusual transaction changes perceived activity.
- Health cannot distinguish missing data from absence of risk.
- Read-only scenario is mistaken for an instruction.
