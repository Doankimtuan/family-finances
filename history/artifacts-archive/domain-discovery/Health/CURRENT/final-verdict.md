# Final Verdict

## Is This Domain Correctly Understood?

Partially, with a strong foundation.

Health is correctly understood as a read-only household financial condition mirror, not an operational money domain. Its core responsibility is reflection: interpreting source-domain facts into stability, pressure, resilience, and attention signals.

The domain should not become medical health management, financial advice, insurance advice, automated planning, or money movement.

## Knowledge Gaps

- How Vietnamese young families personally define "financially healthy."
- Which Health factors create trust versus anxiety.
- How households think about medical expense exposure and insurance protection.
- How partner disagreement should be represented without creating blame.
- How much missing data users will tolerate before distrusting the signal.
- Whether a numeric score, named level, or factor list is most understandable.

## Validation Needed Before Design Begins

- Interview young households in Vietnam about financial-health language and mental models.
- Validate medical-cost and insurance-friction assumptions with real household stories.
- Validate whether read-only Health observations are understood as observations, not instructions.
- Validate source-domain completeness required for a credible signal.
- Review any future legal or compliance constraints before Health discusses insurance, credit, or medical-risk topics.

## Confidence Score

Confidence: 0.78.

The read-only domain boundary is clear and stable. Real-world household pressure patterns are well understood at a discovery level. Confidence is reduced by the need for direct Vietnamese household interviews and compliance validation before product design.
