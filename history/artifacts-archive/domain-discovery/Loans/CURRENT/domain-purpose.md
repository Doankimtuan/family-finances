# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

The Loans domain exists because households often use future income to pay for present needs. A loan converts a current need into a future repayment obligation.

This domain makes that obligation visible, understandable, and separable from normal spending.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility is to represent scheduled borrowing obligations:

- Who or what the household owes.
- How much was originally borrowed.
- How much remains.
- What repayment terms apply.
- When payments are due.
- What part of repayment is principal, interest, fee, or penalty in the real world.
- Whether the obligation is active, changed, defaulted, cancelled, or completed.

## Financial Principle

Loans are liabilities. They are not spending categories, savings goals, accounts, or virtual plans.

The central financial principle is time-shifted purchasing power: the borrower receives value now and repays value later, usually with interest or fees as the cost of time, risk, and capital.

## Value Provided

For a household, the domain provides:

- A truthful view of obligations that reduce future disposable income.
- Awareness of due dates and cash-flow pressure.
- Separation between real payments and projected repayment plans.
- A way to understand total repayment burden, not only the original borrowed amount.
- A record of progress toward being free of the obligation.
