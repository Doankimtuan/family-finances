# Edge Cases

## Account Existence

- Account opened but not yet funded.
- Account created in product before real-world account is active.
- Duplicate account record.
- Account renamed at the bank.
- Account number changes after card reissue or bank migration.
- Institution merger changes account metadata.

## Balance Reality

- Opening balance wrong.
- Cash count differs from recorded balance.
- Bank balance differs because pending transactions are not posted.
- Available balance differs from current balance.
- Authorization hold appears then disappears.
- Fee or interest posted unexpectedly.
- Negative balance from overdraft or data error.
- Wallet promotional balance is not withdrawable.

## Movement

- Own-account transfer double-counted as income and expense.
- Bank transfer pending, failed, reversed, or duplicated.
- QR payment sent to wrong recipient.
- Refund posted to different account than original purchase.
- Chargeback or dispute temporarily credits account.
- Cash withdrawal recorded without matching cash increase.
- Cash deposit recorded without matching cash decrease.

## Lifecycle

- Account frozen.
- Wallet restricted because verification is incomplete.
- Card blocked for fraud.
- Lost phone prevents wallet access.
- Closed account still has historical transactions.
- Account becomes dormant.
- Household forgets a small e-wallet balance.
- Member leaves household but account history remains relevant.

## Currency and Geography

- Foreign currency account.
- Overseas card transaction with exchange rate difference.
- Travel wallet balance.
- Remittance received in converted currency.
- Cross-border QR or transfer settlement delay.

## Household Reality

- One partner tracks an account the other does not know well.
- Personal account used for household expense.
- Shared expense paid from a relative's account.
- Money temporarily held by a parent or sibling.
- Informal cash loan looks like account money unless classified correctly elsewhere.

## Information Quality

- Missing institution name.
- Unknown account type.
- Old bank statement unavailable.
- Imported feed delayed.
- Manual entry conflicts with imported transaction.
- Provider outage.
- Deleted external connection.
- Partial historical data after integration starts.

No solutions are proposed in this document.
