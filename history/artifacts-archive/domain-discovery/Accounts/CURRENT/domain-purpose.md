# Domain Purpose

## Why This Domain Exists

The Accounts domain exists because household money must have a real-world home before it can be trusted, planned, spent, saved, borrowed, or discussed.

An account is the household's record of a financial container: bank payment account, cash wallet, e-wallet, savings account, brokerage account, credit card account, or another place where a real balance or obligation exists.

## Core Responsibility

The core responsibility of Accounts is to represent real containers and expose their factual position.

It answers:

- Where is money or financial capacity held?
- What type of container is it?
- Who or what institution maintains the real-world record?
- What balance would be seen if the household checked that real-world source?
- Is the container active, closed, inaccessible, or no longer relevant?

## Financial Principle

The financial principle is custody and ownership.

Money is not useful as an abstract number. It exists in custody somewhere: a bank, a wallet provider, a cash drawer, a broker, a credit issuer, or another person temporarily holding it. Household planning becomes reliable only when each real amount is anchored to a container that can be verified.

## Value Provided

Accounts provide:

- A reality layer before intention or analysis.
- A common household view of scattered money.
- Context for transactions.
- Liquidity awareness.
- Reconciliation against real institutions.
- A trust foundation for planning, health, and household discussions.

The domain's value is clarity, not advice. It makes the household's real financial position visible without deciding what that money should be used for.
