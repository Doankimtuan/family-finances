# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

Natural core capabilities of the Accounts domain:

- Represent real-world money containers.
- Identify account type.
- Name accounts in household language.
- Hold opening or starting balance.
- Expose current recorded balance.
- Contribute to real household position.
- Support active versus historical account distinction.
- Provide account context for transactions.
- Support reconciliation against real-world records.
- Preserve history after closure or archival.
- Distinguish liquid and non-liquid containers.
- Distinguish real money from virtual planning.

## Optional Capabilities

Natural optional capabilities:

- Institution metadata.
- Account number mask.
- Currency per account.
- Holder or ownership metadata.
- Manual balance adjustment record.
- Balance history snapshots.
- Imported bank or wallet balances.
- Staleness indicator for manually updated accounts.
- Account grouping by household member, institution, or liquidity.
- Transfer recognition between owned accounts.
- Statement attachment or reference.
- Export of account records.

## Future Capabilities

Possible future capabilities that remain natural to the domain:

- Multi-currency account support.
- Open-banking or provider feed integration where legally and commercially available.
- More precise liquidity classification.
- Account sub-classification by product type.
- Cross-border wallet or QR payment awareness.
- Read-only investment or wealth accounts.
- Account access status such as frozen, restricted, pending verification, or closed.
- Stronger reconciliation workflow for manual and imported records.

These are observations only, not roadmap commitments.
