# Capabilities

## Core Capabilities

- Represent a card as a distinct financial instrument.
- Distinguish debit, credit, and prepaid behavior.
- Identify issuer, network, cardholder role, and card status.
- Track credit limit and available credit for credit cards.
- Track statement date, due date, statement balance, paid amount, and remaining due amount.
- Associate card transactions with billing periods.
- Separate authorization, posting, repayment, refund, fee, interest, and reward concepts.
- Reflect supplementary-card usage under primary-card responsibility.
- Support card repayment as movement from a real money account to a card obligation.
- Preserve card history after expiry, replacement, closure, or archive.

## Optional Capabilities

- Track annual fee, late fee, cash-advance fee, FX markup, installment fee, and other issuer charges.
- Track rewards, points, miles, vouchers, and cashback.
- Track installments created from card purchases.
- Track disputes, chargebacks, and provisional credits.
- Track provider statement imports or user-uploaded statements.
- Track card tokenization in wallets or online merchants.
- Track card replacement and masked-card identity changes.
- Track supplementary-card spending separately from primary-card spending.
- Track full-payment, partial-payment, minimum-payment, and autopay behavior.

## Future Capabilities

- Provider-verified card feeds.
- Statement parsing and reconciliation.
- Fraud or unusual-spend detection.
- Subscription and card-on-file visibility.
- Multi-currency card cost analysis.
- Credit-health analysis tied to card utilization and repayment behavior.
- Reward optimization analysis.
- Household policy controls for partner or dependent card use.
- Country-specific card rule packs.

Future capabilities are observations only and are not roadmap commitments.
