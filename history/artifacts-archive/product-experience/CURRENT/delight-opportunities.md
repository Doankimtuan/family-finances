# Delight Opportunities

## Principle

Delight should reinforce financial discipline, not gamify responsibility. No points, streak pressure, leaderboards, or competitive household scoring.

## Approved Delight Moments

| Moment | Delight UX | Guardrail |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Goal completed | Quiet completion card and next planning prompt | Do not imply cash moved unless source facts prove it |
| Debt paid off | Mark obligation complete and suggest reviewing freed cash flow | Do not force reallocation |
| Savings maturity handled | Calm confirmation of decision recorded | Do not imply provider settlement unless confirmed |
| Investment milestone | Mark value update or realized proceeds clearly | No investment advice |
| One-year review | Annual household reflection snapshot | No ranking or guilt |
| Birthday/anniversary | Optional plan reflection if already relevant | No spending encouragement |
| Child education milestone | Goal progress acknowledgment | No pressure comparison |
| Month Close | Subtle closure moment | No streak dependency before Quick Close eligibility |

## Recommendation

### PX-DL-01: Build delight as reflection, not reward

Problem: Financial responsibility can be cheapened by game-like rewards.

User Impact: Users may chase app feedback instead of household clarity.

Affected Screens: Goals, Debts/Loans, Savings, Month Ritual, Health.

Frequency: Event-based.

Business Impact: Medium retention impact.

Recommended UX: Use simple milestone cards that answer "what changed" and "what is the responsible next review", with sparse motion.

Implementation Cost: Low.

Priority: P2.
